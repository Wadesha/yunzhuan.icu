SAT

学术评估测试

Scholastic Assessment Test · 1600 scale

Course Overview 课程介绍

美国大学理事会主办的本科入学学术能力测试，已成为美本申请的常用标化之一。

The SAT is a standardized test administered by the College Board and widely used for US undergraduate admission. It measures reading, writing and mathematical reasoning skills developed throughout a student's education. Since 2024 the SAT has transitioned to a fully digital, adaptive format, with two parallel modules per section that adjust question difficulty based on performance.

Test Structure 考试结构

分阅读与文法、数学两大模块，全部机考自适应。

The digital SAT consists of two stages: (1) Reading and Writing, organized into two 32-minute modules with short passages and discrete questions; (2) Math, organized into two 35-minute modules allowing calculator use throughout. Each stage begins with a calibration module that determines the difficulty of the second module, providing a more individualized assessment in a shorter total testing time of about 2 hours 14 minutes plus breaks.

Scoring 评分机制

总分 1600，由阅读文法与数学两区合计，无倒扣分。

The SAT total score ranges from 400 to 1600, combining two section scores of 200-800 each: Reading and Writing, and Math. There is no penalty for incorrect answers, so test-takers should attempt every question. Subscores and cross-test scores provide additional diagnostic feedback but are not used independently by most colleges for admission decisions.

Test Dates & Registration 考试时间与报名

每年约 7 次考次，建议提前在 College Board 官网注册。

The SAT is offered roughly seven times per year, typically in March, May, June, August, October, November and December. Registration is conducted through the College Board website; international candidates should register early as test center capacity is limited. Scores are usually released within two to three weeks after the test date, and students may send four free score reports within nine days of taking the exam.

Preparation & Strategy 备考策略

熟悉机考节奏与自适应题型，配合官方 BlueBank 题库练习。

Effective SAT preparation combines content review with deliberate practice on the digital platform. Students should familiarize themselves with the Bluebook testing app, complete official practice tests under timed conditions, and focus on weak areas identified through error logs. Time management across the short modules and efficient use of the built-in graphing calculator are critical to maximizing score.
