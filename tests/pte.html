PTE Academic

培生学术英语考试

Pearson Test of English Academic · 10-90 scale

Course Overview 课程介绍

培生集团主办的全机考英语能力测试，出分快、认可度持续上升。

PTE Academic (Pearson Test of English Academic) is a computer-based English language proficiency test administered by Pearson PLC. It assesses reading, writing, listening and speaking skills through an integrated task format, with all scoring performed by automated AI systems rather than human raters. The test is accepted by thousands of universities worldwide, including a growing number of US undergraduate programs, and is known for its fast score delivery (typically within 48 hours) and flexible test scheduling.

Test Structure 考试结构

三大模块，20 种题型，大量综合题型交叉考查听说读写。

PTE Academic consists of three main parts: Speaking & Writing (54-67 min), Reading (29-30 min), and Listening (30-43 min), with an optional 10-minute break between Reading and Listening. The test features 20 different question types, many of which are integrated-such as Read Aloud (speaking + reading), Repeat Sentence (listening + speaking), and Summarize Written Text (reading + writing). The fully computer-delivered format means test-takers use a headset and microphone for speaking tasks and type all written responses.

Scoring 评分机制

10-90 分制，与 CEFR 对标，全 AI 评分客观一致。

Scores range from 10 to 90, with each 10-point band roughly corresponding to a CEFR level (e.g., 43-50 = B1, 59-64 = B2, 76-84 = C1). The automated scoring system evaluates over 50 features of speech and writing, including grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, fluency and discourse structure. Because scoring is entirely machine-based, results are consistent and typically available within 48 hours. Competitive US undergraduate programs often require a PTE score of 58-68 or above, equivalent to approximately TOEFL 90-100 or IELTS 6.5-7.0.

Test Dates & Registration 考试时间与报名

考次极多，可提前 24 小时报名，最快 48 小时出分。

PTE Academic is offered year-round at Pearson VUE test centers in over 100 countries, with test slots available multiple times per day in most major cities. Registration is done through the official PTE website, and candidates can often book a test as little as 24 hours in advance depending on seat availability. The test can be retaken as often as every 5 days with no limit on the number of attempts. Official scores are typically released within 48 hours, making PTE one of the fastest-scoring major English proficiency exams.

Preparation & Strategy 备考策略

熟悉题型模板，重点训练口语流利度和打字速度，善用机经。

Effective PTE preparation focuses on mastering the specific task formats and understanding the AI scoring criteria. Speaking tasks such as Read Aloud and Describe Image benefit from structured templates and fluency practice-the algorithm prioritizes smooth delivery over accent perfection. For Writing, Summarize Written Text and Essay tasks reward proper essay structure and vocabulary range. Because the test is heavily integrated, improving one skill area often lifts scores across multiple sections. Official practice tests from Pearson are the most reliable way to estimate your score range.
