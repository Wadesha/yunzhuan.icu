ACT

美国大学测试

American College Testing · 36 scale

Course Overview 课程介绍

美国本科入学标化考试，包含科学推理部分，与 SAT 同等接受度。

The ACT is a standardized test administered by ACT Inc. and accepted by all US four-year colleges alongside the SAT. It assesses English, mathematics, reading and scientific reasoning, with an optional Writing section. Unlike the SAT, the ACT includes a dedicated Science section that evaluates interpretation and analysis of scientific data rather than recall of scientific facts.

Key Facts 基本信息

Exam Board (主办方)ACT Inc.
Score Range (分数)1-36
Sections (科目)English, Math, Reading, Science, Writing (optional)
Duration (时长)2h55m without Writing, 3h35m with Writing
Test Dates (考次)7 times / year

Test Structure 考试结构

四大必考科目 + 可选写作，强调速度与时间管理。

The ACT consists of four required multiple-choice sections: English (45 min, 75 questions), Mathematics (60 min, 60 questions), Reading (35 min, 40 questions), and Science (35 min, 40 questions). The optional Writing section (40 min) requires a single essay response. The tight pacing, particularly in Science and Reading, makes time management the most common challenge for test-takers.

Scoring 评分机制

四科分别 1-36 分，取平均为综合分；写作单独计 2-12 分。

Each of the four required sections is scored on a 1-36 scale; the Composite score is the rounded average of these four section scores. The optional Writing test receives a separate 2-12 score and does not affect the Composite. There is no penalty for wrong answers, so students should answer every question. Subscores provide additional detail but are not typically used in admission decisions.

Test Dates & Registration 考试时间与报名

每年约 7 次考次，国际考生通过 ACT 官网在线注册。

The ACT is offered approximately seven times per year, typically in September, October, December, February, April, June and July. Registration is handled through the ACT website, with both paper-based and online testing options available depending on the country. Score reports are released within two to eight weeks, with online scores typically available first.

Preparation & Strategy 备考策略

强化限时训练，特别关注科学推理节奏与文法速度。

ACT preparation should prioritize pacing drills, as the per-question time is shorter than on the SAT. Students should build stamina through full-length timed practice tests and develop efficient strategies for the Science section, which often rewards graph-reading and data interpretation over content knowledge. Reviewing grammar rules and practicing mental math can also yield quick score gains.
