标化考试中心

SAT · ACT · TOEFL · IELTS · Duolingo English Test · PTE Academic

This section is a navigation hub for the standardized tests most commonly required for undergraduate admission overseas. It covers academic aptitude tests (SAT, ACT) and English-language proficiency tests (TOEFL, IELTS, Duolingo English Test), with each exam summarized on a single page covering structure, scoring, key facts and preparation pointers.

美国大学入学考试，阅读+文法+数学，1600 分制。 ACT American College Testing US college admissions test covering English, Math, Reading, Science plus optional Writing, on a 36-point scale.
美国大学入学考试，英语+数学+阅读+科学+写作，36 分制。 TOEFL Test of English as a Foreign Language English proficiency test on a 120-point scale, the most widely accepted by US undergraduate programs.
英语能力测试，120 分制，美本最常用。 IELTS International English Language Testing System English proficiency test on a 9.0 band scale, commonly used in Commonwealth countries.
英语能力测试，9 分制，英联邦国家常用。 Duolingo English Test DET Online on-demand English test on a 160-point scale, with growing acceptance in recent years.
在线英语测试，160 分制，近年接受度上升。 PTE Academic Pearson Test of English Computer-based English test on a 10-90 scale, AI-scored with results in 48 hours.
全机考英语测试，10-90 分制，AI 评分 48 小时出分。
