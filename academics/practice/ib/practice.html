Mathematics (7 questions)

Q1 · IB Math AA HL · Calculus

Find the maximum value of f(x) = x³ - 6x² + 9x + 2 on the interval [0, 4].

  • A) 6
  • B) 2
  • C) 4
  • D) 9
Answer: A) 6. f'(x) = 3x² - 12x + 9 = 0 → x=1 or x=3. f(1)=6, f(3)=2, f(0)=2, f(4)=6. Maximum is 6 at x=1.
Q4 · IB Math AA HL · Algebra · Medium

Given log₂(x) + log₂(x-2) = 3, find x.

  • A) 4
  • B) -2
  • C) 4 or -2
  • D) 8
Answer: A) 4. log₂(x(x-2))=3, x(x-2)=8, x²-2x-8=0, (x-4)(x+2)=0, x=4 (x=-2 rejected since log of negative is undefined).
Q5 · IB Math AA HL · Functions · Hard

If f(x) = 2x + 1 and g(x) = x² - 3, find g(f(2)).

  • A) 22
  • B) 12
  • C) 6
  • D) 9
Answer: A) 22. f(2)=2(2)+1=5, g(5)=5²-3=22.
Q11 · IB Math AA HL · Algebra · Easy

Solve: 2^x = 32.

  • A) 3
  • B) 4
  • C) 5
  • D) 6
Answer: C) 5. 2^5 = 32.
Q12 · IB Math AA HL · Calculus · Medium

Find dy/dx if y = 3x⁴ - 2x² + 5.

  • A) 12x³ - 4x
  • B) 12x³ - 2
  • C) 3x³ - 2x
  • D) 12x⁴ - 4x²
Answer: A) 12x³ - 4x. Power rule: 4·3x³ - 2·2x = 12x³ - 4x.
Q13 · IB Math AA HL · Statistics · Medium

In a normal distribution with mean 50 and SD 10, what percentage of data lies between 40 and 60?

  • A) 68%
  • B) 95%
  • C) 34%
  • D) 99.7%
Answer: A) 68%. 40 and 60 are ±1 SD from mean, covering ~68% of data.
Q14 · IB Math AA HL · Vectors · Hard

Given u = (3, 4), find |u|.

  • A) 7
  • B) 12
  • C) 5
  • D) 25
Answer: C) 5. |u| = √(9+16) = √25 = 5.

Physics (6 questions)

Q2 · IB Physics HL · Mechanics

A ball is thrown vertically upward at 20 m/s. What is the maximum height reached? (g = 10 m/s²)

  • A) 10 m
  • B) 20 m
  • C) 40 m
  • D) 5 m
Answer: B) 20 m. v² = u² - 2gh, 0 = 400 - 20h, h = 20 m.
Q6 · IB Physics HL · Waves · Medium

A wave has frequency 500 Hz and speed 340 m/s. What is its wavelength?

  • A) 0.34 m
  • B) 0.68 m
  • C) 1.47 m
  • D) 170000 m
Answer: B) 0.68 m. λ = v/f = 340/500 = 0.68 m.
Q7 · IB Physics HL · Thermodynamics · Hard

An ideal gas at 300 K has its temperature doubled to 600 K at constant volume. If the initial pressure was 100 kPa, what is the new pressure?

  • A) 50 kPa
  • B) 100 kPa
  • C) 200 kPa
  • D) 400 kPa
Answer: C) 200 kPa. At constant volume, P/T = constant. P₂ = P₁ × T₂/T₁ = 100 × 600/300 = 200 kPa.
Q15 · IB Physics HL · Mechanics · Easy

A car decelerates from 30 m/s to 0 in 6 seconds. What is the deceleration?

  • A) 5 m/s²
  • B) -5 m/s²
  • C) 30 m/s²
  • D) 180 m/s²
Answer: B) -5 m/s². a = Δv/Δt = (0-30)/6 = -5 m/s².
Q16 · IB Physics HL · Electricity · Medium

A 12V battery drives 3A through a circuit. What is the resistance?

  • A) 36Ω
  • B) 4Ω
  • C) 9Ω
  • D) 0.25Ω
Answer: B) 4Ω. R = V/I = 12/3 = 4Ω.
Q17 · IB Physics HL · Modern Physics · Hard

What is the energy of a photon with frequency 5×10¹⁴ Hz? (h = 6.6×10⁻³⁴ J·s)

  • A) 3.3×10⁻¹⁹ J
  • B) 7.6×10⁻²⁰ J
  • C) 1.3×10⁻⁴⁸ J
  • D) 5×10¹⁴ J
Answer: A) 3.3×10⁻¹⁹ J. E = hf = 6.6×10⁻³⁴ × 5×10¹⁴ = 3.3×10⁻¹⁹ J.

Economics, Business & Chemistry (7 questions)

Q3 · IB Economics HL · Microeconomics

Which of the following will cause a shift of the demand curve to the right?

  • A) A decrease in the price of the good
  • B) An increase in consumer income (normal good)
  • C) A decrease in the price of a substitute good
  • D) An improvement in production technology
Answer: B). Income increase shifts demand right for normal goods. A is movement along curve, C shifts left, D shifts supply.
Q8 · IB Economics HL · Macroeconomics · Medium

Which of the following is an example of expansionary fiscal policy?

  • A) Increasing interest rates
  • B) Increasing government spending
  • C) Selling government bonds
  • D) Increasing reserve requirements
Answer: B). Expansionary fiscal policy involves increasing government spending or decreasing taxes to stimulate aggregate demand.
Q9 · IB Chemistry HL · Equilibrium · Hard

For the reaction N₂ + 3H₂ ⇌ 2NH₃, what happens to the equilibrium when pressure is increased?

  • A) Shifts left
  • B) Shifts right
  • C) No change
  • D) Reaction stops
Answer: B) Shifts right. Increasing pressure shifts equilibrium toward the side with fewer moles of gas. Products have 2 moles vs 4 moles of reactants.
Q10 · IB Business Management HL · Marketing · Easy

Which of the following is NOT one of the 4 Ps of the marketing mix?

  • A) Price
  • B) Promotion
  • C) Position
  • D) Product
Answer: C) Position. The 4 Ps are Product, Price, Place, Promotion.
Q18 · IB Economics HL · Microeconomics · Easy

If the price of coffee rises, what happens to demand for tea (a substitute)?

  • A) Decreases
  • B) Increases
  • C) No change
  • D) Becomes zero
Answer: B) Increases. Substitutes: when coffee price rises, consumers switch to tea.
Q19 · IB Economics HL · Macroeconomics · Medium

What is the primary tool used by central banks to control inflation?

  • A) Tax rates
  • B) Government spending
  • C) Interest rates
  • D) Exchange rates
Answer: C) Interest rates. Central banks adjust interest rates to control money supply and inflation.
Q20 · IB Business Management HL · Finance · Medium

A company's revenue is $500,000 and costs are $350,000. What is the profit margin?

  • A) 25%
  • B) 30%
  • C) 70%
  • D) 42.9%
Answer: B) 30%. Profit = $150,000. Margin = 150,000/500,000 = 30%.
