知识点清单（示例：Math AA HL）

以下以 Math Analysis & Approaches HL 为示例，列出 5 大模块知识大纲。

Topic 1: Algebra

  • Sequences & Series
  • Exponents & Logarithms
  • Binomial Theorem
  • Complex Numbers (Argand diagram, polar form)
  • Proofs (induction, contradiction)

Topic 2: Functions

  • Linear, Quadratic, Rational Functions
  • Exponential & Logarithmic Functions
  • Transformations of Graphs
  • Inverse & Composite Functions

Topic 3: Geometry & Trigonometry

  • Trigonometric Identities & Equations
  • Sine & Cosine Rules
  • Vectors (dot/cross product)
  • Vector Lines & Planes

Topic 4: Calculus

  • Limits & Continuity
  • Differentiation (chain, product, quotient rules)
  • Integration techniques (substitution, parts)
  • Applications (optimization, kinematics)
  • Maclaurin Series

Topic 5: Statistics & Probability

  • Descriptive Statistics
  • Probability Distributions (Binomial, Normal)
  • Hypothesis Testing
  • Correlation & Regression
