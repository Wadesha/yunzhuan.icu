Group 1 · Mathematics (20 questions)

Q1 · IGCSE Math (0580) Extended · Number · Easy

A shop gives a 15% discount on a jacket originally priced at $80. What is the sale price?

  • A) $12
  • B) $68
  • C) $65
  • D) $72
Answer: B) $68. Discount = 15% × $80 = $12. Sale price = $80 − $12 = $68.
Q4 · IGCSE Math · Algebra · Easy

Factorise: x² + 5x + 6.

  • A) (x + 1)(x + 6)
  • B) (x + 2)(x + 3)
  • C) (x − 2)(x − 3)
  • D) (x + 1)(x + 5)
Answer: B) (x + 2)(x + 3). We need two numbers that multiply to 6 and add to 5: 2 and 3.
Q5 · IGCSE Math · Geometry · Medium

A circle has radius 7 cm. What is its area? (π = 22/7)

  • A) 154 cm²
  • B) 44 cm²
  • C) 22 cm²
  • D) 308 cm²
Answer: A) 154 cm². A = πr² = (22/7) × 49 = 22 × 7 = 154 cm².
Q6 · IGCSE Math · Statistics · Medium

The mean of 5 numbers is 12. If a 6th number is added and the mean becomes 13, what is the 6th number?

  • A) 13
  • B) 15
  • C) 18
  • D) 78
Answer: C) 18. Sum of 5 = 60. Sum of 6 = 78. 6th number = 78 − 60 = 18.
Q11 · IGCSE Math · Number · Easy

Express 0.45 as a fraction in lowest terms.

  • A) 45/100
  • B) 9/20
  • C) 4/5
  • D) 9/100
Answer: B) 9/20. 0.45 = 45/100 = 9/20.
Q12 · IGCSE Math · Algebra · Medium

Solve: 3(x − 2) = 15.

  • A) x = 3
  • B) x = 5
  • C) x = 7
  • D) x = 9
Answer: C) x = 7. 3x − 6 = 15, 3x = 21, x = 7.
Q13 · IGCSE Math · Geometry · Medium

A triangle has angles 50° and 60°. What is the third angle?

  • A) 60°
  • B) 70°
  • C) 80°
  • D) 90°
Answer: B) 70°. Sum = 180°, 180 − 50 − 60 = 70°.
Q14 · IGCSE Math · Statistics · Easy

What is the median of: 3, 7, 9, 1, 5?

  • A) 3
  • B) 5
  • C) 7
  • D) 9
Answer: B) 5. Sorted: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9. Middle = 5.
Q21 · IGCSE Math · Percentages · Easy

A shirt price increases from $40 to $50. What is the percentage increase?

  • A) 10%
  • B) 20%
  • C) 25%
  • D) 50%
Answer: C) 25%. Increase = 50 − 40 = 10. % increase = 10/40 × 100 = 25%.
Q22 · IGCSE Math · Ratio · Easy

Divide $120 in the ratio 2 : 3.

  • A) $40 and $80
  • B) $48 and $72
  • C) $50 and $70
  • D) $60 and $60
Answer: B) $48 and $72. Total parts = 5. 2/5 × 120 = 48, 3/5 × 120 = 72.
Q23 · IGCSE Math · Standard Form · Medium

Write 0.00034 in standard form.

  • A) 3.4 × 10⁻⁴
  • B) 3.4 × 10⁴
  • C) 34 × 10⁻⁵
  • D) 0.34 × 10⁻³
Answer: A) 3.4 × 10⁻⁴. Move the decimal 4 places right to get a number between 1 and 10, with a negative exponent.
Q24 · IGCSE Math · Inequalities · Medium

Solve: 2x − 5 < 7.

  • A) x < 1
  • B) x < 6
  • C) x < 12
  • D) x > 6
Answer: B) x < 6. 2x < 12, so x < 6.
Q25 · IGCSE Math · Pythagoras · Easy

A right triangle has legs 6 cm and 8 cm. What is the hypotenuse?

  • A) 10 cm
  • B) 12 cm
  • C) 14 cm
  • D) 100 cm
Answer: A) 10 cm. c = √(6² + 8²) = √(36 + 64) = √100 = 10.
Q26 · IGCSE Math · Trigonometry · Medium

In a right triangle, the side opposite to angle θ is 5 and the hypotenuse is 13. What is sin(θ)?

  • A) 5/12
  • B) 12/13
  • C) 5/13
  • D) 13/5
Answer: C) 5/13. sin(θ) = opposite / hypotenuse = 5/13 (SOH).
Q27 · IGCSE Math · Sine Rule · Hard

In a triangle, a = 8 cm, A = 30°, B = 45°. Find b. (Use sin 30° = 0.5, sin 45° ≈ 0.707)

  • A) 4 cm
  • B) 5.66 cm
  • C) 8 cm
  • D) 11.31 cm
Answer: D) 11.31 cm. a/sin A = b/sin B → 8/0.5 = b/0.707 → b = 16 × 0.707 ≈ 11.31 cm.
Q28 · IGCSE Math · Histograms · Medium

A histogram bar has frequency density 5 on a class interval of width 4. What is the frequency?

  • A) 1.25
  • B) 5
  • C) 9
  • D) 20
Answer: D) 20. Frequency = frequency density × class width = 5 × 4 = 20.
Q29 · IGCSE Math · Probability Trees · Medium

A bag has 3 red and 2 blue balls. Two are drawn without replacement. What is P(both red)?

  • A) 3/10
  • B) 6/25
  • C) 9/25
  • D) 1/2
Answer: A) 3/10. P(RR) = 3/5 × 2/4 = 6/20 = 3/10.
Q30 · IGCSE Math · Compound Interest · Medium

$1000 is invested at 10% per year compounded annually for 2 years. What is the total amount?

  • A) $1200
  • B) $1210
  • C) $1100
  • D) $2100
Answer: B) $1210. A = 1000 × 1.1² = 1000 × 1.21 = $1210.
Q31 · IGCSE Math · Surds · Hard

Simplify √72.

  • A) 6√2
  • B) 8√3
  • C) 12√2
  • D) 36√2
Answer: A) 6√2. √72 = √(36 × 2) = 6√2.
Q32 · IGCSE Math · Transformations · Hard

A triangle with vertices (1,1), (3,1), (2,4) is reflected in the x-axis. What are the new vertices?

  • A) (1,−1), (3,−1), (2,−4)
  • B) (−1,1), (−3,1), (−2,4)
  • C) (1,1), (3,1), (4,2)
  • D) (−1,−1), (−3,−1), (−2,−4)
Answer: A) (1,−1), (3,−1), (2,−4). Reflection in the x-axis changes the sign of y-coordinates; x stays the same.

Group 2 · Physics (16 questions)

Q2 · IGCSE Physics (0625) · Mechanics · Medium

A car travels 120 km in 2 hours. What is its average speed in m/s?

  • A) 60 m/s
  • B) 16.7 m/s
  • C) 30 m/s
  • D) 120 m/s
Answer: B) 16.7 m/s. 120 km = 120,000 m. 2 hours = 7,200 s. Speed = 120,000 / 7,200 ≈ 16.7 m/s.
Q7 · IGCSE Physics · Electricity · Easy

What is the current in a circuit with a 12 V battery and a 4 Ω resistor?

  • A) 48 A
  • B) 3 A
  • C) 8 A
  • D) 0.33 A
Answer: B) 3 A. I = V / R = 12 / 4 = 3 A.
Q8 · IGCSE Physics · Waves · Medium

Sound travels at 330 m/s in air. If a sound has wavelength 1.5 m, what is its frequency?

  • A) 165 Hz
  • B) 220 Hz
  • C) 331.5 Hz
  • D) 495 Hz
Answer: B) 220 Hz. f = v / λ = 330 / 1.5 = 220 Hz.

What unit is used to measure force?

Q16 · IGCSE Physics · Energy · Medium

How much energy is needed to heat 2 kg of water by 10°C? (c = 4200 J/kg°C)

  • A) 42000 J
  • B) 84000 J
  • C) 4200 J
  • D) 8400 J
Answer: B) 84000 J. E = mcΔT = 2 × 4200 × 10 = 84000 J.
Q17 · IGCSE Physics · Electricity · Medium

Calculate the power of a device using 10 A at 12 V.

  • A) 1.2 W
  • B) 12 W
  • C) 120 W
  • D) 1200 W
Answer: C) 120 W. P = VI = 12 × 10 = 120 W.
Q33 · IGCSE Physics · Density · Easy

What is the density of an object with mass 60 g and volume 20 cm³?

  • A) 2 g/cm³
  • B) 3 g/cm³
  • C) 40 g/cm³
  • D) 1200 g/cm³
Answer: B) 3 g/cm³. Density = mass / volume = 60 / 20 = 3 g/cm³.
Q34 · IGCSE Physics · Pressure · Medium

A force of 200 N is applied evenly to an area of 0.5 m². What is the pressure?

  • A) 100 Pa
  • B) 200 Pa
  • C) 400 Pa
  • D) 1000 Pa
Answer: C) 400 Pa. Pressure = Force / Area = 200 / 0.5 = 400 Pa.
Q35 · IGCSE Physics · Moments · Medium

A 5 N force acts at 0.4 m from a pivot, perpendicular to the lever. What is the moment?

  • A) 1 Nm
  • B) 2 Nm
  • C) 5.4 Nm
  • D) 20 Nm
Answer: B) 2 Nm. Moment = Force × perpendicular distance = 5 × 0.4 = 2 Nm.
Q36 · IGCSE Physics · Hooke's Law · Medium

A spring extends 4 cm when a 2 N force is applied. What is the spring constant?

  • A) 0.5 N/m
  • B) 8 N/m
  • C) 50 N/m
  • D) 200 N/m
Answer: C) 50 N/m. F = kx → k = F / x = 2 / 0.04 = 50 N/m (converting 4 cm to 0.04 m).
Q37 · IGCSE Physics · Specific Heat Capacity · Hard

0.5 kg of metal (c = 900 J/kg°C) is heated from 20°C to 80°C. How much energy is required?

  • A) 13500 J
  • B) 27000 J
  • C) 45000 J
  • D) 54000 J
Answer: B) 27000 J. E = mcΔT = 0.5 × 900 × (80 − 20) = 0.5 × 900 × 60 = 27000 J.
Q38 · IGCSE Physics · Circuits · Medium

Two 6 Ω resistors are connected in parallel. What is the total resistance?

  • A) 3 Ω
  • B) 6 Ω
  • C) 12 Ω
  • D) 36 Ω
Answer: A) 3 Ω. 1/R = 1/6 + 1/6 = 2/6 = 1/3, so R = 3 Ω.
Q39 · IGCSE Physics · Transformers · Hard

A transformer has 100 primary turns and 400 secondary turns. If the primary voltage is 12 V, what is the secondary voltage (assume ideal)?

  • A) 3 V
  • B) 12 V
  • C) 48 V
  • D) 400 V
Answer: C) 48 V. Vp / Vs = Np / Ns → 12 / Vs = 100 / 400 → Vs = 12 × 4 = 48 V (step-up transformer).
Q40 · IGCSE Physics · Radioactivity · Hard

A radioactive isotope has a half-life of 8 days. After 24 days, what fraction of the original sample remains?

  • A) 1/2
  • B) 1/4
  • C) 1/8
  • D) 1/16
Answer: C) 1/8. 24 / 8 = 3 half-lives, so (1/2)³ = 1/8 remains.
Q41 · IGCSE Physics · Light · Easy

When light bounces off a plane mirror, the angle of reflection equals the angle of:

  • A) Incidence
  • B) Refraction
  • C) Diffraction
  • D) Dispersion
Answer: A) Incidence. Law of reflection: the angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection (both measured from the normal).
Q42 · IGCSE Physics · Energy Conservation · Medium

A 2 kg ball is dropped from 10 m. What is its kinetic energy just before hitting the ground? (g = 10 m/s², ignore air resistance)

  • A) 20 J
  • B) 100 J
  • C) 200 J
  • D) 2000 J
Answer: C) 200 J. PE at top = mgh = 2 × 10 × 10 = 200 J. By conservation of energy, KE just before impact = 200 J.

Q43 · IGCSE Chemistry · Periodic Table · Medium
