Group 1 · Mathematics (8 questions)

Q1 · IGCSE Math (0580) Extended · Number

A shop gives a 15% discount on a jacket originally priced at $80. What is the sale price?

  • A) $12
  • B) $68
  • C) $65
  • D) $72
Answer: B) $68. Discount = 15% × $80 = $12. Sale price = $80 − $12 = $68.
Q4 · IGCSE Math · Algebra · Easy

Factorise: x² + 5x + 6.

  • A) (x + 1)(x + 6)
  • B) (x + 2)(x + 3)
  • C) (x − 2)(x − 3)
  • D) (x + 1)(x + 5)
Answer: B) (x + 2)(x + 3). We need two numbers that multiply to 6 and add to 5: 2 and 3.
Q5 · IGCSE Math · Geometry · Medium

A circle has radius 7 cm. What is its area? (π = 22/7)

  • A) 154 cm²
  • B) 44 cm²
  • C) 22 cm²
  • D) 308 cm²
Answer: A) 154 cm². A = πr² = (22/7) × 49 = 22 × 7 = 154 cm².
Q6 · IGCSE Math · Statistics · Medium

The mean of 5 numbers is 12. If a 6th number is added and the mean becomes 13, what is the 6th number?

  • A) 13
  • B) 15
  • C) 18
  • D) 78
Answer: C) 18. Sum of 5 = 60. Sum of 6 = 78. 6th number = 78 − 60 = 18.
Q11 · IGCSE Math · Number · Easy

Express 0.45 as a fraction in lowest terms.

  • A) 45/100
  • B) 9/20
  • C) 4/5
  • D) 9/100
Answer: B) 9/20. 0.45 = 45/100 = 9/20.
Q12 · IGCSE Math · Algebra · Medium

Solve: 3(x − 2) = 15.

  • A) x = 3
  • B) x = 5
  • C) x = 7
  • D) x = 9
Answer: C) x = 7. 3x − 6 = 15, 3x = 21, x = 7.
Q13 · IGCSE Math · Geometry · Medium

A triangle has angles 50° and 60°. What is the third angle?

  • A) 60°
  • B) 70°
  • C) 80°
  • D) 90°
Answer: B) 70°. Sum = 180°, 180 − 50 − 60 = 70°.
Q14 · IGCSE Math · Statistics · Easy

What is the median of: 3, 7, 9, 1, 5?

  • A) 3
  • B) 5
  • C) 7
  • D) 9
Answer: B) 5. Sorted: 1, 3, 5, 7, 9. Middle = 5.

Group 2 · Physics (6 questions)

Q2 · IGCSE Physics (0625) · Mechanics

A car travels 120 km in 2 hours. What is its average speed in m/s?

  • A) 60 m/s
  • B) 16.7 m/s
  • C) 30 m/s
  • D) 120 m/s
Answer: B) 16.7 m/s. 120 km = 120,000 m. 2 hours = 7,200 s. Speed = 120,000 / 7,200 ≈ 16.7 m/s.
Q7 · IGCSE Physics · Electricity · Easy

What is the current in a circuit with a 12 V battery and a 4 Ω resistor?

  • A) 48 A
  • B) 3 A
  • C) 8 A
  • D) 0.33 A
Answer: B) 3 A. I = V / R = 12 / 4 = 3 A.
Q8 · IGCSE Physics · Waves · Medium

Sound travels at 330 m/s in air. If a sound has wavelength 1.5 m, what is its frequency?

  • A) 165 Hz
  • B) 220 Hz
  • C) 331.5 Hz
  • D) 495 Hz
Answer: B) 220 Hz. f = v / λ = 330 / 1.5 = 220 Hz.
Q15 · IGCSE Physics · Mechanics · Easy

What unit is used to measure force?

  • A) Joule
  • B) Watt
  • C) Newton
  • D) Pascal
Answer: C) Newton. Force is measured in Newtons (N).
Q16 · IGCSE Physics · Energy · Medium

How much energy is needed to heat 2 kg of water by 10°C? (c = 4200 J/kg°C)

  • A) 42000 J
  • B) 84000 J
  • C) 4200 J
  • D) 8400 J
Answer: B) 84000 J. E = mcΔT = 2 × 4200 × 10 = 84000 J.
Q17 · IGCSE Physics · Electricity · Medium

Calculate the power of a device using 10 A at 12 V.

  • A) 1.2 W
  • B) 12 W
  • C) 120 W
  • D) 1200 W
Answer: C) 120 W. P = VI = 12 × 10 = 120 W.

Group 3 · Chemistry & Biology (6 questions)

Q3 · IGCSE Chemistry (0620) · Atomic Structure

What is the number of neutrons in an atom of ²³Na (sodium-23)?

  • A) 11
  • B) 12
  • C) 23
  • D) 34
Answer: B) 12. Mass number = 23, Atomic number = 11. Neutrons = 23 − 11 = 12.
Q9 · IGCSE Chemistry · Bonding · Easy

Which type of bond is formed when sodium (Na) transfers an electron to chlorine (Cl)?

  • A) Covalent
  • B) Ionic
  • C) Metallic
  • D) Hydrogen
Answer: B) Ionic. Sodium loses an electron to become Na⁺, chlorine gains it to become Cl⁻, forming an ionic bond.
Q10 · IGCSE Biology · Cells · Easy

Which organelle is responsible for photosynthesis in plant cells?

  • A) Mitochondria
  • B) Nucleus
  • C) Chloroplast
  • D) Ribosome
Answer: C) Chloroplast. Chloroplasts contain chlorophyll which captures light energy for photosynthesis.
Q18 · IGCSE Chemistry · Formulae · Easy

What is the chemical formula for water?

  • A) H₂O
  • B) CO₂
  • C) HO₂
  • D) H₂O₂
Answer: A) H₂O.
Q19 · IGCSE Chemistry · Equations · Medium

Balance: __H₂ + __O₂ → __H₂O.

  • A) 1, 1, 1
  • B) 2, 1, 2
  • C) 2, 2, 2
  • D) 1, 2, 1
Answer: B) 2, 1, 2. 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O.
Q20 · IGCSE Biology · Blood · Easy

Which blood cells are responsible for fighting infection?

  • A) Red blood cells
  • B) White blood cells
  • C) Platelets
  • D) Plasma
Answer: B) White blood cells.
