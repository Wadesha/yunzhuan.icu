Q1 · IGCSE Math (0580) Extended · Number

A shop gives a 15% discount on a jacket originally priced at $80. What is the sale price?

  • A) $12
  • B) $68
  • C) $65
  • D) $72
Answer: B) $68. Discount = 15% × $80 = $12. Sale price = $80 − $12 = $68.
Q2 · IGCSE Physics (0625) · Mechanics

A car travels 120 km in 2 hours. What is its average speed in m/s?

  • A) 60 m/s
  • B) 16.7 m/s
  • C) 30 m/s
  • D) 120 m/s
Answer: B) 16.7 m/s. 120 km = 120,000 m. 2 hours = 7,200 s. Speed = 120,000 / 7,200 ≈ 16.7 m/s.
Q3 · IGCSE Chemistry (0620) · Atomic Structure

What is the number of neutrons in an atom of ²³Na (sodium-23)?

  • A) 11
  • B) 12
  • C) 23
  • D) 34
Answer: B) 12. Mass number = 23, Atomic number = 11. Neutrons = 23 − 11 = 12.
Q4 · IGCSE Math · Algebra · Easy

Factorise: x² + 5x + 6.

  • A) (x + 1)(x + 6)
  • B) (x + 2)(x + 3)
  • C) (x − 2)(x − 3)
  • D) (x + 1)(x + 5)
Answer: B) (x + 2)(x + 3). We need two numbers that multiply to 6 and add to 5: 2 and 3.
Q5 · IGCSE Math · Geometry · Medium

A circle has radius 7 cm. What is its area? (π = 22/7)

  • A) 154 cm²
  • B) 44 cm²
  • C) 22 cm²
  • D) 308 cm²
Answer: A) 154 cm². A = πr² = (22/7) × 49 = 22 × 7 = 154 cm².
Q6 · IGCSE Math · Statistics · Medium

The mean of 5 numbers is 12. If a 6th number is added and the mean becomes 13, what is the 6th number?

  • A) 13
  • B) 15
  • C) 18
  • D) 78
Answer: C) 18. Sum of 5 = 60. Sum of 6 = 78. 6th number = 78 − 60 = 18.
Q7 · IGCSE Physics · Electricity · Easy

What is the current in a circuit with a 12 V battery and a 4 Ω resistor?

  • A) 48 A
  • B) 3 A
  • C) 8 A
  • D) 0.33 A
Answer: B) 3 A. I = V / R = 12 / 4 = 3 A.
Q8 · IGCSE Physics · Waves · Medium

Sound travels at 330 m/s in air. If a sound has wavelength 1.5 m, what is its frequency?

  • A) 165 Hz
  • B) 220 Hz
  • C) 331.5 Hz
  • D) 495 Hz
Answer: B) 220 Hz. f = v / λ = 330 / 1.5 = 220 Hz.
Q9 · IGCSE Chemistry · Bonding · Easy

Which type of bond is formed when sodium (Na) transfers an electron to chlorine (Cl)?

  • A) Covalent
  • B) Ionic
  • C) Metallic
  • D) Hydrogen
Answer: B) Ionic. Sodium loses an electron to become Na⁺, chlorine gains it to become Cl⁻, forming an ionic bond.
Q10 · IGCSE Biology · Cells · Easy

Which organelle is responsible for photosynthesis in plant cells?

  • A) Mitochondria
  • B) Nucleus
  • C) Chloroplast
  • D) Ribosome
Answer: C) Chloroplast. Chloroplasts contain chlorophyll which captures light energy for photosynthesis.
