知识点大纲

以下以 Mathematics (0580) Extended 为示例，列出 5 大模块知识大纲。

Number

  • Decimals, Fractions, Percentages
  • Ratio, Standard Form

Algebra

  • Equations, Factorisation
  • Sequences, Inequalities

Geometry

  • Angles, Polygons, Circles
  • Constructions, Transformations

Trigonometry

  • Pythagoras, SOHCAHTOA
  • Sine / Cosine Rule

Statistics

  • Mean / Median / Mode
  • Histograms, Probability, Tree Diagrams
