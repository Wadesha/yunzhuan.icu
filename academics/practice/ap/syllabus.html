多数大学以 3 分及以上为通过（qualified），顶尖院校常要求 4-5 分方可换学分。

知识点大纲

以下以 AP Calculus BC 为示例，列出 10 大模块及权重。

  • Limits and Continuity (10-12%)
  • Differentiation: Definition and Properties (10-12%)
  • Differentiation: Composite and Implicit (9-13%)
  • Contextual Applications of Differentiation (6-9%)
  • Analytical Applications of Differentiation (8-11%)
  • Integration and Accumulation of Change (17-20%)
  • Differential Equations (6-9%)
  • Applications of Integration (6-9%)
  • Parametric / Polar / Vector (11-12%)
  • Infinite Sequences and Series (17-18%)
