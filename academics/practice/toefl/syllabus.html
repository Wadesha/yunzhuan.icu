Writing (29min · 2 Tasks · 0-30)

Integrated Writing (Read 3min · Listen 2min lecture反驳 · Write 20min 150-225w)

  • 结构：阅读 3 点支持 X；教授逐点反驳（3 counter-reasons）→ 引用双方 + 过渡连接
    对应Q： Q9‧Q45‧Q105‧Q106‧Q113 · 刷全本Topic（v14生效）
    • 对应Q： Q9‧Q45‧Q105‧Q106‧Q113 · 刷全本Topic（v14生效）
  • 答题结构：
    • Intro：The reading and the lecture are both about ___. The author of the reading presents three reasons to support ___. However, the lecturer casts doubt on each of these arguments.
    • Body 1：First, the reading claims that ___. The lecturer refutes this by saying that ___.
    • Body 2：Second, the reading suggests that ___. The lecturer argues that ___.
    • Body 3：Third, the reading posits that ___. The lecturer counters that ___.

Academic Discussion (new! 10min · 100+ words · 代替原Independent)

  • 结构：教授抛讨论题 + 2 学生发言 → 你的贡献：表态 + 自己的例子理由 + 和同学观点呼应或反驳
    对应Q： Q3‧Q18‧Q19‧Q46‧Q47‧Q48‧Q107‧Q108‧Q109‧Q110 · 刷全本Topic（v14生效）
    • 对应Q： Q3‧Q18‧Q19‧Q46‧Q47‧Q48‧Q107‧Q108‧Q109‧Q110 · 刷全本Topic（v14生效）
  • 答题结构：
    • 先表态：I agree with ___ that ___, but I would also add that ___.
    • 自己的理由/例子：For example, in my own experience / A good illustration of this is ___.
    • 呼应同学：Unlike ___ who argues that ___, I believe ___ because ___.
