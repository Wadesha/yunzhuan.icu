Q1 · TOEFL Reading · Vocabulary in Context
Passage excerpt: 'The discovery of penicillin was largely serendipitous, arising from an accidental contamination of a bacterial culture.' The word 'serendipitous' in the passage is closest in meaning to:
  • A) planned
  • B) fortunate
  • C) unusual
  • D) deliberate
答案：B) fortunate. Serendipitous means a happy accident or fortunate discovery, especially in the context of scientific discoveries.
Q2 · TOEFL Reading · Inference
Passage: 'While the Industrial Revolution brought unprecedented economic growth, it also led to widespread environmental degradation that would not be fully understood for decades.' It can be inferred that:
  • A) Environmental degradation was immediately recognized
  • B) The economic benefits outweighed environmental costs
  • C) The full environmental impact was not apparent at the time
  • D) Industrial Revolution had no environmental impact
答案：C). The phrase 'would not be fully understood for decades' implies the impact was not recognized at the time.
Q3 · TOEFL Writing · Independent (Practice Prompt)
Do you agree or disagree with the following statement? 'It is better to study alone than to study in a group.' Use specific reasons and examples to support your answer.
写作提示：Aim for 300+ words. Structure: Introduction (thesis) → 2-3 body paragraphs (each with a clear reason + example) → Conclusion (restate thesis). Spend 3-5 min planning, 20 min writing, 3 min reviewing.
