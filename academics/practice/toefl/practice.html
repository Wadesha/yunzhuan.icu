Group 1 · Reading

Q1 【RD-1e】 · TOEFL Reading · Vocabulary in Context · Easy
Passage excerpt: 'The discovery of penicillin was largely serendipitous, arising from an accidental contamination of a bacterial culture.' The word 'serendipitous' in the passage is closest in meaning to:
  • A) planned
  • B) fortunate
  • C) unusual
  • D) deliberate
答案：B) fortunate. Serendipitous means a happy accident or fortunate discovery, especially in the context of scientific discoveries.
Q2 【RD-1c】 · TOEFL Reading · Inference · Medium
Passage: 'While the Industrial Revolution brought unprecedented economic growth, it also led to widespread environmental degradation that would not be fully understood for decades.' It can be inferred that:
  • A) Environmental degradation was immediately recognized
  • B) The economic benefits outweighed environmental costs
  • C) The full environmental impact was not apparent at the time
  • D) Industrial Revolution had no environmental impact
答案：C). The phrase 'would not be fully understood for decades' implies the impact was not recognized at the time.
Q4 【RD-1a】 · TOEFL Reading · Detail · Easy
Passage: 'Photosynthesis occurs in two stages: the light-dependent reactions, which take place in the thylakoid membranes, and the Calvin cycle, which occurs in the stroma.' Where does the Calvin cycle take place?
  • A) Thylakoid membranes
  • B) Stroma
  • C) Cell wall
  • D) Nucleus
答案：B) Stroma. The passage explicitly states the Calvin cycle occurs in the stroma.
Q5 【RD-1d】 · TOEFL Reading · Rhetorical Purpose · Medium
Passage: 'The professor's theory, though initially dismissed by the academic community, eventually gained widespread acceptance after independent verification.' Why does the author mention 'independent verification'?
  • A) To show the professor was wrong
  • B) To explain why the theory was initially dismissed
  • C) To explain why the theory was eventually accepted
  • D) To criticize the academic community
答案：C). The independent verification is what led to the theory's acceptance.
Q10 【RD-1g】 · TOEFL Reading · Sentence Simplification · Hard
Passage: 'Notwithstanding the formidable challenges posed by the mountainous terrain, the engineering team persevered, ultimately completing the railway ahead of schedule.' Which best simplifies this sentence?
  • A) The team gave up because of the mountains
  • B) Despite difficult terrain, the team finished the railway early
  • C) The railway was completed on schedule
  • D) The mountains made the project impossible
答案：B). "Notwithstanding" = despite; "formidable challenges" = difficult; "ahead of schedule" = early.
Q11 【RD-1e】 · TOEFL Reading · Vocabulary in Context · Medium
Passage: 'The novel's protagonist was known for her duplicity, often saying one thing while doing another.' The word 'duplicity' most nearly means:
  • A) honesty
  • B) deceit
  • C) kindness
  • D) courage
答案：B) deceit. Duplicity means deceitfulness or double-dealing, which matches the clue "saying one thing while doing another."
Q12 【RD-1c】 · TOEFL Reading · Inference · Hard
Passage: 'The ancient city's walls, though crumbling, still bore marks of their former grandeur—faded frescoes and intricate carvings that spoke of a civilization long past.' It can be inferred that:
  • A) The city is still prosperous
  • B) The city was once magnificent but is now in decay
  • C) The walls were recently built
  • D) The frescoes are new
答案：B). Words like "crumbling," "faded," and "long past" indicate former grandeur now in decay.
Q13 【RD-1h】 · TOEFL Reading · Insert Text · Medium
Passage: '[1] The researchers found unexpected results. [2] The bacteria had developed resistance overnight. [3] This suggested rapid evolution. [4] Further studies confirmed the finding.' Where does this sentence best fit: 'This phenomenon had never been observed before.'?
  • A) After [1]
  • B) After [2]
  • C) After [3]
  • D) After [4]
答案：B) After [2]. The new sentence explains the significance of the resistance finding before [3] draws a conclusion.
Q21 【RD-1b】 · TOEFL Reading · Negative Factual · Medium
Passage: 'Early Viking longships were constructed using clinker-built planks, propelled by both square sails and rows of oars, and steered with a side-mounted rudder. They featured shallow drafts that allowed river navigation, and decorative figureheads at the prow.' According to the passage, all of the following are features of Viking longships EXCEPT:
  • A) Clinker-built planks
  • B) Square sails and oars
  • C) Deep keels for ocean stability
  • D) Side-mounted rudder
答案：C). The passage mentions "shallow drafts," which contradicts "deep keels." The other options are stated explicitly.
Q22 【RD-1f】 · TOEFL Reading · Reference (Pronoun) · Easy
Passage: 'Coral reefs support roughly 25% of all marine species despite covering less than 1% of the ocean floor. They are highly sensitive to temperature changes, which can trigger bleaching events.' The word 'They' in the second sentence refers to:
  • A) Marine species
  • B) Coral reefs
  • C) Temperature changes
  • D) Bleaching events
答案：B) Coral reefs. The pronoun "They" refers back to the subject of the previous sentence, which is "Coral reefs."
Q23 【RD-1e】 · TOEFL Reading · Vocabulary (Academic) · Hard
Passage: 'The researcher's methodology was deemed untenable by the review committee, as the sample size was insufficient to support any statistically meaningful conclusion.' The word 'untenable' most nearly means:
  • A) innovative
  • B) defensible
  • C) indefensible
  • D) complicated
答案：C) indefensible. "Untenable" means unable to be defended or justified, which matches the context of insufficient sample size.
Q24 【RD-1h】 · TOEFL Reading · Insert Text · Hard
Passage: '[1] The Mayan civilization developed a sophisticated writing system. [2] It combined logograms with syllabic signs. [3] Scholars spent over a century deciphering it. [4] Today, most ancient Mayan texts can be read.' Where does this sentence best fit: 'This dual structure made the script particularly difficult to decode.'?
  • A) After [1]
  • B) After [2]
  • C) After [3]
  • D) After [4]
答案：B) After [2]. "This dual structure" refers to the combination of logograms and syllabic signs mentioned in [2].
Q25 【RD-1i】 · TOEFL Reading · Prose Summary · Hard
Passage (abridged): 'The Renaissance transformed European art through the rediscovery of classical techniques. Artists like Leonardo and Michelangelo studied anatomy to render the human form accurately. The invention of oil paint allowed for richer colors and finer detail. Patronage from wealthy families, particularly the Medici, funded ambitious projects. Perspective systems gave paintings new realism.' Which three statements best express the most important ideas? (Choose 3)
  • A) Oil paint was the single most important innovation of the Renaissance.
  • B) Classical techniques were rediscovered and adapted by Renaissance artists.
  • C) Leonardo and Michelangelo were the only great Renaissance artists.
  • D) Wealthy patronage enabled ambitious artistic projects.
  • E) Perspective systems brought new realism to paintings.
  • F) The Medici family controlled all European art.
答案：B, D, E. These capture the three main ideas: classical revival, patronage, and perspective. A is too narrow; C and F are factually incorrect or unsupported.
Q26 【RD-1j】 · TOEFL Reading · Table Completion · Hard
Passage: 'Migratory birds navigate using multiple cues: the sun compass by day, the star compass at night, and the magnetic compass at all times. Landmarks such as coastlines and mountain ranges provide supplementary guidance during daylight.' Complete the table by matching each cue to its condition of use:
  • A) Sun compass → Day; Star compass → Night; Magnetic compass → Always

  • C) Sun compass → Always; Star compass → Night; Magnetic compass → Day

Q27 【RD-1c】 · TOEFL Reading · Inference · Medium

Q28 【RD-1d】 · TOEFL Reading · Rhetorical Purpose · Hard
Passage: 'The honeybee's waggle dance is a remarkable example of symbolic communication in non-human animals. Unlike the simple round dance, which merely indicates nearby food, the waggle dance conveys both direction and distance.' Why does the author mention the round dance?
  • A) To argue that the round dance is superior
  • B) To contrast it with the waggle dance and highlight the waggle dance's complexity

Q29 【RD-1a】 · TOEFL Reading · Detail · Easy
Passage: 'The water cycle consists of three main stages: evaporation, in which water turns to vapor; condensation, in which vapor forms clouds; and precipitation, in which water returns to the surface as rain or snow.' According to the passage, what happens during condensation?
  • A) Water turns to vapor
  • B) Vapor forms clouds
  • C) Water returns as rain
  • D) Water freezes into ice

Q30 【RD-TOP-1m】 · TOEFL Reading · Main Idea · Medium
Passage: 'Symbiotic relationships take many forms in nature. In mutualism, both species benefit, as seen in bees and flowers. In commensalism, one benefits while the other is unaffected, like barnacles on whales. In parasitism, one benefits at the other's expense, as with ticks on mammals.' What is the main idea of the passage?
  • A) Bees and flowers depend on each other.
  • B) Parasites harm their hosts.
  • C) Symbiotic relationships exist in three main forms with different effects on each species.
  • D) Barnacles are harmless to whales.
答案：C). The passage classifies symbiotic relationships into three types and explains each, making this the overarching main idea.
Q31 【RD-1g】 · TOEFL Reading · Sentence Simplification · Medium
Passage: 'Although the initial experiment yielded results that appeared to contradict the established theory, subsequent replications demonstrated that the anomaly was attributable to a flaw in the experimental apparatus.' Which best simplifies this sentence?
  • A) The experiment disproved the established theory.
  • B) Later replications showed the contradictory result was caused by faulty equipment.
  • C) The experiment was perfectly designed.
  • D) The anomaly confirmed the theory.
答案：B). "Subsequent replications" = later replications; "attributable to a flaw in the experimental apparatus" = caused by faulty equipment.
Q32 【RD-1d】 · TOEFL Reading · Author's Purpose · Hard
Passage: 'Many historians describe the fall of Rome as a sudden collapse. In reality, the process unfolded over centuries, with the Eastern Empire enduring nearly a thousand years longer than the West.' Why does the author include the second sentence?
  • A) To support the common view of a sudden collapse
  • B) To correct a common misconception about the speed of Rome's decline
  • C) To praise the Eastern Empire's military
  • D) To compare Rome and Greece
答案：B). The phrase "In reality" signals a correction to the preceding common description of a "sudden collapse."

Group 3 · Speaking

Q8 【SPK-3a】 · TOEFL Speaking · Task 1 · Independent · Easy
Some people prefer to live in small towns, while others prefer big cities. Which do you prefer and why? You have 15 seconds to prepare and 45 seconds to speak.
口语提示：State preference clearly in 5 seconds. Give 2 reasons with brief examples in 35 seconds. Conclude in 5 seconds. Aim for ~100 words.
Q16 【SPK-3b】 · TOEFL Speaking · Task 2 · Integrated · Medium
Read a campus announcement about a new policy (45 sec). Listen to two students discussing it (90 sec). State the woman's opinion and explain her reasons. (60 sec).
口语提示：Structure: Opinion (5 sec) → Reason 1 + detail (25 sec) → Reason 2 + detail (25 sec) → Brief conclusion (5 sec).
Q17 【SPK-3d】 · TOEFL Speaking · Task 4 · Integrated · Hard
Listen to a lecture about animal camouflage (90 sec). Explain two types of camouflage discussed. (60 sec).
口语提示：Structure: Intro (5 sec) → Type 1 + example (25 sec) → Type 2 + example (25 sec) → Conclusion (5 sec).
Q41 【SPK-3a】 · TOEFL Speaking · Task 1 Preferences · Easy
Some people prefer to study in the morning, while others prefer to study at night. Which do you prefer, and why? You have 15 seconds to prepare and 45 seconds to speak.
口语提示：State your preference in the first sentence. Give one strong reason with a personal example. Mention a brief counterpoint if time allows. Aim for ~100 words, steady pace, clear pronunciation.
Q42 【SPK-3a】 · TOEFL Speaking · Task 1 Agree/Disagree · Medium
Do you agree or disagree with the following statement? 'Students should be required to learn a second language in school.' Use specific reasons and examples to support your answer. 15 sec prep, 45 sec speak.
口语提示：Choose one side clearly. State agreement or disagreement in sentence 1. Give 2 reasons in sentences 2-3 with brief examples. Conclude in the final sentence. Avoid "I think" repetition.
Q43 【SPK-3c】 · TOEFL Speaking · Task 3 Integrated Concept · Hard
Read a short passage defining the psychological concept of 'confirmation bias' (45 sec). Then listen to a lecture where the professor gives an example of a manager who only notices evidence supporting his hiring decision (90 sec). Explain how the example illustrates confirmation bias. (60 sec).
口语提示：Structure: Define the concept in 1-2 sentences (10 sec) → Summarize the professor's example (35 sec) → Connect the example back to the concept (15 sec). Use phrases like "The professor illustrates this by..." and "This shows that..."
Q44 【SPK-3d】 · TOEFL Speaking · Task 4 Lecture Summary · Hard
Listen to a lecture about two strategies animals use to conserve energy during food scarcity: hibernation and torpor (90 sec). Explain the two strategies and how they differ. (60 sec).
口语提示：Structure: Intro stating the topic (5 sec) → Strategy 1 (hibernation) with key details (25 sec) → Strategy 2 (torpor) with key details (25 sec) → Brief contrast (5 sec). Use signal words: "The first strategy is...", "In contrast, the second..."

Group 4 · Writing

Q3 【WR-4b】 · TOEFL Writing · Independent (Practice Prompt) · Medium
Do you agree or disagree with the following statement? 'It is better to study alone than to study in a group.' Use specific reasons and examples to support your answer.
写作提示：Aim for 300+ words. Structure: Introduction (thesis) → 2-3 body paragraphs (each with a clear reason + example) → Conclusion (restate thesis). Spend 3-5 min planning, 20 min writing, 3 min reviewing.
Q9 【WR-4a】 · TOEFL Writing · Integrated · Hard
Read a passage about the benefits of remote work (200 words, 3 min). Then listen to a lecture that challenges each point (2 min). Write a 150-225 word summary of how the lecture challenges the reading.
写作提示：Structure: Intro (1 sentence: the lecture challenges the reading) → 3 body paragraphs (each: reading's claim → lecture's counter) → No conclusion needed. Use reporting verbs: "The professor argues...", "According to the reading...".
Q18 【WR-4b】 · TOEFL Writing · Independent · Medium
Do you agree or disagree? 'Technology has made children less creative.' Use specific reasons and examples.
写作提示：Choose a clear position. If disagree: technology enables creativity (digital art, coding, music production). Aim for 300+ words, 4 paragraphs.
Q19 【WR-4b】 · TOEFL Writing · Independent · Hard
Some people prefer to learn from books. Others prefer to learn from experience. Which do you prefer?
写作提示：This is a preference question. Balance both sides but take a clear stance. Use personal examples to support.
Q20 【WR-4b】 · TOEFL Writing · Grammar Tip · Easy
Identify the error: 'Each of the students have a textbook.'
  • A) Each
  • B) have
  • C) a
  • D) textbook
答案：B) have → has. "Each" is singular, so the verb must be "has."
Q45 【WR-4a】 · TOEFL Writing · Integrated Reading+Lecture · Hard
Read a passage arguing that the extinction of the dinosaurs was caused primarily by volcanic activity (250 words, 3 min). Then listen to a lecture that presents evidence supporting the asteroid-impact hypothesis and refutes each volcanic point (2 min). Write a 150-225 word summary explaining how the lecture challenges the reading.
写作提示：Open with one sentence stating the lecture opposes the reading. Then write 3 paragraphs, each: (1) what the reading claims, (2) what the professor says in response. Do NOT give your opinion. Use verbs like "contends," "asserts," "counters."
Q46 【WR-4b】 · TOEFL Writing · Independent Agree/Disagree · Medium
Do you agree or disagree with the following statement? 'Successful people are those who take risks.' Use specific reasons and examples to support your answer. (30 min, 300+ words)
写作提示：Take a clear stance. If agree: cite entrepreneurs, explorers. If disagree: argue preparation and persistence matter more. Structure: Intro (thesis) → 2-3 body paragraphs with examples → Conclusion. Vary sentence structure.
Q47 【WR-4b】 · TOEFL Writing · Independent Preference · Medium
Some people prefer to spend their free time outdoors. Others prefer to spend it indoors. Which do you prefer? Use specific reasons and examples. (30 min, 300+ words)
写作提示：State preference clearly in the introduction. Give 2-3 reasons with personal examples. Acknowledge the other side briefly. Conclude by restating your preference. Use transitions: "First," "Furthermore," "On the other hand."
Q48 【WR-4b】 · TOEFL Writing · Independent Compare · Hard
Compare the advantages of living in a small town with those of living in a large city. Which do you find more appealing? Use specific reasons and examples. (30 min, 300+ words)
写作提示：This requires genuine comparison. Dedicate one body paragraph to small-town advantages (community, quiet), one to city advantages (opportunity, culture), then state your preference with reasoning. Avoid listing without analysis.
Q49 【WR-4b】 · TOEFL Writing · Grammar MCQ · Easy
Choose the correct sentence:
  • A) Neither the manager nor the employees was aware of the change.
  • B) Neither the manager nor the employees were aware of the change.
  • C) Neither the manager or the employees were aware of the change.
  • D) Neither the manager nor the employees is aware of the change.
答案：B). With "neither...nor," the verb agrees with the nearer subject ("employees," which is plural). "Or" is incorrect; "nor" is required.
Q50 【WR-4b】 · TOEFL Writing · Grammar MCQ · Medium
Identify the sentence with correct punctuation:
  • A) The report which was due yesterday, is finally complete.
  • B) The report, which was due yesterday, is finally complete.
  • C) The report, which was due yesterday is finally complete.
  • D) The report which was due yesterday is finally, complete.
答案：B). "Which was due yesterday" is a non-restrictive clause and must be set off by commas on both sides. The other options misplace or omit commas.

Q111 【WR-4b】 · TOEFL Writing · Grammar MCQ · Easy
Identify the error: 'The committee was divided in their opinions about the proposal.'
  • A) committee
  • B) was
  • C) their
  • D) about
答案：C) their → its. "Committee" is treated as singular here ("was"), so the pronoun should be "its," not "their."
Q112 【WR-4b】 · TOEFL Writing · Grammar MCQ · Medium
Choose the correct sentence:

  • B) The data show a clear trend toward urbanization.
  • C) The data showing a clear trend toward urbanization.
  • D) The data shown a clear trend toward urbanization.
答案：B). In academic writing, "data" is treated as plural (singular: datum), so "show" is correct. Sentence A is acceptable in informal contexts but B is preferred formally.

Q118 【WR-4b】 · TOEFL Writing · Grammar MCQ · Easy
Identify the error: 'She is one of the students who has passed the entrance exam.'
  • A) is
  • B) who
  • C) has
  • D) the
答案：C) has → have. In "one of the students who," the relative pronoun "who" refers to "students" (plural), so the verb should be "have."
Q119 【WR-4b】 · TOEFL Writing · Grammar MCQ · Medium
Choose the sentence with correct parallel structure:
  • A) She likes hiking, swimming, and to run.
  • B) She likes to hike, swim, and running.
  • C) She likes hiking, swimming, and running.
  • D) She likes hiking, to swim, and running.
答案：C). All three items use the -ing gerund form, maintaining parallel structure.
Q120 【WR-4b】 · TOEFL Writing · Grammar MCQ · Hard
Identify the error: 'Not only the students but also the teacher have expressed concern about the schedule.'
  • A) Not only
  • B) but also
  • C) have
  • D) about
答案：C) have → has. With "not only...but also," the verb agrees with the nearer subject ("teacher," singular), so "has" is correct.
