Group 1 · Math (8 questions)

Q1 · ACT Math · Pre-Algebra · Medium
A shirt is marked up 40% from its cost of $25. What is the selling price?
  • A) $10
  • B) $35
  • C) $40
  • D) $45
  • E) $50
Answer: B) $35. Markup = 40% × $25 = $10，Selling price = $25 + $10 = $35.
Q4 · ACT Math · Coordinate Geometry · Medium
What is the slope of the line passing through (2, 3) and (6, 11)?
  • A) 1/2
  • B) 2
  • C) 4
  • D) 8
  • E) −2
Answer: B) 2. Slope = (11 − 3) / (6 − 2) = 8 / 4 = 2.
Q5 · ACT Math · Plane Geometry · Easy
What is the sum of interior angles of a hexagon?
  • A) 360°
  • B) 540°
  • C) 720°
  • D) 900°
  • E) 1080°
Answer: C) 720°. Sum = (n − 2) × 180 = (6 − 2) × 180 = 720°.
Q9 · ACT Math · Trigonometry · Hard
If sin(θ) = 3/5 and θ is in the first quadrant, what is cos(θ)?
  • A) 3/4
  • B) 4/5
  • C) 5/3
  • D) 5/4
  • E) 3/5
Answer: B) 4/5. sin² + cos² = 1，cos² = 1 − 9/25 = 16/25，cos = 4/5（第一象限为正值）。
Q11 · ACT Math · Pre-Algebra · Easy
What is 25% of 80?
  • A) 15
  • B) 20
  • C) 25
  • D) 30
  • E) 40
Answer: B) 20. 0.25 × 80 = 20.
Q12 · ACT Math · Elementary Algebra · Medium
If 3x − 7 = 14, what is x?
  • A) 5
  • B) 6
  • C) 7
  • D) 8
  • E) 21
Answer: C) 7. 3x = 21，x = 7.
Q13 · ACT Math · Intermediate Algebra · Hard
If f(x) = x² − 3x + 2, what are the roots?
  • A) x = 1, x = 2
  • B) x = −1, x = −2
  • C) x = 1, x = 3
  • D) x = 2, x = 3
  • E) x = 0
Answer: A) x = 1, x = 2. 因式分解：(x − 1)(x − 2) = 0，故 x = 1 或 x = 2.
Q14 · ACT Math · Plane Geometry · Medium
A circle has diameter 10. What is its circumference? (π ≈ 3.14)
  • A) 15.7
  • B) 31.4
  • C) 78.5
  • D) 62.8
  • E) 100
Answer: B) 31.4. C = πd = 3.14 × 10 = 31.4.

Group 2 · English (5 questions)

Q2 · ACT English · Usage · Easy
Choose the best version: “Neither the students nor the teacher _____ aware of the schedule change.”
  • A) was
  • B) were
  • C) is
  • D) are
  • E) being
Answer: A) was. 在 “neither…nor” 结构中，谓语动词与最近的主语保持一致（“teacher” 为单数），故用 was.
Q6 · ACT English · Rhetoric · Medium
Which sentence best concludes this paragraph about climate change research? The paragraph discusses recent findings on ocean temperature rise.
  • A) “In conclusion, more research is needed.”
  • B) “Ocean temperatures have risen by 0.5°C since 2015.”
  • C) “These findings underscore the urgent need for coordinated global action to reduce carbon emissions.”
  • D) “I think climate change is bad.”
  • E) “The ocean is very large.”
Answer: C). 该句综合了段落的研究发现，并给出有力的结论性观点。
Q10 · ACT English · Sentence Structure · Easy
Choose the correct sentence:
  • A) Walking down the street, the rain started to fall.

Answer: E) Both C and D. C 与 D 都修正了悬垂修饰语；A 与 B 存在悬垂修饰语错误。
Q15 · ACT English · Usage · Easy
Choose the correct form: “She has _____ the book already.”
  • A) read

Q16 · ACT English · Rhetoric · Medium
Which transition best connects: “The data shows increased sales. _____, customer satisfaction has declined.”

Group 3 · Reading (3 questions)

Q7 · ACT Reading · Inference · Hard
Passage: “The old lighthouse stood as a silent sentinel, its lamp long extinguished, guarding memories of ships that would never return.” The description of the lighthouse suggests:

Answer: A). 文段强调北极海冰以前所未有的速度加速消融，是一个严峻且加剧的问题。

Group 4 · Science (4 questions)

Q3 · ACT Science · Data Interpretation · Easy
An experiment measures plant growth under different light colors. After 14 days, plants under blue light grew 12 cm, red light 15 cm, green light 3 cm, and white light 10 cm. What is the average growth across all conditions?
  • A) 8 cm
  • B) 9 cm
  • C) 10 cm
  • D) 12 cm
  • E) 15 cm
Answer: C) 10 cm. (12 + 15 + 3 + 10) / 4 = 40 / 4 = 10 cm.
Q8 · ACT Science · Research Summary · Medium
Experiment: Students tested 3 fertilizers (A, B, C) on tomato plants. After 6 weeks: A = 25 cm, B = 32 cm, C = 18 cm, Control = 15 cm. Which fertilizer produced the greatest percentage increase over control?
  • A) Fertilizer A
  • B) Fertilizer B
  • C) Fertilizer C
  • D) All equal
  • E) Cannot determine
Answer: B). A: (25 − 15) / 15 = 67%，B: (32 − 15) / 15 = 113%，C: (18 − 15) / 15 = 20%. B 的增长率最高。
Q19 · ACT Science · Data Interpretation · Medium
An experiment measures reaction time at different temperatures: 20°C → 0.5s, 30°C → 0.3s, 40°C → 0.2s. What is the trend?
  • A) Higher temp → slower reaction
  • B) Higher temp → faster reaction
  • C) No trend
  • D) Reaction time is constant
  • E) Temperature has no effect
Answer: B) Higher temp → faster reaction. 温度升高时反应时间变短，即反应更快。
Q20 · ACT Science · Conflicting Viewpoints · Hard
Scientist A claims CO₂ is the main driver of climate change. Scientist B claims solar activity is. A key difference in their arguments is:
  • A) Both agree on CO₂
  • B) Both agree on solar activity
  • C) They identify different primary causes
  • D) Scientist A denies climate change
  • E) Scientist B denies climate change
Answer: C) They identify different primary causes. 两位科学家对气候变化的主要驱动力给出了不同的归因。

  • A) 8

Q24 · ACT Math · Pre-Algebra · Medium
If 5 workers can build a wall in 12 days, how many days would it take 6 workers to build the same wall (assuming equal rates)?
  • A) 8
  • B) 10
  • C) 14
  • D) 15
  • E) 18
Answer: B) 10. Total work = 5 × 12 = 60 worker-days；60 ÷ 6 = 10 days.

Q26 · ACT Math · Elementary Algebra · Medium
Expand (x + 3)(x − 2).

  • B) x² − x − 6
  • C) x² + x + 6
  • D) x² − 5x − 6
  • E) x² + 5x − 6
Answer: A) x² + x − 6. x² − 2x + 3x − 6 = x² + x − 6.
Q27 · ACT Math · Intermediate Algebra · Hard
Factor 2x² + 7x + 3.

  • B) (2x + 3)(x + 1)
  • C) (2x − 1)(x − 3)
  • D) (x + 3)(x + 2)
  • E) (2x + 1)(x − 3)
Answer: A) (2x + 1)(x + 3). 验证：2x² + 6x + x + 3 = 2x² + 7x + 3 ✓.
Q28 · ACT Math · Coordinate Geometry · Easy
What is the y-intercept of the line y = 3x − 7?
  • A) 3
  • B) −3
  • C) 7
  • D) −7
  • E) 0
Answer: D) −7. y = mx + b，b = −7.
Q29 · ACT Math · Coordinate Geometry · Medium
What is the distance between the points (1, 2) and (4, 6)?
  • A) 3
  • B) 4
  • C) 5
  • D) 7
  • E) 25
Answer: C) 5. d = √((4 − 1)² + (6 − 2)²) = √(9 + 16) = √25 = 5.
Q30 · ACT Math · Plane Geometry · Medium
In a triangle, two angles measure 50° and 60°. What is the measure of the third angle?
  • A) 50°
  • B) 60°
  • C) 70°
  • D) 90°
  • E) 110°
Answer: C) 70°. 三角形内角和 = 180°，第三角 = 180 − 50 − 60 = 70°.
Q31 · ACT Math · Plane Geometry · Hard
A circle has area 49π. What is its circumference?
  • A) 7π
  • B) 14π
  • C) 28π
  • D) 49π
  • E) 98π
Answer: B) 14π. A = πr² = 49π → r = 7；C = 2πr = 14π.
Q32 · ACT Math · Pre-Algebra · Easy
A fair 6-sided die is rolled. What is the probability of rolling a number greater than 4?
  • A) 1/6

  • D) 1/2
  • E) 2/3
Answer: C) 1/3. 大于 4 的有 5、6 两个，2/6 = 1/3.

Group 8 · Science (NEW) (5 questions)

Q46 · ACT Science · Data Interpretation · Easy
A data table records: Trial 1 → 5.2s, Trial 2 → 5.4s, Trial 3 → 5.3s. What is the average reaction time?
  • A) 5.0 s
  • B) 5.3 s
  • C) 5.4 s
  • D) 5.5 s
  • E) 16.0 s
Answer: B) 5.3 s. (5.2 + 5.4 + 5.3) / 3 = 15.9 / 3 = 5.3.
Q47 · ACT Science · Data Representation · Medium
A line graph shows temperature rising linearly from 20°C at t = 0 min to 60°C at t = 10 min. What is the temperature at t = 5 min?
  • A) 25°C
  • B) 30°C
  • C) 40°C
  • D) 50°C
  • E) 60°C
Answer: C) 40°C. 线性增长，t = 5 为中点，对应 20 + (60 − 20)/2 = 40°C.
Q48 · ACT Science · Research Summary · Medium
A study tests a new drug's effect on blood pressure. Which control group is most appropriate?
  • A) Patients taking a different blood pressure drug
  • B) Patients taking a placebo
  • C) Patients with normal blood pressure
  • D) Patients who exercise daily
  • E) No control group needed
Answer: B). 对照组服用安慰剂，以隔离药物本身的效果。
Q49 · ACT Science · Conflicting Viewpoints · Hard
Scientist A: Species decline is caused by habitat loss. Scientist B: Species decline is caused by climate change. Which finding would best support Scientist A over Scientist B?
  • A) Species decline correlates with rising temperatures
  • B) Species decline is greatest in regions with high deforestation, regardless of temperature change
  • C) Both habitat loss and climate change affect species
  • D) Species decline occurs worldwide
  • E) Climate change is accelerating
Answer: B). 在 “温度不变但森林砍伐高” 的地区物种衰退最严重，支持栖息地丧失为主要原因。
Q50 · ACT Science · Research Summary · Easy
An experiment tests 3 fertilizers on plant height after 4 weeks: A = 15 cm, B = 22 cm, Control = 10 cm. Fertilizer B's percentage increase over the control is approximately:
  • A) 50%
  • B) 75%
  • C) 100%
  • D) 120%
  • E) 220%
Answer: D) 120%. (22 − 10) / 10 = 1.2 = 120%.
