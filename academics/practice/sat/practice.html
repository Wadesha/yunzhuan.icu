Group 1 · Math — Algebra & Advanced Math (7 questions)

Q1 · SAT Math · Algebra · Medium
If 3x + 7 = 22, what is the value of x?
  • A) 3
  • B) 5
  • C) 7
  • D) 15
Answer: B) 5. 3x = 22 − 7 = 15，x = 5.
Q2 · SAT Math · Advanced Math · Hard
If f(x) = x² − 4x + 3, for what values of x does f(x) = 0?
  • A) x = 1, x = 3
  • B) x = −1, x = −3
  • C) x = 1, x = 4
  • D) x = 3, x = 4
Answer: A) x = 1, x = 3. 因式分解：(x − 1)(x − 3) = 0，故 x = 1 或 x = 3.
Q6 · SAT Math · Advanced Math · Hard
If f(x) = 2ˣ and f(a) = 32, what is the value of a?
  • A) 4
  • B) 5
  • C) 6
  • D) 16
Answer: B) 5. 2^a = 32 = 2⁵，故 a = 5.
Q11 · SAT Math · Algebra · Easy
If 2(x + 3) = 14, what is x?
  • A) 2
  • B) 4
  • C) 7
  • D) 11
Answer: B) 4. 2x + 6 = 14，2x = 8，x = 4.
Q12 · SAT Math · Algebra · Medium
If x/y = 3 and y = 6, what is x?
  • A) 2
  • B) 3
  • C) 9
  • D) 18
Answer: D) 18. x = 3y = 3 × 6 = 18.
Q13 · SAT Math · Advanced Math · Medium
If g(x) = x² + 2x − 3, what is g(−1)?
  • A) −4
  • B) −3
  • C) 0
  • D) −2
Answer: A) −4. g(−1) = (−1)² + 2(−1) − 3 = 1 − 2 − 3 = −4.
Q14 · SAT Math · Advanced Math · Hard
How many real solutions does x² + 4 = 0 have?
  • A) 0
  • B) 1
  • C) 2
  • D) 4
Answer: A) 0. x² = −4，由于 x² ≥ 0，故无实数解。

Group 2 · Math — Problem Solving, Data & Geometry (5 questions)

Q4 · SAT Math · Problem Solving · Easy
A store sells 3 apples for $1.50. How much would 5 apples cost?
  • A) $2.00
  • B) $2.50
  • C) $3.00
  • D) $3.50
Answer: B) $2.50. Unit price = $1.50 / 3 = $0.50，5 × $0.50 = $2.50.
Q5 · SAT Math · Geometry · Medium
A right triangle has legs of 5 and 12. What is the hypotenuse?
  • A) 13
  • B) 17
  • C) 60
  • D) 169
Answer: A) 13. c = √(5² + 12²) = √(25 + 144) = √169 = 13.
Q7 · SAT Math · Data Analysis · Medium
In a survey of 200 students, 120 play sports and 80 play an instrument. If 40 do both, how many do neither?
  • A) 0
  • B) 40
  • C) 80
  • D) 120
Answer: B) 40. Neither = 200 − (120 + 80 − 40) = 200 − 160 = 40.
Q10 · SAT Math · Geometry · Easy
What is the area of a rectangle with length 8 and width 5?
  • A) 13
  • B) 26
  • C) 40
  • D) 80
Answer: C) 40. Area = length × width = 8 × 5 = 40.
Q15 · SAT Math · Data Analysis · Medium
The average of 3, 7, 8, x is 6. What is x?
  • A) 4
  • B) 5
  • C) 6
  • D) 7
Answer: C) 6. Sum = 4 × 6 = 24，3 + 7 + 8 + x = 24，x = 6.

Group 3 · Reading & Writing (6 questions)

Q3 · SAT Reading & Writing · Standard English Conventions
Choose the best version of the underlined portion: “The team of researchers, who had been studying the effects of climate change on coral reefs, _____ their findings in a leading journal.”
  • A) has published
  • B) have published
  • C) publishes
  • D) published
Answer: D) published. 句子描述一个已完成的过去动作，主语 “team”（单数）后接非限制性定语从句 “who had been studying”，主句谓语应使用一般过去时表示已完成的动作。
Q8 · SAT Reading & Writing · Information & Ideas · Medium
Passage: “Despite its reputation for isolation, Japan maintained active trade relationships with the Netherlands and China throughout the Edo period (1603-1868).” What is the main idea of this sentence?
  • A) Japan was completely isolated during the Edo period
  • B) Japan had limited but active trade during the Edo period
  • C) The Netherlands colonized Japan
  • D) China controlled Japan's economy
Answer: B). 该句将日本 “isolated” 的名声与其实际保持的活跃贸易关系进行对比。
Q9 · SAT Reading & Writing · Craft & Structure · Hard
“The scientist's findings were _____, contradicting decades of established wisdom.” Which word best fits?
  • A) conventional
  • B) corroborating
  • C) groundbreaking
  • D) derivative
Answer: C) groundbreaking. 空格处需表达 “与既有定论相悖” 之意，“groundbreaking”（创新的、革命性的）最贴合。
Q16 · SAT Reading & Writing · Standard English Conventions · Easy
Choose the correct word: “The students _____ happy with their results.”
  • A) is
  • B) are
  • C) was
  • D) being
Answer: B) are. 复数主语 “students” 需搭配复数谓语动词。
Q17 · SAT Reading & Writing · Standard English Conventions · Medium
Which sentence uses a semicolon correctly?
  • A) I went to the store; and bought milk.
  • B) I went to the store; I bought milk.
  • C) I went to the store; because I needed milk.
  • D) I went; to the store.
Answer: B). 分号用于连接两个独立分句。
Q18 · SAT Reading & Writing · Information & Ideas · Hard
Passage: “The author's use of first-person narration creates an intimacy that third-person could not achieve.” What is the author's purpose in using first-person?
  • A) To confuse readers
  • B) To create intimacy
  • C) To show omniscience
  • D) To distance the narrator
Answer: B) To create intimacy，正如文中所明示。

What is 15% of 200?

  • B) 20
  • C) 30
  • D) 45
Answer: C) 30. 0.15 × 200 = 30.
Q20 · SAT Reading & Writing · Craft & Structure · Medium
Choose the best transition: “The hike was exhausting. _____, the view from the summit made it worthwhile.”

  • B) However
  • C) Furthermore
  • D) Similarly
Answer: B) However. 第二句与第一句构成转折关系。
