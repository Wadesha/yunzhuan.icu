Group 1 · Math — Algebra & Advanced Math (7 questions)

Q1 · SAT Math · Algebra · Medium
If 3x + 7 = 22, what is the value of x?
  • A) 3
  • B) 5
  • C) 7
  • D) 15
Answer: B) 5. 3x = 22 − 7 = 15，x = 5.
Q2 · SAT Math · Advanced Math · Hard
If f(x) = x² − 4x + 3, for what values of x does f(x) = 0?
  • A) x = 1, x = 3
  • B) x = −1, x = −3
  • C) x = 1, x = 4
  • D) x = 3, x = 4
Answer: A) x = 1, x = 3. 因式分解：(x − 1)(x − 3) = 0，故 x = 1 或 x = 3.
Q6 · SAT Math · Advanced Math · Hard
If f(x) = 2ˣ and f(a) = 32, what is the value of a?
  • A) 4
  • B) 5
  • C) 6
  • D) 16
Answer: B) 5. 2^a = 32 = 2⁵，故 a = 5.
Q11 · SAT Math · Algebra · Easy
If 2(x + 3) = 14, what is x?
  • A) 2
  • B) 4
  • C) 7
  • D) 11
Answer: B) 4. 2x + 6 = 14，2x = 8，x = 4.
Q12 · SAT Math · Algebra · Medium
If x/y = 3 and y = 6, what is x?
  • A) 2
  • B) 3
  • C) 9
  • D) 18
Answer: D) 18. x = 3y = 3 × 6 = 18.
Q13 · SAT Math · Advanced Math · Medium
If g(x) = x² + 2x − 3, what is g(−1)?
  • A) −4
  • B) −3
  • C) 0
  • D) −2
Answer: A) −4. g(−1) = (−1)² + 2(−1) − 3 = 1 − 2 − 3 = −4.
Q14 · SAT Math · Advanced Math · Hard
How many real solutions does x² + 4 = 0 have?
  • A) 0
  • B) 1
  • C) 2
  • D) 4
Answer: A) 0. x² = −4，由于 x² ≥ 0，故无实数解。

Group 2 · Math — Problem Solving, Data & Geometry (5 questions)

Q4 · SAT Math · Problem Solving · Easy
A store sells 3 apples for $1.50. How much would 5 apples cost?
  • A) $2.00
  • B) $2.50
  • C) $3.00
  • D) $3.50
Answer: B) $2.50. Unit price = $1.50 / 3 = $0.50，5 × $0.50 = $2.50.
Q5 · SAT Math · Geometry · Medium
A right triangle has legs of 5 and 12. What is the hypotenuse?
  • A) 13
  • B) 17
  • C) 60
  • D) 169
Answer: A) 13. c = √(5² + 12²) = √(25 + 144) = √169 = 13.
Q7 · SAT Math · Data Analysis · Medium
In a survey of 200 students, 120 play sports and 80 play an instrument. If 40 do both, how many do neither?
  • A) 0
  • B) 40
  • C) 80
  • D) 120
Answer: B) 40. Neither = 200 − (120 + 80 − 40) = 200 − 160 = 40.
Q10 · SAT Math · Geometry · Easy
What is the area of a rectangle with length 8 and width 5?
  • A) 13
  • B) 26
  • C) 40
  • D) 80
Answer: C) 40. Area = length × width = 8 × 5 = 40.
Q15 · SAT Math · Data Analysis · Medium
The average of 3, 7, 8, x is 6. What is x?
  • A) 4
  • B) 5
  • C) 6
  • D) 7
Answer: C) 6. Sum = 4 × 6 = 24，3 + 7 + 8 + x = 24，x = 6.

Group 3 · Reading & Writing (6 questions)

Q3 · SAT Reading & Writing · Standard English Conventions · Easy
Choose the best version of the underlined portion: “The team of researchers, who had been studying the effects of climate change on coral reefs, _____ their findings in a leading journal.”
  • A) has published
  • B) have published
  • C) publishes
  • D) published
Answer: D) published. 句子描述一个已完成的过去动作，主语 “team”（单数）后接非限制性定语从句 “who had been studying”，主句谓语应使用一般过去时表示已完成的动作。
Q8 · SAT Reading & Writing · Information & Ideas · Medium
Passage: “Despite its reputation for isolation, Japan maintained active trade relationships with the Netherlands and China throughout the Edo period (1603-1868).” What is the main idea of this sentence?
  • A) Japan was completely isolated during the Edo period
  • B) Japan had limited but active trade during the Edo period
  • C) The Netherlands colonized Japan
  • D) China controlled Japan's economy
Answer: B). 该句将日本 “isolated” 的名声与其实际保持的活跃贸易关系进行对比。
Q9 · SAT Reading & Writing · Craft & Structure · Hard
“The scientist's findings were _____, contradicting decades of established wisdom.” Which word best fits?
  • A) conventional
  • B) corroborating
  • C) groundbreaking
  • D) derivative
Answer: C) groundbreaking. 空格处需表达 “与既有定论相悖” 之意，“groundbreaking”（创新的、革命性的）最贴合。
Q16 · SAT Reading & Writing · Standard English Conventions · Easy
Choose the correct word: “The students _____ happy with their results.”
  • A) is
  • B) are
  • C) was
  • D) being
Answer: B) are. 复数主语 “students” 需搭配复数谓语动词。
Q17 · SAT Reading & Writing · Standard English Conventions · Medium
Which sentence uses a semicolon correctly?
  • A) I went to the store; and bought milk.
  • B) I went to the store; I bought milk.
  • C) I went to the store; because I needed milk.
  • D) I went; to the store.
Answer: B). 分号用于连接两个独立分句。
Q18 · SAT Reading & Writing · Information & Ideas · Hard
Passage: “The author's use of first-person narration creates an intimacy that third-person could not achieve.” What is the author's purpose in using first-person?
  • A) To confuse readers
  • B) To create intimacy
  • C) To show omniscience
  • D) To distance the narrator
Answer: B) To create intimacy，正如文中所明示。

Group 5 · Math — Algebra (NEW) (8 questions)

Q21 · SAT Math · Algebra · Easy
If x + y = 7 and x − y = 1, what are the values of x and y?
  • A) x = 3, y = 4
  • B) x = 4, y = 3
  • C) x = 4, y = 5
  • D) x = 5, y = 2
Answer: B) x = 4, y = 3. 两式相加得 2x = 8，x = 4；代入得 y = 3.
Q22 · SAT Math · Algebra · Medium
What is the greatest integer that satisfies 3(x − 2) ≤ 2x + 1?
  • A) 5
  • B) 6
  • C) 7
  • D) 8
Answer: C) 7. 3x − 6 ≤ 2x + 1，x ≤ 7，最大整数为 7.
Q23 · SAT Math · Algebra · Medium
Solve |2x − 3| = 5.
  • A) x = 1 only
  • B) x = 4 only
  • C) x = 1 or x = 4
  • D) x = −1 or x = 4
Answer: D) x = −1 or x = 4. 由 2x − 3 = 5 得 x = 4；由 2x − 3 = −5 得 x = −1.
Q24 · SAT Math · Algebra · Easy
A car travels at 60 mph for 2.5 hours. How far does it travel?
  • A) 120 miles
  • B) 150 miles
  • C) 180 miles
  • D) 250 miles
Answer: B) 150 miles. distance = rate × time = 60 × 2.5 = 150.
Q25 · SAT Math · Algebra · Hard
Pipe A fills a tank in 4 hours; Pipe B fills the same tank in 6 hours. If both pipes work together, how many hours does it take to fill the tank?
  • A) 2.0
  • B) 2.4
  • C) 5.0
  • D) 10.0
Answer: B) 2.4. 合速 = 1/4 + 1/6 = 5/12 per hour，1 ÷ (5/12) = 12/5 = 2.4 hours.
Q26 · SAT Math · Algebra · Medium
How many liters of a 20% acid solution must be added to 10 liters of a 50% acid solution to produce a 30% acid solution?
  • A) 10 L
  • B) 15 L
  • C) 20 L
  • D) 25 L
Answer: C) 20 L. 设加 x L：(0.20x + 0.50·10) / (x + 10) = 0.30，0.20x + 5 = 0.30x + 3，0.10x = 2，x = 20.
Q27 · SAT Math · Algebra · Easy
Tom is 3 years older than his sister. The sum of their ages is 25. How old is Tom?
  • A) 11
  • B) 14
  • C) 22
  • D) 25
Answer: B) 14. 设妹妹 x 岁，Tom 为 x + 3；x + (x + 3) = 25，2x = 22，x = 11，Tom = 14.
Q28 · SAT Math · Algebra · Medium
A train travels 240 miles at a certain speed. On the return trip it goes 20 mph slower and takes 1 hour longer. What was the original speed?
  • A) 60 mph
  • B) 80 mph
  • C) 100 mph
  • D) 120 mph
Answer: B) 80 mph. 240/(r − 20) − 240/r = 1. 验证 r = 80：240/60 − 240/80 = 4 − 3 = 1 ✓.

Group 6 · Math — Advanced/Data/Geometry (NEW) (12 questions)

Q29 · SAT Math · Advanced Math · Medium
What is the vertex of the parabola y = x² − 4x + 1?
  • A) (2, −3)
  • B) (2, 3)
  • C) (−2, −3)
  • D) (4, 1)
Answer: A) (2, −3). x = −b/2a = 4/2 = 2，y = 4 − 8 + 1 = −3.
Q30 · SAT Math · Advanced Math · Hard
If x = 2 is a root of x³ − 3x² + kx − 4 = 0, what is the value of k?
  • A) 2
  • B) 3
  • C) 4
  • D) 5
Answer: C) 4. 代入 x = 2：8 − 12 + 2k − 4 = 0，2k = 8，k = 4.
Q31 · SAT Math · Advanced Math · Medium
A bacteria population doubles every hour. If it starts at 100, what is the population after 5 hours?
  • A) 500
  • B) 1000
  • C) 1600
  • D) 3200
Answer: D) 3200. P = 100 × 2⁵ = 100 × 32 = 3200.
Q32 · SAT Math · Data Analysis · Easy
A bar chart shows quarterly sales: Q1 = 200, Q2 = 300, Q3 = 250, Q4 = 400. What is the total annual sales?
  • A) 950
  • B) 1000
  • C) 1150
  • D) 1200
Answer: C) 1150. 200 + 300 + 250 + 400 = 1150.
Q33 · SAT Math · Data Analysis · Medium
A scatterplot of two variables shows a strong negative linear correlation. Which best describes the relationship?
  • A) As x increases, y increases
  • B) As x increases, y decreases
  • C) There is no relationship
  • D) As x decreases, y stays constant
Answer: B) As x increases, y decreases. 负相关表示 x 增大时 y 减小.
Q34 · SAT Math · Data Analysis · Easy
A bag contains 3 red, 2 blue, and 5 green marbles. What is the probability of drawing a green marble?
  • A) 1/10
  • B) 1/2
  • C) 1/5
  • D) 3/10
Answer: B) 1/2. 5 / (3 + 2 + 5) = 5/10 = 1/2.
Q35 · SAT Math · Data Analysis · Hard
From a group of 8 people, how many distinct committees of 3 can be formed?
  • A) 24
  • B) 56
  • C) 84
  • D) 512
Answer: B) 56. C(8,3) = 8! / (3!·5!) = (8·7·6) / (3·2·1) = 56.
Q36 · SAT Math · Geometry · Medium
Two similar triangles have corresponding sides in the ratio 2:3. If the smaller triangle has area 12, what is the area of the larger triangle?
  • A) 18
  • B) 27
  • C) 36
  • D) 48
Answer: B) 27. 面积比为边长比的平方：(3/2)² = 9/4，12 × 9/4 = 27.
Q37 · SAT Math · Advanced Math · Hard
What is the radius of the circle whose equation is (x − 2)² + (y + 3)² = 49?
  • A) 7
  • B) 14
  • C) 49
  • D) √7
Answer: A) 7. r² = 49，r = 7. 圆心为 (2, −3).
Q38 · SAT Math · Geometry · Medium
In a right triangle with legs of length 3 and 4, what is sin(θ) where θ is the angle opposite the side of length 4?
  • A) 3/5
  • B) 4/5
  • C) 3/4
  • D) 5/4
Answer: B) 4/5. 斜边 = √(9 + 16) = 5，sin(θ) = 对边 / 斜边 = 4/5.
Q39 · SAT Math · Geometry · Easy
What is the volume of a cube with side length 4?
  • A) 16
  • B) 32
  • C) 64
  • D) 96
Answer: C) 64. V = s³ = 4³ = 64.
Q40 · SAT Math · Geometry · Medium
What is the surface area of a cube with side length 3?
  • A) 27
  • B) 36
  • C) 54
  • D) 81
Answer: C) 54. SA = 6s² = 6 × 9 = 54.

  • A) to swim

Q44 · SAT Reading & Writing · Craft & Structure · Hard

Answer: B) For instance. 后句为前述主张提供具体例证，而非因果或转折。
Q45 · SAT Reading & Writing · Information & Ideas · Medium

  • C) Bees use a dance to convey food location

Q46 · SAT Reading & Writing · Information & Ideas · Hard

  • D) Maria had lost her phone

Q47 · SAT Reading & Writing · Craft & Structure · Medium
“The senator's speech was _____; it managed to offend nearly every faction in the room.” Which word best fits the context?
  • A) unifying
  • B) bland
  • C) incendiary
  • D) brief
Answer: C) incendiary. 得罪所有派系暗示言辞 “煽动性”、“挑动性”。
Q48 · SAT Reading & Writing · Standard English Conventions · Easy
Which sentence is grammatically correct?
  • A) I went home, I ate dinner.
  • B) I went home I ate dinner.
  • C) I went home and ate dinner.
  • D) I went home. And ate dinner.
Answer: C) I went home and ate dinner. 用并列连词连接两个谓语，避免逗号拼接与句子片段。
Q49 · SAT Reading & Writing · Standard English Conventions · Easy
Which sentence uses the apostrophe correctly?
  • A) The dogs leash is red.
  • B) The dog's leash is red.
  • C) The dogs' leash is red.
  • D) The dogs leash's red.
Answer: B) The dog's leash is red. 单数所有格在词尾加 ‘s.
Q50 · SAT Reading & Writing · Standard English Conventions · Medium
Choose the correctly punctuated version:
  • A) After the long meeting we, went to lunch.
  • B) After the long meeting we went to lunch.
  • C) After the long meeting, we went to lunch.
  • D) After, the long meeting we went to lunch.
Answer: C). 引导性介词短语后需用逗号分隔主句。
