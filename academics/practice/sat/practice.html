Q1 · SAT Math · Algebra · Medium
If 3x + 7 = 22, what is the value of x?
  • A) 3
  • B) 5
  • C) 7
  • D) 15
Answer: B) 5. 3x = 22 − 7 = 15，x = 5.
Q2 · SAT Math · Advanced Math · Hard
If f(x) = x² − 4x + 3, for what values of x does f(x) = 0?
  • A) x = 1, x = 3
  • B) x = −1, x = −3
  • C) x = 1, x = 4
  • D) x = 3, x = 4
Answer: A) x = 1, x = 3. 因式分解：(x − 1)(x − 3) = 0，故 x = 1 或 x = 3.
Q3 · SAT Reading & Writing · Standard English Conventions
Choose the best version of the underlined portion: “The team of researchers, who had been studying the effects of climate change on coral reefs, _____ their findings in a leading journal.”
  • A) has published
  • B) have published
  • C) publishes
  • D) published
Answer: D) published. 句子描述一个已完成的过去动作，主语 “team”（单数）后接非限制性定语从句 “who had been studying”，主句谓语应使用一般过去时表示已完成的动作。
