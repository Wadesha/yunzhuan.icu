Section 1 · Social / Survival

🎧 Audio 1:

电话咨询健身房会员信息 · 约 7 分钟

【S1-Q1】 Form Completion

Customer wants to join the __________ membership level.

  • A) Basic
  • B) Standard
  • C) Premium
  • D) VIP
Answer: C) Premium
【S1-Q2】 Note Completion

The opening hours on weekends are from __________ to __________.

  • A) 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM
  • B) 7:00 AM - 10:00 PM
  • C) 9:00 AM - 9:00 PM
  • D) 6:00 AM - 6:00 PM
Answer: A) 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM
【S1-Q3】 Map Labeling

The swimming pool is located next to the __________.

  • A) Reception desk
  • B) Gym equipment area
  • C) Changing rooms
  • D) Juice bar
Answer: B) Gym equipment area

Section 2 · Topic / Talk

🎧 Audio 2:

市政府介绍公共交通改善计划 · 约 8 分钟

【S2-Q1】 Multiple Choice

What is the main purpose of the new transport plan?

  • A) To reduce traffic congestion in the city center.
  • B) To build new roads connecting suburbs.
  • C) To promote electric vehicles.
  • D) To replace all buses with trains.
Answer: A) The plan aims to reduce city center congestion.
【S2-Q2】 Matching

Match the improvements to their scheduled completion dates:

ImprovementCompletion Date
Bus lane expansion__________
New bike paths__________
Train station renovation__________
  • A) End of this year
  • B) Mid next year
  • C) End of next year
Answer: Bus - B, Bike - A, Train - C

Section 3 · Academic Discussion

🎧 Audio 3:

学生讨论城市农业研究项目 · 约 7 分钟

【S3-Q1】 Multiple Choice

What data gathering method did the students decide to use?

  • A) Online surveys
  • B) Face-to-face interviews with urban farmers
  • C) Government statistics analysis
  • D) Laboratory experiments
Answer: B. They chose interviews with urban farmers.
【S3-Q2】 Note Completion

The project deadline is __________, and the word count for the report is __________.

  • A) October 15 / 3,000 words
  • B) November 20 / 5,000 words
  • C) December 1 / 4,000 words
  • D) September 30 / 2,500 words
Answer: C) December 1 / 4,000 words

Section 4 · Academic Lecture

🎧 Audio 4:

教授讲解英语语言发展史 · 约 10 分钟

【S4-Q1】 Summary Completion

Fill in the blanks:

The history of English is divided into three main periods: Old English, Middle English, and Modern English. Old English was heavily influenced by __________ languages. The Norman Conquest of 1066 brought __________ influences into Middle English. Modern English began around the __________ century.

Answer: Germanic, French, 15th
【S4-Q2】 Multiple Choice

According to the lecture, what was one effect of the printing press on English?

  • A) It caused English to become less standardized.
  • B) It helped standardize spelling and grammar.
  • C) It replaced Latin as the official language.
  • D) It introduced thousands of new scientific words.
Answer: B. The printing press helped standardize English.
