Group 1 · Reading

Q1 · IELTS Reading · True/False/Not Given · Easy
Passage: 'The honeybee dance language was first decoded by Karl von Frisch in the 1940s. He discovered that bees communicate the location of food sources through a waggle dance.' Statement: 'Karl von Frisch won a Nobel Prize for discovering the honeybee dance language.'
  • A) True
  • B) False
  • C) Not Given
答案：C) Not Given. The passage mentions von Frisch decoded the dance language, but does not mention a Nobel Prize.
Q2 · IELTS Reading · Matching Headings · Medium
Passage excerpt: 'Recent studies have shown that urban green spaces can significantly reduce city temperatures by up to 3°C through shade and evapotranspiration.' Which heading best fits this paragraph?
  • A) The History of Urban Planning
  • B) Cooling Effects of Urban Greenery
  • C) The Problem of Global Warming
  • D) Types of City Parks
答案：B). The paragraph discusses how green spaces reduce urban temperatures, which matches 'Cooling Effects of Urban Greenery.'

Q5 · IELTS Reading · Multiple Choice · Medium
Passage: 'While electric vehicles produce zero emissions during operation, the manufacturing process—particularly battery production—generates significant carbon footprint.' According to the passage:
  • A) Electric vehicles are completely emission-free
  • B) Electric vehicles have no environmental impact
  • C) Electric vehicle production has environmental costs
  • D) Electric vehicles are worse than petrol cars
答案：C). The passage acknowledges zero operational emissions but highlights manufacturing impact.
Q10 · IELTS Reading · Summary Completion · Hard
Passage: 'The invention of the printing press by Johannes Gutenberg in the 15th century revolutionized information dissemination, making books accessible to a broader audience and accelerating the spread of literacy.' Complete: Gutenberg's printing press made books more _____ and increased _____.
  • A) expensive / illiteracy
  • B) accessible / literacy
  • C) rare / knowledge
  • D) popular / wealth
答案：B) accessible / literacy. "Making books accessible" and "accelerating the spread of literacy" are directly from the passage.

Q12 · IELTS Reading · Matching Information · Hard

Q23 · IELTS Reading · Classification · Hard
Passage describes three types of volcanoes: shield, composite, and cinder cone. Classify each characteristic: (1) Gentle slopes, effusive eruptions; (2) Steep slopes, explosive eruptions; (3) Small, short-lived, built from ash fragments.
  • A) (1) Shield, (2) Composite, (3) Cinder cone
  • B) (1) Composite, (2) Shield, (3) Cinder cone
  • C) (1) Cinder cone, (2) Shield, (3) Composite
  • D) (1) Shield, (2) Cinder cone, (3) Composite
答案：A). Shield volcanoes have gentle slopes from flowing lava; composite volcanoes are steep and explosive; cinder cones are small and built from ash.
Q24 · IELTS Reading · Flow-chart Completion · Hard
Passage: 'Sewage treatment begins with screening to remove large objects. Next, primary treatment allows solids to settle in tanks. Secondary treatment uses bacteria to break down organic matter. Finally, the treated water is disinfected before release.' Complete the flow-chart: Stage 1: _____ → Stage 2: _____ → Stage 3: _____ → Stage 4: Disinfection
  • A) Screening → Primary treatment → Secondary treatment
  • B) Primary treatment → Screening → Secondary treatment
  • C) Screening → Secondary treatment → Primary treatment
  • D) Secondary treatment → Screening → Primary treatment
答案：A). The sequence follows the passage: screening first, then primary (settling), then secondary (bacteria), then disinfection.

Q30 · IELTS Reading · Summary with Box · Hard
Passage about the Industrial Revolution. Complete the summary using words from the box: [urban, rural, steam, manual, increased, decreased]. 'The Industrial Revolution saw a shift from _____ labour to machine-based production. The _____ engine was a key innovation. Populations moved from _____ areas to cities, and factory output _____ dramatically.'
  • A) manual / steam / rural / increased
  • B) rural / steam / manual / increased
  • C) manual / steam / urban / decreased
  • D) rural / manual / urban / increased
答案：A). The shift was from manual to machine labor; the steam engine was key; people moved from rural to urban areas; output increased.
Q31 · IELTS Reading · Sentence Completion with Box · Hard
Passage about climate change. Complete each sentence with the correct ending from the box: [A-F]. (1) Rising sea levels threaten _____. (2) Coral bleaching is caused by _____. (3) Glacier retreat indicates _____. Endings: A) coastal cities, B) ocean acidification, C) warming temperatures, D) increased rainfall, E) global warming, F) deforestation.
  • A) (1) A, (2) C, (3) E
  • B) (1) C, (2) A, (3) E
  • C) (1) A, (2) E, (3) C
  • D) (1) E, (2) C, (3) A
答案：A). Rising seas threaten coastal cities (A); coral bleaching is caused by warming temperatures (C); glacier retreat indicates global warming (E).

Group 3 · Writing

Q8 · IELTS Writing · Task 1 (Graph Description) · Medium
The bar chart shows the percentage of people using public transport in five cities (London, Tokyo, New York, Paris, Sydney) in 2010 and 2020. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features. Write at least 150 words.
写作提示：Structure: Intro (paraphrase prompt) → Overview (2-3 key trends) → Body 1 (2010 data) → Body 2 (2020 data + changes). Use: "The bar chart illustrates...", "Overall, ...", "In 2010, ...", "By 2020, ...". Don't give opinions.
Q3 · IELTS Writing · Task 2 (Practice Prompt) · Medium
Some people believe that universities should focus on providing academic skills, while others think they should prepare students for their future careers. Discuss both views and give your opinion. Write at least 250 words.
写作提示：Structure: Intro (paraphrase + thesis) → Body 1 (academic skills view) → Body 2 (career prep view) → Body 3 (your opinion) → Conclusion. Aim for 270-300 words. Use linking words (Furthermore, However, Nevertheless).
Q16 · IELTS Writing · Task 1 · Process Diagram · Medium
The diagram shows the process of photosynthesis in 6 steps. Summarise the process. Write at least 150 words.
写作提示：Use sequencing language: "First," "Next," "Then," "After that," "Finally." Describe each step in order. Don't give opinions.
Q17 · IELTS Writing · Task 2 · Hard
Some people think that social media has a positive effect on society, while others disagree. Discuss both views and give your opinion. Write at least 250 words.
写作提示：Structure: Intro (paraphrase + thesis) → Body 1 (positive view + example) → Body 2 (negative view + example) → Body 3 (your opinion) → Conclusion. Aim for 280+ words.
Q41 · IELTS Writing · Task 1 Line Graph · Medium
The line graph shows the changes in the average monthly temperature in three cities (Sydney, London, New York) over the course of a year. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.
写作提示：Structure: Intro (paraphrase) → Overview (overall trends: peaks, troughs, ranges) → Body 1 (one or two cities) → Body 2 (remaining city/cities, comparisons). Vocabulary: "peaked at," "reached a low of," "remained stable," "in contrast." No opinions.
Q42 · IELTS Writing · Task 1 Pie Chart · Medium
The pie charts show the main reasons people chose to migrate to Country X in 1990 and 2020. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.
写作提示：Structure: Intro (paraphrase) → Overview (most significant change between the two years) → Body 1 (1990 breakdown) → Body 2 (2020 breakdown + key shifts). Vocabulary: "accounted for," "made up," "a small proportion," "the largest share." Compare, don't just list.
Q43 · IELTS Writing · Task 1 Table · Hard
The table below shows the average salaries (in US dollars) of five professions in three countries (USA, UK, Japan) in 2010 and 2020. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant. Write at least 150 words.
写作提示：Structure: Intro (paraphrase) → Overview (highest/lowest earners, biggest changes) → Body 1 (cross-country comparison for one or two professions) → Body 2 (changes over time). Vocabulary: "the highest figure," "compared to," "increased by," "remained unchanged." Group data; don't list every cell.
Q44 · IELTS Writing · Task 2 Opinion Essay · Hard
Some people believe that governments should invest more money in public transportation, while others think funding should go to road infrastructure. Discuss both views and give your opinion. Write at least 250 words.
写作提示：Structure: Intro (paraphrase + clear opinion) → Body 1 (public transport argument: environment, equity) → Body 2 (road infrastructure argument: convenience, economy) → Body 3 (your opinion with reasoning) → Conclusion. Aim for 280+ words. Use topic sentences for each paragraph.
