IELTS Syllabus v1

Listening + Reading + Writing + Speaking · 0-9.0 · Academic Module

考试结构

Section 题型 题数 时长 分值
Listening 4 sections (conversation, monologue, academic discussion, lecture) 40q 30min 0-9.0
Reading (Academic) 3 passages (descriptive, argumentative, analytical) 40q 60min 0-9.0
Writing (Academic) Task 1 (describe graph/chart, 150+ words, 20min) + Task 2 (essay, 250+ words, 40min) 2 60min 0-9.0
Speaking Part 1 (intro, 4-5min) + Part 2 (cue card, 3-4min) + Part 3 (discussion, 4-5min) 3 parts 11-14min 0-9.0
Total — — ~2h45m 0-9.0 (overall = average rounded to 0.5)
