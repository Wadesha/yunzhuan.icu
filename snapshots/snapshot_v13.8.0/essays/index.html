Personal Statement · Common App · Supplemental · UCAS

This section is a navigation hub for undergraduate admission essays used across major overseas application systems. It covers the general Personal Statement framework, the Common App main essay (7 prompts, 650 words), school-specific Supplemental Essays (Why School / Why Major / Activity / Community / Diversity), and the UCAS Personal Statement for UK undergraduate applications (4000 characters, 47 lines). Each page provides a structural framework, writing process, common pitfalls and revision checklists rather than full sample essays.
