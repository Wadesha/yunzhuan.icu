IGCSE Chemistry

IGCSE 化学

CAIE 0620 / 0971

Course Overview 课程介绍

IGCSE 化学 14-16 岁，分 Core/Extended，A-Level 化学、IB Chemistry 与医学化工衔接课程。

IGCSE Chemistry is the international GCSE Chemistry course for students aged 14-16, serving as the standard preparation for A-Level Chemistry, IB Chemistry and science-oriented university programmes. It covers states of matter, atomic structure, stoichiometry, electrochemistry, chemical energetics, reactions, acids/bases/salts, the Periodic Table, metals, environmental chemistry and organic chemistry, with emphasis on practical work, equation writing and mole calculations.

Syllabus 章节大纲

  • Core1. States of matter
    Solid, liquid, gas particle model / diffusion / changes of state / pure substances and mixtures
  • Core2. Atoms, elements and compounds
    Atomic structure / isotopes / ionic and covalent bonding / formulae and equations
  • Ext3. Stoichiometry
    Mole / molar concentration / limiting reactants / yield and purity calculations (Extended focus)
  • Ext4. Electrochemistry
    Electrolysis of molten and aqueous substances / predicting electrolysis products / applications (electroplating, aluminium extraction)
  • Core5. Chemical energetics
    Exothermic and endothermic reactions / bond energies / combustion / simple energy diagrams
  • Ext6. Chemical reactions
    Reaction rate / factors affecting rate / reversible reactions and equilibrium / catalysts / industrial processes (Haber, Contact)
  • Core7. Acids, bases and salts
    Strong and weak acids/bases / pH / neutralisation / preparation and naming of salts / indicators
  • Core8. The Periodic Table
    Periodic law / Groups I, II, VII, VIII / transition metals / period and group trends
  • Core9. Metals
    Metal reactivity / metal extraction / alloys / corrosion and protection
  • Ext10. Chemistry of the environment
    Water purification / air and pollution / greenhouse effect / acid rain / global carbon cycle (Extended)
  • Ext11. Organic chemistry
    Alkanes and alkenes / alcohols / carboxylic acids / esterification / polymers / addition and condensation polymerisation

Core vs Extended Core 与 Extended 区分

Core 不含摩尔定量、电解、有机化学；Extended 增加全部，上限 A*/9。

CoreCore covers fundamental concepts: states of matter, atomic structure, chemical bonding, energy changes, acids/bases/salts, the Periodic Table and metals. Core does not require complex mole calculations, quantitative electrolysis analysis, equilibrium shifts, or organic chemistry nomenclature, with a maximum grade of C.

ExtExtended builds on Core by adding full stoichiometry (mole calculations and limiting reactants), quantitative electrochemistry, reaction rates and quantitative equilibrium, environmental chemistry (water and air pollution), and organic chemistry (alkanes/alkenes, alcohols, carboxylic acids, esterification, polymers). Extended caps at A* / 9 and is the recommended preparation for A-Level Chemistry.

Note: Core candidates sit Paper 1+3+(5 or 6); Extended candidates sit Paper 2+4+(5 or 6). Paper 5 is a practical exam; Paper 6 is a written alternative.

Pathway 适用衔接

Extended 推荐衔接 A-Level 9701、IB Chemistry，医学化工药学建议 A*/9。

IGCSE Chemistry (Extended recommended) is the standard preparation for A-Level Chemistry (CAIE 9701), IB Chemistry HL/SL and medical / chemical / materials / biological science pathways. After completing IGCSE Chemistry, students can progress directly to A-Level AS; those with a solid foundation in bonding, mole calculations, acids/bases/salts and organic chemistry transition more smoothly to A-Level physical chemistry, organic mechanisms and quantitative equilibrium analysis. For students applying to medicine, chemical engineering or pharmacy, an A* or 9 in IGCSE Chemistry is recommended.
