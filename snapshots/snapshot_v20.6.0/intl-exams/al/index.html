Advanced Level · UK Secondary Curriculum

A-Level (Advanced Level) is a UK advanced secondary qualification taken over two years (AS + A2), widely recognized by universities worldwide. Students typically choose 3-4 subjects. Below are the four main exam boards offering A-Level qualifications. A-Level 是英国高中高级课程，2 年制（AS + A2），全球大学广泛认可。
CAIE Cambridge Assessment International Education International version, most commonly used in domestic international departments, pure written exam papers. Edexcel IAL Pearson Edexcel International A-Level International version, modular unit-by-unit exams, allows resitting a single module. AQA Assessment and Qualifications Alliance Mainly used in the UK domestic market, the international version is less common. OCR Oxford, Cambridge and RSA Examinations Used in the UK domestic market, rarely seen in international departments.
