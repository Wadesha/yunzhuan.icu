A-Level 数学

CAIE 9709

Course Overview 课程介绍

CAIE 高级数学课程，分 AS 与 A2，理工科与经济专业核心基础。

A-Level Mathematics is a UK advanced secondary course split into AS (Year 1) and A2 (Year 2). It covers pure mathematics, mechanics, and probability & statistics, with strong emphasis on algebraic technique, function modelling and problem solving. A core subject for mathematics, engineering, physics, computer science, economics, finance and statistics applicants.

AS Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • AS1. Quadratics
    Quadratic functions, discriminant, completing the square, quadratic formula, inequalities
  • AS2. Functions
    Functions, domain and range, composite functions, inverse functions, graph transformations
  • AS3. Coordinate Geometry
    Coordinate geometry, equation of a line, equation of a circle, distance and midpoint
  • AS4. Circular Measure
    Circular measure, radian measure, arc length and sector area, radian-degree conversion
  • AS5. Trigonometry
    Trigonometry, trigonometric identities, trigonometric equations, sine and cosine rules
  • AS6. Vectors
    Vectors, 2D vectors, addition/subtraction and scalar multiplication, magnitude and unit vectors, dot product
  • AS7. Series
    Series, arithmetic and geometric progressions, summation formulae, binomial expansion
  • AS8. Differentiation
    Differentiation, derivative definition, power rule, tangents and normals, extrema and monotonicity
  • AS9. Integration
    Integration, indefinite integrals, definite integrals, area under curve, volume of revolution
  • AS10. Forces and Equilibrium
    Mechanics: forces and equilibrium, composition and resolution of forces, equilibrium conditions, friction
  • AS11. Kinematics of Motion
    Mechanics: kinematics, displacement/velocity/acceleration, equations of uniform acceleration, calculus applications
  • AS12. Representation of Data and Probability
    Statistics: data representation and probability, discrete and continuous data, permutations and combinations, probability fundamentals
  • AS13. Discrete Random Variables
    Statistics: discrete random variables, probability distributions, expectation and variance, binomial distribution
  • AS14. Normal Distribution
    Statistics: normal distribution, standardisation, probability from tables, inverse look-up

A2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A215. Algebra
    Algebra (advanced), modulus and inequalities, partial fractions, higher-degree equations
  • A216. Logarithmic and Exponential Functions
    Logarithmic and exponential functions, natural logarithm, change of base, exponential equations
  • A217. Trigonometry (Advanced)
    Trigonometry (advanced), compound angle formulae, double and half angles, inverse trig functions, hyperbolic functions introduction
  • A218. Differentiation (Advanced)
    Differentiation (advanced), chain rule, implicit and parametric differentiation, chain rule applications
  • A219. Integration (Advanced)
    Integration (advanced), substitution, integration by parts, partial fraction integration, numerical integration
  • A220. Numerical Solutions of Equations
    Numerical solutions of equations, bisection method, Newton-Raphson method, iterative convergence
  • A221. Vectors (Advanced)
    Vectors (advanced), 3D vectors, cross product, equations of lines and planes
  • A222. Differential Equations
    Differential equations, separable variables, first-order linear differential equations, modelling applications
  • A223. Complex Numbers
    Complex numbers, imaginary unit, modulus and argument, polar form, de Moivre's theorem
  • A224. Forces and Equilibrium in 2D
    Mechanics (advanced): forces and equilibrium in 2D, Newton's laws, connected particles, inclined planes
  • A225. Motion in 2D and Projectiles
    Mechanics (advanced): motion in 2D, projectile motion, relative velocity, work and energy
  • A226. Continuous Random Variables
    Statistics (advanced): continuous random variables, probability density functions, cumulative distributions
  • A227. Hypothesis Testing
    Statistics (advanced): hypothesis testing, significance level, one- and two-tailed tests, sampling distributions

Suitable Majors 适用专业与衔接

数学、工程、经济、金融等专业基础科目，英国理工科普遍必修。

A-Level Mathematics is foundational for mathematics, engineering, physics, computer science, economics, finance, statistics, actuarial science, data science and operations research. Most UK STEM and economics programmes require A-Level Mathematics as mandatory. It provides essential prerequisite knowledge for university calculus, linear algebra and probability courses.
