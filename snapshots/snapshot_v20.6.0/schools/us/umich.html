Academic Profile 学术概况

公立旗舰大学，Ross 商学院和工程学院全美顶尖，校队和校园氛围活跃。

University of Michigan offers a 4-year undergraduate curriculum across 19 schools and colleges. The Ross School of Business and College of Engineering are both top-ranked. With over 280 degree programs, Michigan provides exceptional academic breadth and depth. The semester system includes a spring/summer term option.

Notes for Applicants 申请要点

  • EA is non-binding: you can apply to other schools early.
  • Ross School has direct admission from high school or cross-campus transfer.
  • Michigan is known for strong school spirit and Big Ten athletics.
  • Winter in Ann Arbor can be very cold (0°F / -18°C not unusual).
  • Limited merit-based aid available for international top applicants.
