About the University 学校简介

Hong Kongs oldest and most prestigious university, with a strong international reputation. Known for its law, medicine, business, engineering, and dentistry programs. English instruction.

Application Notes 申请要点

  • Interviews for shortlisted applicants (especially medicine, law, etc.)
  • Very competitive - top tier programs accept 5% or fewer applicants
  • IB / A-Level / AP / SAT all accepted

Student Life 校园生活

Located on Hong Kong Island, near the city center. English-language education with international student body. Hall culture is a big part of student life.

Weather 气候环境

Subtropical climate. Hot and humid summers (28-33°C). Mild, pleasant winters (15-20°C). Typhoon season June-October.
