A-Level Business (OCR)

A-Level 商务（OCR）

OCR H031 (AS) / H431 (A-Level)

Course Overview 课程介绍

OCR A-Level 商务，线性结构，覆盖营销、财务、人力与运营，强调案例分析与决策。

OCR A-Level Business (H431) is a linear two-year course assessed by three written papers at the end of Year 13. OCR A-Level has moved to a linear structure since 2015, so all exams are taken at the end of the course. The specification is built around the functional areas of business — marketing, finance, human resources and operations — set within the wider external environment, and develops strategic thinking, decision making and the application of business theory to real case studies. A useful subject for business, management, accounting, marketing and entrepreneurship applicants.

Key Facts 基本信息

AS / Year 1 Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • AS1. Locating Functions and Business in the Wider Environment
    Business objectives, legal structures, external environment and the role of business in society
  • AS2. Marketing
    Market research, segmentation, the marketing mix (4Ps), product life cycle, pricing strategies, promotion and distribution
  • AS3. Finance and Accounts
    Sources of finance, cash flow forecasting, costs, revenue and profit, break-even analysis, financial statements and ratios
  • AS4. People in Organisations
    Motivation theories, leadership styles, recruitment and selection, training and development, organisational structures
  • AS5. Operations Management
    Production, productivity, quality, stock control, economies of scale, lean production, technology in operations

A2 / Year 2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A26. Strategic Analysis and Choice
    SWOT, PESTLE, critical path analysis, decision trees, investment appraisal (NPV, ARR, payback), strategic choice
  • A27. Strategic Implementation and Change
    Corporate and functional strategy, organisational culture, change management, leadership during change
  • A28. Marketing Strategy (Advanced)
    Ansoff matrix, Porter's generic strategies, marketing strategy in different markets, e-commerce and digital marketing
  • A29. Financial Strategy (Advanced)
    Ratio analysis (advanced), gearing, capital structure, dividend policy, financial strategy and corporate restructuring
  • A210. Managing Strategic Change and Globalisation
    International trade, multinational corporations, globalisation, ethics and corporate social responsibility, managing change

Suitable Majors & Pathway 适用专业与衔接

商业、管理、市场营销与会计专业的实用科目，强调案例分析与战略决策。

A-Level Business is a useful subject for business administration, management, accounting, finance, marketing, international business and entrepreneurship. Many UK business and management programmes welcome A-Level Business, though it is sometimes not accepted alongside Economics at top-tier economics degrees. The course develops commercial awareness, case study analysis, numerical reasoning and strategic thinking, and pairs naturally with A-Level Economics, Mathematics or Accounting for applicants to competitive business and finance programmes.
