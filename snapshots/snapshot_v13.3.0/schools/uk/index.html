UCAS · 3-year bachelor 英国大学

Undergraduate admission in the UK is centralized through UCAS, allowing each applicant to choose up to 5 programs. Offers are mostly conditional on A-Level (or IB / equivalent) results, and bachelor programs last 3 years in England (4 in Scotland). The Russell Group represents 24 leading research-intensive universities.
