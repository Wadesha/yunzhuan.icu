A-Level Mathematics (Edexcel IAL)

A-Level 数学（Edexcel IAL）

Edexcel IAL WMA11-14

Course Overview 课程介绍

Edexcel IAL 数学采用模块化按 Unit 考试，纯数 P1-P4 加应用单元，理工科与经济商科核心基础。

Edexcel International A-Level Mathematics is a modular course combining four Pure Mathematics units (P1-P4) with applied units in Mechanics (M1/M2), Statistics (S1/S2) and Decision Mathematics (D1). Students sit each unit exam separately and may retake individual units. Content covers algebra, functions, calculus, vectors, mechanics and probability, with strong emphasis on modelling and problem solving. A core subject for mathematics, engineering, physics, computer science, economics and finance.

Key Facts 基本信息

Grading (评分)A* - E for full A-Level; a - e for AS

AS Syllabus AS 阶段章节（P1-P2）

  • P1Algebra and Equations
    Indices, surds, quadratics, simultaneous equations, inequalities, algebraic manipulation
  • P1Coordinate Geometry and Vectors
    Equation of a straight line, circles, coordinate geometry in two dimensions, 2D vectors
  • P1Trigonometry
    Trigonometric ratios, identities, equations, sine and cosine rules, radians
  • P1Differentiation and Integration
    Differentiation from first principles, tangents and normals, stationary points, integration, area under a curve
  • P1Exponentials and Logarithms
    Exponential functions, logarithms, natural logarithm, modelling growth and decay
  • P2Functions and Graphs
    Factor and remainder theorem, modulus functions, composite and inverse functions, graph transformations
  • P2Sequences and Series
    Arithmetic and geometric sequences, sigma notation, binomial expansion
  • P2Further Calculus and Trigonometry
    Chain, product and quotient rules, integration by substitution, trapezium rule, compound angle formulae

A2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节（P3-P4）

  • P3Algebraic Fractions and Functions
    Algebraic fractions, partial fractions, composite, inverse and modulus functions
  • P3Sequences and Series
    Recurrence relations, sigma notation, binomial series for rational powers
  • P3Trigonometry (Advanced)
    Compound and double angle formulae, R formulae, trigonometric identities and equations
  • P3Further Differentiation and Integration
    Implicit and parametric differentiation, integration by parts and substitution, volumes of revolution
  • P3Numerical Methods and Vectors
    Iteration, Newton-Raphson method, numerical integration, three-dimensional vectors
  • P4Parametric Equations and Proof
    Parametric equations, curves and their cartesian forms, proof by deduction, contradiction and exhaustion
  • P4Differential Equations and Vectors
    First order differential equations, modelling with differential equations, 3D vector lines and planes

Suitable Majors & Pathway 适合专业与升学路径

适合数学、工程、物理、计算机、经济与金融专业，是英国理工与商科专业录取的核心基础科目。

Mathematics is required for mathematics, engineering, physics, computer science, economics, finance and statistics programmes, and strongly preferred for business and natural science degrees. Paired with Further Mathematics it opens doors to top STEM universities. The modular Edexcel IAL structure lets students resit individual Pure or Applied units to maximise their final A-Level grade.
