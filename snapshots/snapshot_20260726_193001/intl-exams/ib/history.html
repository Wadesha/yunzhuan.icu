IB History

IB 历史

IB Diploma · History HL/SL

Course Overview 课程介绍

IB 历史分 HL/SL，含 Paper 1 移动研究、Paper 2 主题、Paper 3 区域（HL）、IA 调查。

IB History is a two-year secondary history course offered by IBO, available at both Higher Level (HL) and Standard Level (SL). The course is structured around a prescribed subject (Paper 1), 20th century world history topics (Paper 2), a regional depth study (Paper 3, HL only), and a historical investigation (IA). It emphasizes primary source analysis, historical argumentation, and multi-perspective interpretation. SL and HL share Papers 1 and 2; HL adds Paper 3 (regional essays). A key subject for history, politics, international relations, law, and journalism applicants.

SL Syllabus SL 阶段章节

  • SL1. Paper 1 prescribed subject (1 of 5)
    Military leaders / conquest and its impact / the move to global war / rights and protest / conflict and intervention (source-based analysis)
  • SL2. Paper 2 20th century world history topics (2 of 4)
    Authoritarian and single-party states / democratic states / the Cold War / multipolarism and power shifts / conflict and intervention
  • SL3. Source analysis skills
    Primary source analysis / origin, value and limitations / OPVL (Origin, Purpose, Value, Limitation) method
  • SL4. Essay writing skills
    Historical argumentative writing / argument construction / evidence support / counter-arguments / synthesis
  • SL5. Historical investigation IA preparation
    Historical investigation / self-chosen research question / primary and secondary sources / 1,500-2,000 word investigation report

HL Additional Topics HL 额外章节

  • HL6. Paper 3 regional depth study (1 of 4)
    Regional depth study / history of Europe / history of Asia and Oceania / history of the Americas / history of Africa and the Middle East (choose 1 region, 3 themes)
  • HL7. Regional topic (Europe)
    European region example: French Revolution / Napoleon / Europe before WWI / interwar Europe / postwar reconstruction
  • HL8. Regional topic (Asia)
    Asian region example: late Qing to Republic / Japanese Meiji Restoration and expansion / Indian independence / Southeast Asian decolonization
  • HL9. Regional topic (Americas)
    Americas region example: American independence and Civil War / Latin American independence / US foreign policy / Canadian history
  • HL10. Regional topic (Africa and the Middle East)
    Africa and Middle East example: colonization and decolonization / South African apartheid / Arab-Israeli conflict / Middle Eastern independence movements
  • HL11. Extended essay writing
    HL Paper 3 requires three long essays (1 of 3 each), emphasizing in-depth argumentation and cross-theme synthesis

Internal Assessment 内部评估 IA

IA 是独立历史调查，占 SL 25% / HL 20%，撰写 1,500-2,000 字报告。

The IB History IA is an individual historical investigation worth 25% (SL) / 20% (HL) of the final grade. Students choose a historical research question, use primary and secondary sources, and produce a 1,500-2,000 word report covering plan, investigation, and reflection. Assessment criteria: research question and plan, investigation and evidence, critical reflection, source analysis, organization and presentation.
