Course Overview 课程介绍

CAIE 高级生物课程，分 AS 与 A2，医学/生命科学申请核心科目。

A-Level Biology is a UK advanced secondary course split into AS (Year 1) and A2 (Year 2). It spans molecules, cells, organisms and ecosystems, covering biological molecules, cell physiology, genetics, evolution, ecology and biotechnology, with strong emphasis on experimental design and data analysis. A key subject for medicine, biology, biomedical science, pharmacy, veterinary science and life sciences.

AS Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • AS1. Cell Structure
    Cell structure, microscopy, prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells, organelles, virus structure
  • AS2. Biological Molecules
    Biological molecules, water, carbohydrates, lipids, proteins, nucleic acids structure and function
  • AS3. Enzymes
    Enzymes, mechanism of enzyme action, factors affecting enzyme activity, Michaelis-Menten equation, inhibitors
  • AS4. Cell Membranes and Transport
    Cell membranes and transport, fluid mosaic model, diffusion/osmosis/active transport, endocytosis and exocytosis
  • AS5. The Mitotic Cell Cycle
    Mitotic cell cycle, chromosomes, stages of cell cycle, cancer
  • AS6. Nucleic Acids and Protein Synthesis
    Nucleic acids and protein synthesis, DNA replication, transcription and translation, genetic code
  • AS7. Transport in Plants
    Transport in plants, xylem and phloem, transpiration, membrane transport mechanisms
  • AS8. Transport in Mammals
    Transport in mammals, blood composition, blood vessel structure, heart structure and circulation, haemoglobin
  • AS9. Gas Exchange and Smoking
    Gas exchange and smoking, mammalian lung structure, ventilation mechanism, effects of smoking
  • AS10. Infectious Diseases
    Infectious diseases, pathogens, malaria/TB/HIV, antibiotics and resistance
  • AS11. Immunity
    Immunity, specific and non-specific immunity, antibodies, B cells and T cells, vaccines

A2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A212. Energy and Respiration
    Energy and respiration, glycolysis, Krebs cycle, electron transport chain, ATP synthesis
  • A213. Photosynthesis
    Photosynthesis, light-dependent reactions, light-independent reactions (Calvin cycle), limiting factors
  • A214. Homeostasis
    Homeostasis, negative feedback, blood glucose regulation, osmoregulation, excretion
  • A215. Control and Coordination
    Control and coordination, neurons and synapses, hormones, action potentials, sense organs
  • A216. Inherited Change
    Inherited change, meiosis, chromosome mutations, epigenetics, sex determination
  • A217. Selection and Evolution
    Selection and evolution, natural selection, Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium, isolation and speciation
  • A218. Biodiversity, Classification and Conservation
    Biodiversity, classification and conservation, species diversity, classification systems, conservation strategies
  • A219. Genetic Technology
    Genetic technology, recombinant DNA, PCR, gel electrophoresis, GMOs, ethics
  • A220. Control of Gene Expression
    Control of gene expression, transcription factors, epigenetic modifications, small RNAs
  • A221. Recombinant DNA Technology
    Recombinant DNA technology, vectors and plasmids, library construction, gene therapy

Suitable Majors 适用专业与衔接

医学、生物、药学、兽医等专业重要基础，多数英国医学院必修。

A-Level Biology is a key subject for medicine, biomedical science, biological sciences, pharmacy, dentistry, veterinary science, nursing, neuroscience, ecology, life sciences and sports science. Most UK medical schools require or strongly recommend Biology as one of the subjects. It provides a solid foundation for university cell biology, genetics, physiology and biochemistry.
