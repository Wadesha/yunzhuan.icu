Canadian Universities

Provincial application · 4-year bachelor 加拿大大学

Canadian universities apply through provincial systems such as Ontario's OUAC; other provinces apply directly. A-Level, IB and AP results are all widely accepted, and bachelor programs run 4 years. Tuition for international students is generally lower than in the US and the UK, and post-study work permits are favorable.
加拿大大学 - 各省独立申请（如 OUAC），A-Level/IB/AP 均可，4 年制本科。
