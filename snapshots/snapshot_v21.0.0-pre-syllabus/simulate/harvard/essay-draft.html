Harvard — 文书初稿 + 导师批注

选 Common App Prompt 1：背景、身份、兴趣、天赋中"那个让申请非提不可"的故事

Prompt 1 — 模拟初稿 v1（by Li Ming）
It was 2 a.m. in a computer lab in Yunnan, and twenty middle-schoolers were staring at me like I was speaking another language. I was teaching them Python. Three years earlier, I had been one of them—a quiet kid in a city middle school who stumbled into coding through an online video and discovered that I could build things that worked. By 10th grade I had won USACO gold; by 11th I was on the IMO team. None of it had felt unfair. The competitions were hard, the teachers were good, the resources were abundant. The pattern of "effort → result" had always held. Then in the summer of 2024 I taught a five-day Python camp at a rural middle school in Yunnan. The students were eager, sharp, and curious—the same materials that had launched me bored them within an hour. They did not lack talent. They lacked access: to faster computers, to mentors, to a community that took them seriously. A girl named Mei asked me a question about loops that I had answered in my sleep a hundred times. I realized, embarrassed, that I had been assuming the entire world had the same starting line I did. That summer I went back twice. I rewrote the curriculum around their questions, not mine. I translated every example into problems from their village. I started a WeChat group that is still active. Two of the students later entered the provincial programming contest—their school's first entries in five years. Harvard will not give me a clearer mirror than what I saw in that lab. I want to study computer science not to build better algorithms but to build better access. The same gift I was given—someone who answered my boring questions without rolling their eyes—I want to give away at scale. Whether that means research on AI for low-resource languages, or a startup in ed-tech, or a career in policy, I want to spend my four years here learning how to make the starting line fairer than mine was. (Word count: 612 / 650)

导师批注（8 处）

批注 1（开头）："It was 2 a.m. in a computer lab in Yunnan..." 开篇场景感强，建议保留。但避免过度戏剧化，第二段可以更早进入"我学到了什么"。
批注 2（"a quiet kid...stumbled into coding"）：具体但有点 cliché。考虑用一两个数字代替形容词：例如"在 6 个月内完成了 4 门在线课程"。
批注 3（"USACO gold"）：此处过于密集，3 行里堆了 4 个奖项，招生官会审美疲劳。把 USACO 和 IMO 留到 Activity List 即可，文书里只留 1 个最强证据（推荐 USACO，因为 IMO 更适合在 Interview 里讲故事）。
批注 4（"the same materials that had launched me bored them"）：这是全文最有冲击力的句子，保留并扩展。考虑加一句"我花了一小时看他们的眼睛然后重写课程"具体化过程。
批注 5（Mei 名字）：使用真实学生姓名需要谨慎。如果 YC 实际不是这个名字，换成化名；如果是真名，注意是否征得同意。Harvard 招生官会注意到这种细节。
批注 6（"provincial programming contest"）：具体名称可以加分，例如"2024 Yunnan Provincial Informatics Olympiad"。把数字从"first entries in 5 years"改成可验证的来源。
批注 7（"I want to build better access"）：结尾立意好，但和 Common App Prompt 1（"the thing that is so meaningful"）的呼应要更紧。建议把"开始 WeChat 群"这句后接一句"我意识到，我之所以能改变这些孩子的生活，是因为我曾经被改变过"——这样能明确点题。
批注 8（最后一句）："a career in policy" 提得略突兀。考虑用 1 句展开"我希望研究 AI 在低资源语言上的应用"更聚焦 CS。
