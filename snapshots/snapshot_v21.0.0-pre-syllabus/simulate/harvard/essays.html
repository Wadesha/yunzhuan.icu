Harvard — 文书题目原文（2025-2026）

所有 Harvard 申请者必须提交 Common App 主文书 + 以下 Harvard 补充文书

Common App 主文书（7 选 1）

Prompt 1（官方原文）

Some students have a background, identity, interest, or talent that is so meaningful they believe their application would be incomplete without it. If this sounds like you, then please share your story.

字数：650 字以内
Prompt 5（官方原文）

Discuss an accomplishment, event, or realization that sparked a period of personal growth and a new understanding of yourself or others.

字数：650 字以内

完整 7 题见 commonapp.org。CS 方向建议选 Prompt 1 或 Prompt 5。

Harvard 补充文书（Harvard Supplement）

Harvard Short Essay 1

Harvard has long recognized the importance of enrolling a student body that is as diverse as the world around them. How will your life experiences, background, or perspective contribute to a campus community that values diversity, equity, and inclusion?

字数：200 字以内
Harvard Short Essay 2

Briefly describe an academic assignment (or similar experience) that sparked your intellectual curiosity and led you to want to learn more.

字数：150 字以内
Harvard Short Essay 3（必填）

List the books you have read during the past 12 months that have been particularly meaningful to you. You may also include a brief explanation of why these books were meaningful.

格式：自由列出（建议 5-10 本）
Harvard Short Essay 4（可选）

Harvard will accept applications for the Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) time zone. Please use this space to describe any additional information that you'd like the Admissions Committee to know.

字数：可选填，建议 100-300 字

Harvard 工学院附加文书（如果选 SEAS）

SEAS Supplement（Computer Science / Engineering 申请人）

If you intend to study engineering at Harvard, please describe how your academic interests, activities, and/or work experience have prepared you to thrive in the SEAS community.

字数：300 字以内
研究兴趣说明（可选）

Briefly describe a research project or engineering challenge you'd like to explore at Harvard.

字数：200 字以内 · 选 SEAS 强烈建议填
