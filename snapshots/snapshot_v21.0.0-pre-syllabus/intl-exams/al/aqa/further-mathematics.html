A-Level Further Mathematics (AQA)

A-Level 进阶数学（AQA）

AQA 7367 · OxfordAQA 9673

Course Overview 课程介绍

AQA 进阶数学，线性结构，数学、物理、CS 顶尖高校强烈推荐或必修。

A-Level Further Mathematics (AQA 7367) is a linear qualification extending well beyond A-Level Mathematics, examined by three terminal papers at the end of two years. Two papers cover compulsory Further Pure content (complex numbers, matrices, further algebra and series, proof, hyperbolic functions, polar coordinates, differential equations) and the third paper offers one optional strand in Mechanics, Statistics, Discrete mathematics or Further Pure. It is highly recommended, and often required, for mathematics, physics, engineering and computer science at top UK universities.

AS / Year 1 Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • AS1. Complex numbers
    Complex numbers, Argand diagrams, modulus-argument form, de Moivre's theorem (introductory), loci in the complex plane
  • AS2. Matrices
    Matrices, matrix algebra, determinants, inverses of 2x2 and 3x3 matrices, linear transformations, solving systems of linear equations
  • AS3. Further algebra and functions
    Roots of polynomials, Vieta's formulae, summation of series using standard results, Maclaurin series (introductory)
  • AS4. Vectors and proof
    Three-dimensional vectors, scalar and vector product, vector equations of lines and planes, proof by induction and contradiction
  • AS5. Series
    Method of differences, higher-order recurrence relations, generating functions (introductory)

A2 / Year 2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A26. Advanced complex numbers and matrices
    de Moivre's theorem (full), nth roots of unity, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, diagonalisation of matrices, Cayley-Hamilton theorem
  • A27. Further calculus
    Improper integrals, mean values, integration using reduction formulae, Maclaurin and Taylor series, arc length and surface area
  • A28. Polar coordinates and hyperbolic functions
    Polar coordinates and curves, area in polar form, hyperbolic functions and their inverses, Osborne's rule
  • A29. Differential equations
    First-order differential equations (separable, integrating factor), second-order linear differential equations, coupled systems, modelling
  • A210. Option (one of Paper 3)
    Option A: Further mechanics (momentum, collisions, circular motion, SHM, centres of mass, moments) · Option B: Further statistics (continuous random variables, chi-squared tests, confidence intervals, regression) · Option C: Discrete mathematics (graphs, algorithms, network flows, linear programming, game theory) · Option D: Further Pure (groups, number theory, further matrices and complex numbers)

Suitable Majors & Pathway 适用专业与衔接

数学、物理、工程、CS 顶尖高校的推荐或必修科目，竞争力提升明显。

A-Level Further Mathematics (AQA) is strongly preferred or required by leading UK universities for mathematics, theoretical physics, engineering, computer science, economics and actuarial science. The linear terminal exams reward deep abstract reasoning and rigorous proof, providing direct preparation for university real analysis, linear algebra, abstract algebra, differential equations and mathematical methods modules. Students targeting Cambridge, Oxford, Imperial, Warwick or UCL for maths-rich degrees are strongly advised to take it.
