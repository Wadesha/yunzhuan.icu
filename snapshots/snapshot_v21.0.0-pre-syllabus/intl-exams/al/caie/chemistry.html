A-Level Chemistry

A-Level 化学

CAIE 9701

Course Overview 课程介绍

CAIE 高级化学课程，分 AS 与 A2，化学/医学/药学申请核心科目。

A-Level Chemistry is a UK advanced secondary course split into AS (Year 1) and A2 (Year 2). It covers physical, inorganic and organic chemistry, with strong emphasis on microscopic structure and reaction mechanisms, plus hands-on practical and quantitative analysis skills. A key subject for chemistry, medicine, pharmacy, materials, chemical engineering and biology applicants.

AS Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • AS1. Atomic Structure
    Atomic structure, subatomic particles, isotopes, electron configuration, mass spectrometry
  • AS2. Atoms, Molecules and Stoichiometry
    Atoms, molecules and stoichiometry, relative atomic mass, mole, empirical/molecular formula, concentration calculations
  • AS3. Chemical Bonding
    Chemical bonding, ionic/covalent/metallic bonds, VSEPR, hybridisation, molecular shapes
  • AS4. States of Matter
    States of matter, kinetic theory of gases, liquids and solids, state changes, ionic lattice energy
  • AS5. Chemical Energetics
    Chemical energetics, enthalpy change, Hess's law, combustion and neutralisation enthalpy, bond energy
  • AS6. Electrochemistry
    Electrochemistry, galvanic cells, standard electrode potential, electrolysis, redox equation balancing
  • AS7. Equilibria
    Chemical equilibria, Le Chatelier's principle, equilibrium constant Kc, factors affecting equilibrium
  • AS8. Reaction Kinetics
    Reaction kinetics, collision theory, factors affecting rate, catalysts, Boltzmann distribution
  • AS9. The Periodic Table
    Periodic trends, periodicity of atomic radius and ionisation energy, main group property variation
  • AS10. Group 2 and Group 17
    Group 2 and Group 17, alkaline earth metals and halogens properties, reaction trends, uses
  • AS11. Nitrogen and Sulfur
    Nitrogen and sulfur, nitrogen oxides and ammonia, sulfur dioxide and sulfuric acid, environmental impact
  • AS12. Introduction to Organic Chemistry
    Introduction to organic chemistry, functional groups, homologous series, isomers, nomenclature, mechanism representation
  • AS13. Hydrocarbons
    Hydrocarbons, alkane free-radical substitution, alkene electrophilic addition, aromatic hydrocarbons and benzene structure
  • AS14. Halogenoalkanes and Alcohols
    Halogenoalkanes and alcohols, nucleophilic substitution and elimination, ethanol oxidation, identification reactions

A2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A215. Chemical Energetics (Advanced)
    Chemical energetics (advanced), Born-Haber cycle, lattice energy, entropy change, Gibbs free energy
  • A216. Electrochemistry (Advanced)
    Electrochemistry (advanced), electrochemical series applications, Nernst equation introduction, electrolysis quantitative calculations
  • A217. Equilibria (Advanced)
    Equilibria (advanced), Kp and gas-phase equilibrium, buffer solutions, solubility product Ksp
  • A218. Reaction Kinetics (Advanced)
    Reaction kinetics (advanced), rate equations, order determination, reaction mechanisms, Arrhenius equation
  • A219. Transition Elements
    Transition elements, d-orbitals and variable oxidation states, complex colour, catalysis
  • A220. Group 17 and Nitrogen (Advanced)
    Group 17 and nitrogen compounds (advanced), oxyacids, identification of nitrogen-containing inorganic ions
  • A221. Anions, Cations and Inorganic Analysis
    Qualitative analysis of anions and cations, precipitation reactions, ion identification schemes
  • A222. Phenols, Carboxylic Acids and Derivatives
    Phenols, carboxylic acids and derivatives, acidity comparison, esterification and hydrolysis
  • A223. Carbonyl Compounds
    Carbonyl compounds, aldehyde and ketone properties, nucleophilic addition, identification reactions, carboxylic acid synthesis
  • A224. Nitrogen Compounds
    Nitrogen-containing organic compounds, amines, amino acids, proteins, polyamides, diazonium salts
  • A225. Polymerisation
    Polymerisation, addition and condensation polymers, biodegradable polymers, properties and uses
  • A226. Organic Synthesis
    Organic synthesis, multi-step synthesis routes, functional group interconversion, route design
  • A227. Analytical Techniques
    Analytical techniques, infrared spectroscopy, mass spectrometry, NMR (1H and 13C), chromatography

Suitable Majors 适用专业与衔接

化学、医学、药学、材料等专业的重要前置科目，多所英国医学院必修。

A-Level Chemistry is a key prerequisite for chemistry, chemical engineering, pharmacy, medicine, dentistry, biochemistry, materials science, environmental science, food science and forensics. Many UK medical schools require Chemistry as a mandatory A-Level. It provides a solid foundation for university organic synthesis, physical chemistry and biochemistry courses.
