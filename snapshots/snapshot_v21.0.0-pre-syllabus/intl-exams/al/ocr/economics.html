A-Level Economics (OCR)

A-Level 经济（OCR）

OCR H060 (AS) / H460 (A-Level) — Withdrawn

Course Overview 课程介绍

OCR 已不再提供 A-Level 经济课程，原 H060/H460 规范于 2018 年完成最后一次评估后撤回。请改选 AQA 7136、Edexcel 9EC0 或 CAIE 9708。

OCR A-Level Economics (H060/H460) was a linear two-year course covering microeconomics, macroeconomics, international economics and development. OCR A-Level has moved to a linear structure since 2015, so all exams were taken at the end of the course. However, OCR withdrew the specification after the final assessment in 2018 and no longer offers an A-Level Economics qualification. Candidates wishing to study A-Level Economics should choose an alternative exam board such as AQA (7136), Pearson Edexcel (9EC0/9EB1) or CAIE (9708).

AS / Year 1 Syllabus AS 阶段章节（历史大纲）

  • ASUnit F581: Markets in Action
    Scarcity and choice, demand and supply, price mechanism, market failure, government intervention in markets (historical spec)
  • ASUnit F582: The National and International Economy
    Aggregate demand and supply, macroeconomic objectives, policy instruments, international trade and the balance of payments (historical spec)

A2 / Year 2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节（历史大纲）

  • A2Unit F583: Economics of Work and Leisure
    Labour markets, leisure industries, distribution of income and wealth, government intervention in labour markets (historical spec)
  • A2Unit F584: Transport Economics
    Transport markets, demand and supply in transport, market failure and intervention in transport, transport policy (historical spec)
  • A2Unit F585: The Global Economy
    Macroeconomic performance in a global context, international trade, development, globalisation, exchange rate systems (historical spec)

Suitable Majors & Pathway 适用专业与衔接

经济、金融、管理等专业基础科目，培养数据分析与论证能力。OCR 不再提供此科目，请改选其他考试局。

A-Level Economics is a key subject for economics, finance, accounting, business administration, public policy, international relations, politics and sociology. Most UK economics and finance programmes recommend or require A-Level Economics. As OCR no longer offers this qualification, candidates should select AQA (7136), Pearson Edexcel (9EC0/9EB1) or CAIE (9708) instead. The course content and university progression are equivalent across boards, developing data analysis, diagrammatic reasoning and argumentation skills.
