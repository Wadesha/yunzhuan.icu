IB English

IB 英语

IB Diploma · English A & B · HL/SL

Course Overview 课程介绍

IB 英语分 A（文学/语言与文学，近母语）与 B（外语）两系列，各设 HL/SL。

IB English has several strands: English A: Literature and English A: Language and Literature, for students at near-native level, emphasizing close reading, literary/non-literary analysis and comparative study; English B for learners of English as a foreign language, emphasizing communicative use and the four skills (listening, reading, writing, speaking); plus English A: Literature and Performance. All strands are available at HL and SL, with A strands reading 4 (SL) or 6 (HL) works. A foundational subject for humanities, media, law, and social science applicants.

SL Syllabus SL 阶段章节（English A 三大探索领域）

  • SL1. Readers, writers and texts
    Text selection / reader response / authorial intent / analysis of literary and non-literary texts
  • SL2. Time and space
    Literary and cultural context / historical background / cross-cultural reading / translation and adaptation
  • SL3. Intertextuality
    Relations between texts / thematic comparison / genre dialogue / adaptation and intertextuality
  • SL4. English B five themes
    English B themes: Identities / Experiences / Human ingenuity / Social organization / Sharing the planet
  • SL5. Individual Oral preparation
    Individual oral / oral expression on a global issue / 10-minute presentation + 5-minute Q&A

HL Additional Topics HL 额外章节

  • HL6. Extended reading list
    A strands HL reads 2 more works than SL (6 total vs SL 4)
  • HL7. Higher Level Essay
    1,200-1,500 word independent research essay, self-chosen topic and works, demonstrating critical inquiry
  • HL8. Comparative depth (Paper 2)
    HL Paper 2 comparison requires deeper theoretical frameworks and critical analysis
  • HL9. English B HL extension
    English B HL: longer and more complex writing / deeper reading texts / more abstract oral discussion
  • HL10. Introduction to critical theory
    HL encourages introductory critical theory (feminism, post-colonialism, deconstruction, etc.) to support textual analysis

Internal Assessment 内部评估 IA

IA 为 Individual Oral 个体口试，A 系列基于全球性问题，B 基于主题 stimulus。

The IB English IA is the Individual Oral, conducted in school and externally moderated. English A: based on one studied work plus a self-chosen non-literary text, students explore a global issue in a 10-minute oral + 5-minute Q&A, worth 30% (SL) / 20% (HL). English B: based on a stimulus and theme, students do a 12-15 min (HL) or 8-10 min (SL) oral presentation and discussion, worth 30% (SL) / 25% (HL). HL also completes an HL Essay as part of external assessment.
