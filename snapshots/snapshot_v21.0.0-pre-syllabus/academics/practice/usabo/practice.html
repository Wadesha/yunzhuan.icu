【B-EL-01】 Q1 · Cell Biology · Easy

Which organelle is known as the "powerhouse of the cell"?

  • A) Nucleus
  • B) Ribosome
  • C) Mitochondrion
  • D) Golgi apparatus
  • E) Lysosome
Answer: C) Mitochondrion. Mitochondria generate ATP through cellular respiration.
【B-GN-01】 Q2 · Genetics · Easy

In Mendelian genetics, if both parents are heterozygous (Aa), what is the probability that their child is homozygous recessive (aa)?

  • A) 0%
  • B) 25%
  • C) 50%
  • D) 75%
  • E) 100%
Answer: B) 25%. Punnett square: Aa × Aa → AA, Aa, Aa, aa. Probability of aa = 1/4 = 25%.
【B-MB-01】 Q3 · Molecular Biology · Easy

What is the basic unit of DNA structure?

  • A) Amino acid
  • B) Nucleotide
  • C) Monosaccharide
  • D) Fatty acid
  • E) Peptide
Answer: B) Nucleotide. DNA consists of nucleotides (phosphate + sugar + base).
【B-BC-01】 Q4 · Biochemistry · Easy

Which of the following is NOT a macromolecule?

  • A) Protein
  • B) Carbohydrate
  • C) Lipid
  • D) Nucleic acid
  • E) Amino acid
Answer: E) Amino acid. Amino acids are monomers, not macromolecules.
【B-EC-01】 Q5 · Ecology · Easy

What is the primary source of energy for most ecosystems?

  • A) Water
  • B) Sunlight
  • C) Carbon dioxide
  • D) Oxygen
  • E) Minerals
Answer: B) Sunlight. Photosynthetic organisms convert sunlight to chemical energy.
【B-PH-01】 Q6 · Physiology · Easy

Which part of the neuron receives signals from other neurons?

  • A) Axon
  • B) Dendrite
  • C) Cell body
  • D) Myelin sheath
  • E) Synapse
Answer: B) Dendrite. Dendrites receive incoming signals; axons send outgoing signals.
【B-EL-02】 Q7 · Cell Biology · Medium

During which phase of mitosis do the chromosomes align at the cell's equator?

  • A) Prophase
  • B) Metaphase
  • C) Anaphase
  • D) Telophase
  • E) Interphase
Answer: B) Metaphase. Chromosomes line up at the metaphase plate during metaphase.
【B-GN-02】 Q8 · Genetics · Medium

A woman with type A blood has a child with type O blood. What is the woman's genotype?

  • A) IAIA
  • B) IAi
  • C) ii
  • D) IAIB
  • E) Cannot determine
Answer: B) IAi. Type A blood can be IAIA or IAi. To have a type O child (ii), she must be IAi, passing i allele.
【B-MB-02】 Q9 · Molecular Biology · Medium

Which process synthesizes mRNA from a DNA template?

  • A) Translation
  • B) Transcription
  • C) Replication
  • D) Mutation
  • E) Repair
Answer: B) Transcription. RNA polymerase synthesizes mRNA from DNA during transcription.
【B-BC-02】 Q10 · Biochemistry · Medium

What is the net ATP yield from glycolysis alone?

  • A) 0
  • B) 2
  • C) 4
  • D) 36
  • E) 38
Answer: B) 2. Glycolysis produces 4 ATP but invests 2 ATP, net yield = 2 ATP.
【B-EC-02】 Q11 · Ecology · Medium

The maximum number of individuals an environment can support is called the:

  • A) Birth rate
  • B) Carrying capacity
  • C) Growth rate
  • D) Limiting factor
  • E) Survivorship
Answer: B) Carrying capacity (K). It represents the maximum sustainable population size.
【B-PH-02】 Q12 · Physiology · Medium

Which hormone is released in response to low blood calcium levels?

  • A) Insulin
  • B) Parathyroid hormone
  • C) Thyroxine
  • D) Cortisol
  • E) Estrogen
Answer: B) Parathyroid hormone (PTH). PTH raises blood calcium by stimulating bone resorption.
【B-EL-03】 Q13 · Cell Biology · Hard

A cell is placed in a hypertonic solution. What will happen to the cell?

  • A) It swells
  • B) It shrinks
  • C) It stays the same
  • D) It bursts
  • E) It becomes turgid
Answer: B) It shrinks. In a hypertonic solution, water moves out of the cell, causing it to shrink (crenation).
【B-GN-03】 Q14 · Genetics · Hard

What is the mode of inheritance for mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA)?

  • A) Autosomal dominant
  • B) Autosomal recessive
  • C) X-linked
  • D) Maternal
  • E) Paternal
Answer: D) Maternal. mtDNA is inherited exclusively from the mother's egg cell.
【B-MB-03】 Q15 · Molecular Biology · Hard

Which of the following is the correct sequence of events during PCR?

  • A) Extension → Annealing → Denaturation
  • B) Denaturation → Annealing → Extension
  • C) Annealing → Denaturation → Extension
  • D) Denaturation → Extension → Annealing
  • E) Extension → Denaturation → Annealing
Answer: B) Denaturation → Annealing → Extension. This is the standard PCR cycle: heat to separate strands, cool for primers to anneal, extend with polymerase.
【B-BC-03】 Q16 · Biochemistry · Hard

The enzyme responsible for converting pyruvate to acetyl-CoA is located in the:

  • A) Cytoplasm
  • B) Mitochondrial matrix
  • C) Inner mitochondrial membrane
  • D) Outer mitochondrial membrane
  • E) Nucleus
Answer: B) Mitochondrial matrix. Pyruvate dehydrogenase complex is in the mitochondrial matrix.
【B-EC-03】 Q17 · Ecology · Hard

Which type of ecological interaction benefits one species while the other is harmed?

  • A) Mutualism
  • B) Commensalism
  • C) Parasitism
  • D) Competition
  • E) Amensalism
Answer: C) Parasitism. One species (parasite) benefits at the expense of the other (host).
【B-PH-03】 Q18 · Physiology · Hard

The resting membrane potential of a neuron is primarily maintained by:

  • A) Na⁺/K⁺ pump
  • B) Ca²⁺ channels
  • C) Cl⁻ channels
  • D) H⁺ pump
  • E) Glucose transporters
Answer: A) Na⁺/K⁺ pump. The sodium-potassium pump maintains the resting potential by pumping 3 Na⁺ out and 2 K⁺ in.
【B-EV-01】 Q19 · Evolution · Hard

Which of the following is an example of convergent evolution?

  • A) Forelimbs of humans, bats, and whales
  • B) Wings of birds and insects
  • C) DNA sequences of related species
  • D) Embryonic development
  • E) Fossil record
Answer: B) Wings of birds and insects. These analogous structures evolved independently for flight, not from a common ancestor.
【B-EL-04】 Q20 · Cell Biology · Hard

Which of the following is NOT part of the endomembrane system?

  • A) Rough ER
  • B) Smooth ER
  • C) Golgi apparatus
  • D) Mitochondria
  • E) Lysosomes
Answer: D) Mitochondria. Mitochondria have their own membranes and are not part of the endomembrane system (which includes ER, Golgi, vesicles, lysosomes, and nuclear envelope).
