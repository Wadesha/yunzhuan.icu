Part 1 · Introduction & Interview

Part 1 · Daily Questions

Topic: Hometown & Living

  1. Let's talk about your hometown. Where is it? What do you like about it?
  2. Is there anything you would like to change about your hometown?
  3. Do you prefer living in a house or an apartment? Why?
  4. What kind of accommodation would you like to live in the future?

Part 2 · Individual Long Turn

你会收到一张 Cue Card，有 1 分钟准备时间，然后需要说 1-2 分钟。

Part 2 · Cue Card

Describe a skill you would like to learn in the future.

You should say:

  • What the skill is
  • How you learned about it
  • Why you want to learn it
  • And explain how you think you would use it in the future

Part 3 · Two-way Discussion

考官会就 Part 2 的话题进行更深入的讨论，约 4-5 分钟。

Part 3 · Discussion Questions

Topic: Learning Skills in the Digital Age

  1. Do you think it's important to keep learning new skills throughout life? Why/Why not?
  2. How has technology changed the way people learn new skills?
  3. Some people argue that traditional skills are being lost. Do you agree?
  4. Should schools teach more practical skills? Why/Why not?
  5. How might AI affect skill learning in the future?
