Environmental Engineering

专业介绍

环境工程应用科学和工程原理解决环境问题，包括水处理、空气污染控制、废物管理、可再生能源、可持续发展。毕业生在政府环保部门、咨询公司、能源公司、制造业等领域工作。

Environmental Engineering applies scientific and engineering principles to solve environmental problems, including water treatment, air pollution control, waste management, renewable energy, and sustainable development. Graduates work in government environmental agencies, consulting firms, energy companies, manufacturing, and more.

核心课程 Core Courses

Water Resources Engineering Air Pollution Control Solid Waste Management Environmental Chemistry Sustainable Engineering Renewable Energy

就业方向 Career Paths

Environmental Engineer、Water Resources Engineer、Environmental Consultant、Sustainability Specialist
