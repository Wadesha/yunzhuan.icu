A-Level Psychology (AQA)

A-Level 心理学（AQA）

AQA 7182 · OxfordAQA 9685

Course Overview 课程介绍

AQA A-Level 心理学，线性结构，社会、记忆、临床与生物心理学并重，含三组选修。

A-Level Psychology (AQA 7182) is a linear qualification covering social influence, memory, attachment, psychopathology, approaches, biopsychology and research methods, plus issues and debates and three optional topics, examined by three terminal papers at the end of two years. The course combines scientific methodology with theories of behaviour and mental processes. It is a popular subject for psychology, neuroscience, criminology, sociology, education, nursing and business applicants.

AS / Year 1 Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • AS1. Social influence
    Conformity (Asch, types and explanations), obedience (Milgram, agentic state, legitimacy of authority), resistance to social influence, minority influence, social change
  • AS2. Memory
    Multi-store model (Atkinson-Shiffrin), working memory model (Baddeley), types of long-term memory, eyewitness testimony, cognitive interview, misleading information
  • AS3. Attachment
    Caregiver-infant interactions, Schaffer's stages, animal studies (Lorenz, Harlow), Bowlby's monotropic theory and internal working model, Ainsworth's Strange Situation, maternal deprivation, Romanian orphan studies, influence of early attachment on later relationships
  • AS4. Psychopathology
    Phobias (behavioural explanation and treatment), depression (cognitive explanation and CBT), OCD (biological explanation and drug treatment), deviation from ideal mental health, statistical infrequency, failure to function adequately
  • AS5. Approaches in psychology
    Origins of psychology, behaviourist (Pavlov, Skinner), social learning theory (Bandura), cognitive approach, biological approach, humanistic psychology, comparison of approaches
  • AS6. Biopsychology
    Nervous system (CNS, PNS, ANS, endocrine), fight or flight response, neurons and synaptic transmission, brain localisation, ways of studying the brain, plasticity and functional recovery
  • AS7. Research methods
    Experimental and non-experimental methods, sampling, ethics, hypothesis testing, types of data, descriptive statistics, inferential statistics, features of science, reporting psychological investigations

A2 / Year 2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A28. Research methods (advanced) and inferential statistics
    Extended research methods, statistical testing (sign test, Spearman's rho, Pearson's r, Wilcoxon, Mann-Whitney, related and unrelated t-test, Chi-squared), probability and significance, Type I and Type II errors
  • A29. Issues and debates in psychology
    Gender bias, culture bias, free will vs determinism, nature-nurture, holism vs reductionism, idiographic vs nomothetic approaches, ethical implications of research
  • A210. Option 1 (one of)
    Relationships (evolutionary explanations, sexual selection, self-disclosure, virtual relationships) · Gender (biosocial, psychodynamic, cognitive, gender dysphoria) · Cognition and development (Piaget, Vygotsky, theory of mind, Baillargeon)
  • A211. Option 2 (one of)
    Schizophrenia (classification, biological and psychological explanations, drug and psychological therapies, interactionist approach) · Eating behaviour (evolutionary, neural and cognitive, eating disorders) · Stress (Selye's GAS, immune system, life changes, workplace, personality, drug and psychological therapies)
  • A212. Option 3 (one of)
    Aggression (neural and hormonal, ethological, evolutionary, social psychological, media, institutional) · Forensic psychology (offender profiling, biological and psychological explanations of crime, custody, behaviour modification, anger management) · Addiction (biological, cognitive and behavioural, risk factors, treatment including CBT and drug therapy)

Suitable Majors & Pathway 适用专业与衔接

心理学、神经科学、犯罪学、教育、护理、商科等方向的常见前置科目。

A-Level Psychology (AQA) is a popular subject for psychology, neuroscience, criminology, sociology, education, nursing, occupational therapy, sports science, marketing and human resource management applicants. The linear terminal exams reward scientific evaluation of theories, methodology and statistical reasoning, providing a strong foundation for university cognitive psychology, biological psychology, social psychology, research methods and abnormal psychology modules. Many UK universities regard Psychology as a science subject for BSc Psychology entry.
