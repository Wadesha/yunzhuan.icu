Common App · 4-year bachelor 美国大学

The United States hosts 4000+ degree-granting institutions, the largest higher-education market in the world. Most undergraduate applications go through the Common App (or Coalition / direct), with SAT or ACT scores widely considered, plus transcripts, essays and recommendation letters. Bachelor programs run 4 years with a flexible liberal-arts style general education in the first two years.
