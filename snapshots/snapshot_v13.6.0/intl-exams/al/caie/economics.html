Course Overview 课程介绍

CAIE 高级经济课程，含微观与宏观两大板块，强调图表分析。

A-Level Economics is a UK advanced secondary course split into AS (Year 1) and A2 (Year 2). It covers microeconomics (price mechanism, market failure, government intervention) and macroeconomics (national income, employment, inflation, trade, development), with strong emphasis on diagrammatic analysis, data interpretation and economic argumentation. A key subject for economics, finance, management, accounting and public policy.

AS Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • AS1. Basic Economic Problem and Resource Allocation
    Basic economic problem and resource allocation, scarcity, opportunity cost, production possibility curve
  • AS2. The Price System and the Microeconomy
    Price system and microeconomy, demand and supply, price elasticity, utility theory
  • AS3. Market Failure and Government Intervention
    Market failure and government intervention, externalities, public goods, asymmetric information, taxes and subsidies
  • AS4. The Macroeconomy: Aggregate Demand and Supply
    Macroeconomy: aggregate demand and supply, national income, multiplier, equilibrium
  • AS5. The Macroeconomy: Inflation and Unemployment
    Macroeconomy: inflation and unemployment, causes and measurement of inflation, types of unemployment, Phillips curve
  • AS6. The Macroeconomy: Economic Growth and Living Standards
    Macroeconomy: economic growth and living standards, actual and potential growth, sustainable development
  • AS7. The Macroeconomy: Money and Banking
    Macroeconomy: money and banking, functions of money, interest rates, central bank
  • AS8. International Trade
    International trade, comparative advantage, terms of trade, tariffs and quotas, current account
  • AS9. The Balance of Payments and Exchange Rates
    Balance of payments and exchange rates, floating and fixed exchange rates, exchange rate determination, BoP equilibrium
  • AS10. Government Macroeconomic Policy Objectives
    Government macroeconomic policy objectives, policy instruments, fiscal and monetary policy

A2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A211. The Price System and the Microeconomy (Advanced)
    Price system and microeconomy (advanced), marginal utility, indifference curves, cost theory
  • A212. Market Structure and Competition
    Market structure and competition, perfect competition, monopoly, oligopoly, monopolistic competition, pricing strategies
  • A213. Government Microeconomic Intervention (Advanced)
    Government microeconomic intervention (advanced), price controls, regulation and privatisation, income redistribution
  • A214. The Macroeconomy (Advanced): AD-AS Analysis
    Macroeconomy (advanced): AD-AS analysis, short-run and long-run aggregate supply, economic shocks
  • A215. Government Macroeconomic Policy (Advanced)
    Government macroeconomic policy (advanced), fiscal and monetary policy, supply-side policy, policy conflicts
  • A216. International Trade (Advanced)
    International trade (advanced), arguments for trade protection, regional trade agreements, WTO
  • A217. Exchange Rates and Balance of Payments (Advanced)
    Exchange rates and balance of payments (advanced), Marshall-Lerner condition, J-curve, exchange rate policy
  • A218. Economic Development
    Economic development, development indicators, barriers to development, development strategies, poverty trap
  • A219. Growth, Development and Sustainability
    Growth, development and sustainability, resource and environmental constraints, equity and efficiency, globalisation
  • A220. Globalisation and International Economic Integration
    Globalisation and international economic integration, multinational corporations, capital flows, integration effects

Suitable Majors 适用专业与衔接

经济、金融、管理等专业基础科目，培养数据分析与论证能力。

A-Level Economics is a key subject for economics, finance, accounting, business administration, public policy, international relations, politics and sociology. Most UK economics and finance programmes recommend or require A-Level Economics. It provides solid prerequisite knowledge for university microeconomics, macroeconomics and econometrics, and develops data analysis and argumentation skills.
