A-Level Chemistry (Edexcel IAL)

A-Level 化学（Edexcel IAL）

Edexcel IAL WCH11-15

Course Overview 课程介绍

Edexcel IAL 化学采用模块化按 Unit 考试，可重考单模块，化学、医学、药学与材料科学申请核心科目。

Edexcel International A-Level Chemistry is a modular course assessed unit by unit across five written units. Students sit each Unit exam separately and may retake individual units. Content spans atomic structure and bonding, energetics and group chemistry, organic chemistry, rates and equilibria, transition metals and organic nitrogen chemistry, with practical skills examined in Unit 3. A core subject for chemistry, medicine, pharmacy, chemical engineering and materials science.

AS Syllabus AS 阶段章节（Units 1-3）

  • Unit 1Atomic Structure and the Periodic Table
    Atomic structure, electron configuration, isotopes, mass spectrometry, periodicity and ionisation energies
  • Unit 1Bonding and Structure
    Ionic, covalent, metallic and dative bonding, shapes of molecules (VSEPR), electronegativity, intermolecular forces
  • Unit 1Introduction to Organic Chemistry
    Alkanes, fractional distillation, cracking, isomerism, alkenes, organic reaction mechanisms and nomenclature
  • Unit 2Energetics
    Enthalpy changes, Hess's law, bond enthalpies, calorimetry, experimental determination of enthalpy changes
  • Unit 2Group Chemistry
    Group 1 and Group 2 metals, Group 7 halogens, trends in properties and reactivity, displacement reactions
  • Unit 2Halogenoalkanes and Alkenes
    Nucleophilic substitution and elimination of halogenoalkanes, electrophilic addition to alkenes, polymerisation
  • Unit 3Practical Skills in Chemistry I
    Practical techniques, titrations, qualitative ion analysis, data collection and evaluation (written practical paper)

A2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节（Units 4-5）

  • Unit 4Kinetics
    Rates of reaction, rate equations, orders of reaction, Arrhenius equation, catalysis and reaction mechanisms
  • Unit 4Chemical Equilibria
    Dynamic equilibrium, Le Chatelier's principle, equilibrium constants Kc and Kp, industrial applications
  • Unit 4Acid-Base Equilibria
    pH, Ka and Kw, weak acids and bases, buffer solutions, titration curves and indicators
  • Unit 4Further Organic Chemistry
    Alcohols, carbonyl compounds, carboxylic acids, esters, arenes, chirality and organic synthesis routes
  • Unit 5Transition Metals
    Transition metal properties, complex ions, ligand substitution, redox titrations and colorimetry
  • Unit 5Organic Nitrogen Chemistry
    Amines, amides, amino acids, proteins, condensation polymers and organic synthesis
  • Unit 5Organic Synthesis and Analysis
    NMR spectroscopy, IR spectroscopy, mass spectrometry, chromatography and combined analytical techniques

Suitable Majors & Pathway 适合专业与升学路径

适合化学、医学、药学、化工、材料科学与生命科学专业，是英国医学与化学相关专业的必选科目。

Chemistry is essential for medicine, dentistry, veterinary science, pharmacy, chemistry, chemical engineering, biochemistry and materials science. Combined with Biology and Mathematics, it supports applications to life science and biomedical programmes. The modular Edexcel IAL format allows students to retake individual units to strengthen their final grade before university application.
