A-Level 物理

CAIE 9702

Course Overview 课程介绍

CAIE 高级物理课程，分 AS 与 A2 两阶段，理工科申请核心科目。

A-Level Physics is a UK advanced secondary course split into AS (Year 1) and A2 (Year 2). It covers mechanics, electromagnetism, waves, thermal physics, quantum physics and nuclear physics, with strong emphasis on practical skills and data analysis. A core subject for engineering, physics, CS and medicine applicants.

AS Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • AS1. Physical Quantities and Units
    Physical quantities, SI units, scalars and vectors, uncertainty and error analysis
  • AS2. Kinematics
    Kinematics, displacement/velocity/acceleration, equations of motion, graphical analysis
  • AS3. Dynamics
    Dynamics, Newton's laws of motion, momentum and impulse, composition and resolution of forces
  • AS4. Forces, Density and Pressure
    Forces, density and pressure, buoyancy, moments and equilibrium
  • AS5. Work, Energy and Power
    Work, kinetic and potential energy, energy conservation, power, efficiency
  • AS6. Deformation of Solids
    Deformation of solids, Hooke's law, Young's modulus, elastic potential energy
  • AS7. Waves
    Waves, progressive and stationary waves, transverse and longitudinal waves, wave speed/wavelength/frequency, refraction and reflection
  • AS8. Superposition
    Superposition, interference, diffraction, double-slit experiment, stationary wave formation
  • AS9. Electric Fields
    Electric fields, Coulomb's law, electric field strength, electric potential, uniform fields
  • AS10. D.C. Circuits
    D.C. circuits, series and parallel resistors, Kirchhoff's laws, internal resistance, electrical power
  • AS11. Particle Physics
    Particle physics, fundamental particles, quark model, beta decay, photoelectric effect

A2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A212. Motion in a Circle
    Circular motion, angular velocity, centripetal acceleration, centripetal force
  • A213. Gravitational Fields
    Gravitational fields, Newton's law of gravitation, field strength, gravitational potential, satellite orbits
  • A214. Temperature
    Temperature, thermal equilibrium, absolute temperature scale, thermometry principles
  • A215. Ideal Gases
    Ideal gases, Boyle's law, Charles's law, kinetic theory of gases
  • A216. Thermodynamics
    Thermodynamics, internal energy, specific heat capacity, latent heat, first law of thermodynamics
  • A217. Oscillations
    Oscillations, simple harmonic motion, damping and resonance, pendulum and spring-mass systems
  • A218. Electric Fields (Advanced)
    Electric fields (advanced), uniform fields, electric potential energy and potential difference
  • A219. Capacitance
    Capacitance, charging and discharging capacitors, energy storage, dielectrics
  • A220. Magnetic Fields
    Magnetic fields, Lorentz force, force on current-carrying conductors, magnetic flux
  • A221. Electromagnetic Induction
    Electromagnetic induction, Faraday's law, Lenz's law, transformers and generators
  • A222. Alternating Currents
    Alternating currents, RMS values, rectification, oscilloscope
  • A223. Quantum Physics
    Quantum physics, photon energy, de Broglie wavelength, wave-particle duality, energy levels and spectra
  • A224. Nuclear Physics
    Nuclear physics, binding energy, mass-energy equivalence, fission and fusion, radioactive decay

Exam Structure (CAIE 9702) 考试结构

Paper 1AS Multiple Choice · 1h15m · 31% of AS / 15.5% of A-Level
Paper 2AS Structured Questions · 1h15m · 46% of AS / 23% of A-Level
Paper 3AS Practical Skills · 2h · 23% of AS / 11.5% of A-Level
Paper 4A2 Structured Questions · 2h · 38.5% of A-Level
Paper 5A2 Planning, Analysis and Evaluation · 1h15m · 11.5% of A-Level

Practical Skills 实验技能

Paper 3 实操考试，Paper 5 实验设计与分析评估，重数据处理与误差分析。

Paper 3 is a hands-on practical exam; Paper 5 evaluates planning, analysis and evaluation without lab work. Both require instrument handling, error analysis, uncertainty propagation, graphing and linearization.
