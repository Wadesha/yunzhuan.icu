A-Level Business

A-Level 商科

CAIE 9609

Course Overview 课程介绍

CAIE 高级商科，涵盖六大板块，强调案例分析与商业决策。

A-Level Business is a UK advanced secondary course split into AS (Year 1) and A2 (Year 2). It covers six areas: business environment, people in organisations, marketing, operations, finance & accounting, and strategic management, with strong emphasis on case analysis, business decision-making and contextual application. A key subject for business administration, marketing, HR, accounting, entrepreneurship and international business.

AS Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • AS1. Business and Its Environment
    Business and its environment, types of business, business objectives, stakeholders, business size
  • AS2. Size and Growth of Business
    Size and growth of business, internal and external growth, mergers and acquisitions, multinational enterprises
  • AS3. People in Organisations
    People in organisations, organisational structure, leadership styles, motivation theories, recruitment and training
  • AS4. Human Resource Management
    Human resource management, performance appraisal, labour relations, organisational culture
  • AS5. Marketing
    Marketing, market research, market segmentation, marketing mix 4Ps
  • AS6. Marketing Strategy and Planning
    Marketing strategy and planning, product life cycle, branding strategy, pricing strategy
  • AS7. Operations Management
    Operations management, production methods, inventory management, quality, productivity
  • AS8. Operations Planning and Lean Production
    Operations planning and lean production, capacity utilisation, lean methods, supply chain
  • AS9. Finance and Accounting
    Finance and accounting, sources of finance, cost classification, cash flow forecasting
  • AS10. Financial Statements and Analysis
    Financial statements and analysis, income statement, balance sheet, financial ratios
  • AS11. Cost and Investment Appraisal
    Cost and investment appraisal, break-even analysis, payback period, net present value
  • AS12. External Environment Influences
    External environment influences, PESTEL analysis, business cycle, legal environment

A2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A213. Strategic Management
    Strategic management, strategic levels, SWOT analysis, strategic choice and evaluation
  • A214. Strategic Choice and Implementation
    Strategic choice and implementation, Ansoff matrix, strategy implementation, change management
  • A215. Business and Its Environment (Advanced)
    Business environment (advanced), globalisation impacts, multinational strategy, ethics and social responsibility
  • A216. People in Organisations (Advanced)
    People in organisations (advanced), management theory, team building, organisational change and culture
  • A217. Marketing (Advanced)
    Marketing (advanced), marketing audit, integrated marketing mix, global marketing
  • A218. Operations and Project Management (Advanced)
    Operations and project management (advanced), project management techniques, information management, innovation
  • A219. Finance and Accounting (Advanced)
    Finance (advanced), financial ratio analysis, investment decisions, risk assessment
  • A220. Investment Appraisal and Risk
    Investment appraisal and risk, internal rate of return, sensitivity analysis, decision-making under uncertainty
  • A221. Strategic Position and Direction
    Strategic position and direction, Porter's five forces, competitive advantage, strategic alliances
  • A222. Strategic Implementation, Evaluation and Change
    Strategic implementation, evaluation and change, implementation barriers, strategic evaluation, strategic failure

Exam Structure (CAIE 9609) 考试结构

Paper 1AS Short Answer and Essay · 1h30m · 40% of AS / 20% of A-Level
Paper 2AS Data Response · 1h30m · 60% of AS / 20% of A-Level
Paper 3A2 Case Study · 3h · 60% of A-Level

Suitable Majors 适用专业与衔接

工商管理、市场营销、会计等专业基础，培养商业决策能力。

A-Level Business is a key subject for business administration, marketing, HR, accounting, finance, international business, entrepreneurship, hospitality and retail management. It provides solid prerequisite knowledge for university business case studies, principles of management, marketing, and organisational behaviour, and develops business decision-making and analytical skills.
