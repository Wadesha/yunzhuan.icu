AP Further Mathematics

AP 进阶数学

College Board · Advanced Placement

Course Overview 课程介绍

AP 不提供 Further Mathematics 独立科目，深度上限为 Calculus BC + Statistics。

It should be stated plainly: the AP system does not offer a stand-alone "Further Mathematics" course, unlike A-Level Further Maths or IB Mathematics AA/AI HL. The ceiling of AP math depth is AP Calculus BC (which adds series and parametric/polar/vector functions on top of AB), optionally paired with AP Statistics. Students seeking more advanced math typically take university Linear Algebra, Multivariable Calculus or Discrete Math via Dual Enrollment.

Closest Substitutes 相关替代科目章节

推荐 Calculus BC 作深度上限、Statistics 作统计方向、Dual Enrollment 补足更高阶数学。

  • BCAP Calculus BC (depth ceiling)
    All of AB plus parametric / polar / vector functions and sequences & series (incl. Taylor/Maclaurin); the most mathematically advanced AP exam
  • StatAP Statistics (statistics track)
    Exploring data; sampling and experiments; probability and random variables; sampling distributions; inference (proportions, means, chi-square, regression slopes)
  • DualDual Enrollment (recommended supplement)
    For Linear Algebra, Multivariable Calculus, Differential Equations or Discrete Math, take university courses via Dual Enrollment during high school

Statistics · Section IIFRQ · 6 questions · 90 min · 50%
Note (说明)AP has no Further Math exam; the above are the two closest AP exam structures

Credit Transfer 学分转换与衔接

无 Further Math 考试，按所选 AP 科目分别换分；更高阶数学走 Dual Enrollment。

AP has no Further Math exam; credit is granted per AP course taken. A BC score of 5 often earns Calculus I + II credit, while Statistics 4/5 maps to an introductory statistics course. For more advanced math, Dual Enrollment is recommended. See the AP Mathematics page for details.
