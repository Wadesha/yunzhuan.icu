IGCSE History

IGCSE 历史

CAIE 0470

Course Overview 课程介绍

IGCSE 历史 14-16 岁，Core Content（1919 年以来国际关系）+ Depth Study 四选一，无 Core/Extended 分档，A-Level/IB 衔接课程。

IGCSE History is the international GCSE History course for students aged 14-16, preparing students for A-Level History, IB History and humanities pathways. It comprises a Core Content (International Relations since 1919) and one Depth Study (Germany 1918-45, Russia 1905-41, USA 1919-41, or China 1930-90). It develops source analysis, causation, change and continuity, and interpretation skills. There is no Core/Extended tier; all candidates sit the same papers.

Syllabus 章节大纲

Core Content - 20th-century International Relations (since 1919)

  • Core1. Were the peace treaties of 1919-23 fair?
    Treaty of Versailles 1919-23 / Paris Peace Conference / aims of the main victors / terms and evaluation
  • Core2. To what extent was the League of Nations a success?
    League of Nations / successes and failures in the 1920s / causes of failure in the 1930s / Abyssinia and Manchuria crises
  • Ext3. How far was Hitler's foreign policy to blame for the outbreak of war in 1939?
    Hitler's foreign policy / rearmament / Rhineland / Munich Agreement / Nazi-Soviet Pact / origins of WWII
  • Ext4. Who was to blame for the Cold War?
    Origins of the Cold War / Yalta and Potsdam / Truman Doctrine and Marshall Plan / Berlin Blockade / Communist expansion
  • Ext5. How effectively did the USA contain the spread of Communism?
    US containment of Communism / Cuban Missile Crisis / Korean War / Vietnam War / Castro
  • Ext6. How secure was the USSR's control over Eastern Europe, 1948-c.1989?
    USSR control over Eastern Europe / Hungary 1956 / Czechoslovakia 1968 / Solidarity / Fall of the Berlin Wall

Depth Study (choose one of four)

  • ExtDepth Study A: Germany, 1918-45
    Germany 1918-45 / Weimar Republic / Hitler's rise to power / Nazi dictatorship / the Holocaust
  • ExtDepth Study B: Russia, 1905-41
    Russia 1905-41 / 1917 Revolutions / Lenin and Stalin / Five-Year Plans / Great Purge
  • ExtDepth Study C: USA, 1919-41
    USA 1919-41 / Boom and Bust / Roosevelt's New Deal / Isolationism and the eve of WWII
  • ExtDepth Study D: China, 1930-90
    China 1930-90 / Civil War (KMT vs CPC) / Mao era / Great Leap Forward and Cultural Revolution / Reform and Opening

Core vs Extended Core 与 Extended 区分

0470 不设 Core/Extended 分档，难度由题型与评估深度区分，Paper 2 史源分析要求最高。

ExtNote: IGCSE History (0470 / 0977) has no Core/Extended tier; all candidates sit the same Paper 1, Paper 2 and Component 3 (or Paper 4), graded A*-G or 9-1. Difficulty gradients reflect question type and assessment depth rather than tiering.

Specifically: Paper 1 is structured questions (causation, impact and significance essays); Paper 2 is source-based analysis (a prescribed topic announced annually, requiring source-value analysis, cross-corroboration and refutation), carrying the highest evaluative demand; Component 3 / Paper 4 covers Depth Study extended writing. Topic tags here reflect relative difficulty: Core Content 1-2 are basic narrative and causal analysis (core), while 3-6 and all Depth Studies involve more complex causal argument and interpretation (ext).

Exam Structure (CAIE 0470) 考试结构

Paper 1Structured Questions · 2h · 60 marks · 40%
Paper 2Historical Source Investigation · 2h · 50 marks · 33%
Component 3Coursework · 1500-2000 words · 40 marks · 27%
Paper 4Alternative to Coursework · 1h · 40 marks · 27%

Note: All candidates sit Paper 1+2 and choose either Component 3 or Paper 4 for Depth Study assessment. Paper 2's prescribed topic (drawn from one of the 6 Core Content themes) is announced annually.

Pathway 适用衔接

衔接 A-Level History 9484/9389、IB History，政治/国际关系/法律/新闻建议 A*/9。

IGCSE History is the standard preparation for A-Level History (CAIE 9484 / 9389), IB History HL/SL and humanities pathways (politics, international relations, law, journalism). After completing IGCSE History, students progress directly to A-Level AS; those with a solid foundation in source analysis, causation and interpretation transition more smoothly to A-Level broader time periods (medieval, early modern, modern), thematic comparative history and independent coursework. For students applying to history, politics, law, international relations or journalism, an A* or 9 in IGCSE History is recommended.
