IB Physics

IB 物理

IB Diploma · Physics HL/SL

Course Overview 课程介绍

IB 物理分 HL 与 SL，覆盖力学、电磁、量子等核心主题，IA 占 20%。

IB Physics is a two-year secondary physics course offered by the International Baccalaureate Organization (IBO), available at both Higher Level (HL) and Standard Level (SL). The course covers measurements and uncertainties, mechanics, thermal physics, waves, electricity and magnetism, circular motion and gravitation, atomic nuclear and particle physics, and energy production. HL extends into wave phenomena, fields, electromagnetic induction, and quantum and nuclear physics, plus one Option chosen from engineering physics, astrophysics, imaging or relativity. It emphasizes experimental inquiry, data analysis, and scientific thinking — a key subject for engineering, physics, and CS applicants.

SL Syllabus SL 阶段章节

  • SL1. Measurements and uncertainties
    SI units / scalars and vectors / errors and uncertainty propagation / graph analysis
  • SL2. Mechanics
    Kinematics / dynamics / momentum and energy / circular motion (basics)
  • SL3. Thermal physics
    Temperature and thermal equilibrium / ideal gases / first law of thermodynamics / specific and latent heat
  • SL4. Waves
    Oscillations / wave characteristics / wave behavior / refraction and reflection / diffraction and interference
  • SL5. Electricity and magnetism
    Electric fields / current and circuits / magnetic fields / electromagnetic induction (basics)
  • SL6. Circular motion and gravitation
    Centripetal acceleration / Newton's law of gravitation / satellite orbits
  • SL7. Atomic, nuclear and particle physics
    Atomic models / nuclear reactions and binding energy / elementary particles and quarks
  • SL8. Energy production
    Primary energy sources / heat transfer / solar power / nuclear power / environmental impact

HL Additional Topics HL 额外章节

  • HL9. Wave phenomena
    Simple harmonic motion (in depth) / double-slit and single-slit diffraction / multiple-slit diffraction / resolution / Doppler effect
  • HL10. Fields
    Gravitational fields (in depth) / electric fields (in depth) / electric potential energy and potential / magnetic fields (in depth) / field superposition
  • HL11. Electromagnetic induction
    Faraday's law / Lenz's law / AC generators / transformers / rectification
  • HL12. Quantum and nuclear physics
    Matter waves / uncertainty principle / tunneling / nuclear stability
  • HLOption A. Relativity
    Option: Special relativity / Lorentz transformations / introduction to general relativity
  • HLOption B. Engineering physics
    Option: Rotational dynamics / fluid mechanics / heat engines and heat pumps / damped oscillations
  • HLOption C. Imaging
    Option: Optical imaging / medical imaging / sonar and ultrasound / fiber optics and diffraction imaging
  • HLOption D. Astrophysics
    Option: Stellar spectra and classification / Hertzsprung-Russell diagram / cosmology / Big Bang and dark matter

Internal Assessment 内部评估 IA

IA 是独立实验探究，占 20%，需自行设计实验、收集数据并撰写 6-12 页报告。

The IB Physics IA is an individual experimental investigation worth 20% of the final grade. Students formulate a research question, design and conduct an experiment, analyze data, and produce a 6-12 page report (~10 hours of work). Assessment criteria cover personal engagement, exploration, analysis, evaluation, and communication. Topics may come from any part of the syllabus and real-world contexts are encouraged.
