传播学

Communications & Media

专业介绍

传播学研究信息如何在个人、群体、社会中传递和影响，涵盖新闻学、广告、公共关系、媒体研究、数字媒体。毕业生在媒体、广告、公关、市场营销、企业传播、新闻等领域。

Communications studies how information is transmitted and influences individuals, groups, and society, covering journalism, advertising, public relations, media studies, and digital media. Graduates work in media, advertising, PR, marketing, corporate communications, journalism, and more.
