HARVARD UNIVERSITY
Office of Admissions and Financial Aid · Cambridge, Massachusetts 02138

December 15, 2025

Dear Li Ming,

On behalf of the President and Fellows of Harvard College, I am delighted to inform you that you have been admitted to the Class of 2030 under Harvard's Restrictive Early Action plan. The Committee on Admissions was deeply impressed by your academic record, your sustained commitment to expanding access to computer science education, and the thoughtfulness with which you approached our supplemental questions.

As a member of the Class of 2030, you will join a community of approximately 1,650 students chosen from a pool of 9,500 early applicants and 56,000 total applicants. We are confident that you will contribute distinctively to our community of scholars, and we hope you will find the same inspiration in your classmates that we found in your application.

Harvard College is need-blind for all admitted students, including international applicants. Your financial aid eligibility has been provisionally determined and is detailed in the accompanying Financial Aid Notification, which is viewable in the Applicant Portal. Please note that your admission is contingent upon continued strong academic performance through the remainder of your secondary school career and the successful completion of all coursework in progress.

To confirm your place in the Class of 2030, please submit your enrollment reply by January 1, 2026, via the Applicant Portal. You are under no obligation to enroll; Harvard's Restrictive Early Action is non-binding, and you retain the freedom to apply to and enroll at any institution of your choice.

Should you choose to enroll, you will be invited to our admitted students program in April, at which time you will be able to meet with faculty, current students, and other admitted students. We will be in touch with details in the coming months.

Congratulations on this remarkable achievement. We look forward to learning of your decision.

William R. Fitzsimmons
Dean of Admissions and Financial Aid
Harvard College
