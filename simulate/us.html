你的文书初稿（v1，612 字）
It was 2 a.m. in a computer lab in Yunnan, and twenty middle-schoolers were staring at me like I was speaking another language. I was teaching them Python.

Three years earlier, I had been one of them—a quiet kid in a city middle school who stumbled into coding through an online video and discovered that I could build things that worked. By 10th grade I had won USACO gold; by 11th I was on the IMO team. None of it had felt unfair. The competitions were hard, the teachers were good, the resources were abundant. The pattern of "effort → result" had always held.

Then in the summer of 2024 I taught a five-day Python camp at a rural middle school in Yunnan. The students were eager, sharp, and curious—the same materials that had launched me bored them within an hour. They did not lack talent. They lacked access: to faster computers, to mentors, to a community that took them seriously. A girl named Mei asked me a question about loops that I had answered in my sleep a hundred times. I realized, embarrassed, that I had been assuming the entire world had the same starting line I did.

That summer I went back twice. I rewrote the curriculum around their questions, not mine. I translated every example into problems from their village. I started a WeChat group that is still active. Two of the students later entered the provincial programming contest—their school's first entries in five years.

I want to study computer science not to build better algorithms but to build better access. The same gift I was given—someone who answered my boring questions without rolling their eyes—I want to give away at scale. Whether that means research on AI for low-resource languages, or a startup in ed-tech, or a career in policy, I want to spend my four years here learning how to make the starting line fairer than mine was.

④ 模拟面试（校友面试 30-45 分钟）

Q1. Tell me about yourself.
我今年 11 年级，在北京读高中。我从小喜欢数学和编程，10 年级时创办了学校 AI 社团。去年暑假我去云南乡村教 Python，那些孩子改变了我对"教育"的看法，所以我想研究 AI 在低资源场景下的应用。
评分：8/10
Q2. Why Computer Science?
第一次感受到 CS 的力量是在云南。那些孩子的问题不是"能不能学"，而是"用得起吗"。我意识到一个算法如果在 4GB 内存上跑不起来，那它对 90% 的世界是没用的。CS 在我眼里不是"造最聪明的 AI"，而是"让 AI 在最普通的硬件上也跑得动"。
评分：9/10
Q3. Tell me about a time you failed.
高一时 USACO 铂金组月赛，我交了卷才发现用了错误的输入格式，连续 3 道题零分。那次以后我学会了"先写测试再写代码"——只有真正被它咬过的人才知道价值。
评分：8/10
Q4. What's a book that changed you?
Mindstorms by Seymour Papert。他讲的"通过创造来学习"的哲学，正好解释了我在云南看到的现象——孩子们不是不能学，是没人给他们一个可以"创造"的工具。
评分：9/10
Q5. What would you ask me?
您当年在读本科时最受益的一门课是什么？您对现在 AI 领域"能力越大责任越大"这句话怎么看？
评分：9/10
