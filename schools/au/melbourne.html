About the University 学校简介

Australia's oldest and most prestigious university, known for its Melbourne Model (generalist first year + specialization). Strong in medicine, law, business, engineering, and arts.

Application Notes 申请要点

  • Direct application or through UAC (for Australian high school students)
  • Melbourne Model: Bachelor of Arts / Science / Commerce / Environments / Fine Arts / Music
  • Professional degrees (Medicine, Law, Engineering) at graduate level
  • ATAR or equivalent qualification required
  • Prerequisites vary by degree

Student Life 校园生活

Melbourne is consistently ranked the worlds most livable city. Great coffee, food, arts, and sports culture. Beautiful campus with historic buildings and parklands.

Weather 气候环境

Four seasons in one day is the saying! Mild climate. Winter: 10-15°C, cool and rainy. Summer: 20-30°C, with occasional heatwaves.
