UNSW 是领先的研究型大学，工程、商科和科技专业实力强劲。以紧密的行业联系和高毕业生就业率著称，是澳洲八大名校之一。

About the University 学校简介

UNSW is a leading research university with strengths in engineering, business, and technology. Known for its strong industry connections and high graduate employment rate. Part of the Group of Eight.
