Direct application · 3-year bachelor 澳大利亚大学

Australian universities accept direct applications from international students, with admission based on ATAR (domestic), A-Level, IB or Gaokao scores depending on the pathway. Bachelor programs typically run 3 years, with an optional Honours year. The Group of Eight (Go8) represents Australia's leading research-intensive universities.
