About the University 学校简介

Australias first university, with its iconic sandstone buildings. Strong across the board, particularly in medicine, law, business, and veterinary science. Direct-entry professional degrees available at undergraduate level.

Student Life 校园生活

Sydney is Australias largest city with iconic beaches, harbor, and vibrant culture. Beautiful campus near the city center. Excellent work opportunities in finance and tech.

Weather 气候环境

Warm temperate climate. Winter: 10-17°C, mild. Summer: 22-30°C, with occasional heatwaves. Best weather in spring and autumn.
