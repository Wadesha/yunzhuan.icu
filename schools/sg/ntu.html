About the University 学校简介

One of Asias leading technological universities, known for engineering, business, and science. Beautiful modern campus with eco-friendly design. Strong focus on innovation and entrepreneurship.

Student Life 校园生活

Large, modern campus in western Singapore. Beautiful gardens and eco-friendly buildings. Strong hall culture with many activities and competitions. English medium of instruction.
