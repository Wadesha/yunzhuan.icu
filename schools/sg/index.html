Singapore Universities

Direct application · 4-year bachelor 新加坡大学

Singapore's autonomous universities admit international students via direct application, accepting A-Level, IB, Gaokao and Singapore-Cambridge GCE A-Level results. Bachelor programs run 4 years and teaching is in English. NUS and NTU consistently rank among the top universities in Asia and the world.
