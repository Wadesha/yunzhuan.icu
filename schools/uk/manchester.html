Academic Profile 学术概况

综合性大学，3 年制本科，工程、商科、社科都很强，学校规模大。

Manchester offers 3-year undergraduate degrees across 4 faculties. It is the largest single-site university in the UK and consistently ranks in the UK top 10. Manchester has strong programs in engineering, business, social sciences, and life sciences. The university has a very diverse student body.
