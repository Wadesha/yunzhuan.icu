Academic Profile 学术概况

综合性大学，3 年制本科，医学、法学、商科、人文社科都很强。

King's offers 3-year undergraduate degrees across 9 faculties. It has particularly strong programs in medicine, dentistry, law, business, and humanities. With 5 campuses across London, King's is one of the largest universities in the UK and has a very diverse international student body.

Notes for Applicants 申请要点

  • King's is part of the University of London federation.
  • Multiple campuses across London (Strand, Waterloo, Guy's, etc.).
  • Strong medical school with several London teaching hospitals.
  • War Studies department is unique and world-renowned.
  • Less competitive than G5 but still a top Russell Group university.
