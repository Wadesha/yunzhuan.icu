Academic Profile 学术概况

4 年制研究型大学，新闻与传播学院全美顶尖，商科、工科、表演艺术均衡强势。

Northwestern offers a 4-year undergraduate curriculum across 12 schools and colleges. The Medill School of Journalism is nationally renowned, while the Kellogg School of Management offers undergraduate certificates. The McCormick School of Engineering and Bienen School of Music add to the university interdisciplinary strength. The quarter system provides flexibility with 3 terms per year.

Notes for Applicants 申请要点

  • ED is binding: must attend if admitted and withdraw other applications.
  • Northwestern supplemental essays include "Why Northwestern" and short takes.
  • Quarter system means 10-week terms; pace can be intense.
  • Strong reputation in consulting and journalism career placement.
  • Need-aware for international: applying for aid may reduce admission chances.
