Academic Profile 学术概况

4 年制文理学院+研究生院综合大学，强项覆盖人文、社科、自然科学与应用学科。

Harvard College offers a 4-year liberal arts curriculum with 50+ concentrations (majors) across the humanities, social sciences, natural sciences and engineering. General education distribution requirements expose students to broad disciplines, while the concentration provides depth. The house system groups juniors and seniors into residential communities of ~350 students each.

Notes for Applicants 申请要点

  • REA is single-choice early action: no other early applications to private US colleges allowed.
  • SAT / ACT required again for the 2025-26 cycle; check the official policy before applying.
  • Supplemental essays are short but weighted; treat each as a chance to show a distinct facet.
  • Alumni interviews are informational, not screening; prepare thoughtful questions.
  • Need-blind for international students means your aid need does not affect admission.
