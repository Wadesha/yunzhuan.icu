Hong Kong Universities

Direct / JUPAS · 4-year bachelor 香港大学

Hong Kong universities admit mainland students via direct application or Non-JUPAS, while local students use the JUPAS system based on HKDSE. Mainland Gaokao, IB and A-Level results are all accepted. Bachelor programs run 4 years and most teaching is in English, with bilingual support in Cantonese / Mandarin.
香港大学 - 内地生高考/IB/A-Level 均可，本地生走 JUPAS，4 年制本科。
