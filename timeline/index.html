Application Timeline

申请时间线Grade 9-12 · Step-by-step planning

This section provides a grade-by-grade planning timeline for students preparing undergraduate applications to overseas universities. From early exploration in Grade 9 to final submission in Grade 12, each phase outlines academic focus, standardized tests, extracurricular engagement and key deliverables so that families can track progress and stay ahead of deadlines.
按年级规划海外大学本科申请，从探索到递交的全流程。
Exploration Grade 9-10 Foundation phase: explore subject interests, start extracurricular activities and maintain a strong GPA.
探索期 - 学科兴趣探索、课外活动起步、保持好成绩。 Preparation Grade 11 Critical year: standardized testing, finalize a balanced school list and deepen activities.
关键准备期 - 标化考试、确定选校名单、深入活动。 Application Grade 12 Application phase: essay writing, submit applications, interviews and admission decisions.
申请期 - 文书写作、递交申请、面试、录取。
