Exploration Phase (Grade 9-10)

探索期（9-10 年级）Grade 9-10 · Foundation building

1. Overview 阶段概述

Grade 9 and 10 form the foundation of a competitive application. The goal is not to specialize early, but to build strong study habits, explore a wide range of subjects and activities, and start a transcript that shows consistent upward trajectory. 9-10 年级是申请地基：培养学习习惯、广度探索、稳住 GPA。

2. Academic Focus 学术重点

Maintain the highest GPA you can manage; grades from Grade 9 onward appear on your transcript. Choose a rigorous but sustainable course load and plan ahead for honors, AP, IB HL or A-Level tracks in later years. 保持好成绩，9 年级起的成绩都会进入成绩单，合理选课为后续高难度课程做准备。

  • Keep all core subjects (Math, English, Science, Social Studies, World Language) above B.
  • Plan a 4-year course map aligned with target school expectations.
  • Identify strong subjects to potentially pursue at higher level in Grade 11.

3. Standardized Tests 标化准备

No need to rush official SAT/ACT yet. Use this stage to build English proficiency, take the PSAT for early diagnostic feedback, and start low-pressure TOEFL or IELTS practice if you are a non-native English speaker. 不急于考 SAT/ACT，重在英语能力积累；可参加 PSAT 摸底，托福起步。

  • Take PSAT in Grade 10 for diagnostic baseline.
  • Begin TOEFL / IELTS vocabulary and listening practice.
  • Build reading habit in English across non-fiction and literature.

4. Extracurricular Activities 课外活动

Explore broadly rather than committing to a single track. Join 2-3 clubs or teams, try different domains (STEM, humanities, arts, service) and observe which genuinely hold your interest. Sustained participation matters more than collecting titles. 广度探索，参与 2-3 个社团，重在长期投入而非头衔积累。

  • Join clubs aligned with genuine curiosity, not perceived prestige.
  • Volunteer consistently with one organization over time.
  • Keep a simple activity journal to track hours and reflections.

5. Summer Plans 暑期规划

Summers are a chance to deepen an interest without school pressure. Options include academic summer camps, language immersion, structured volunteer programs or a self-directed project. Avoid expensive "trophy" programs that do not match your real interests. 暑期用于深化兴趣：夏令营、语言沉浸、志愿者或自主项目，避免堆砌名头。

  • Grade 9 summer: explore a new subject area or attend a general camp.
  • Grade 10 summer: pursue a more focused program or volunteer commitment.
  • Document takeaways and contacts for future recommendation letters.

6. Checklist 待办清单
