专业介绍

动画专业学习2D/3D动画制作，角色动画、视觉效果、游戏动画、电影特效。课程涵盖绘画、动画原理、3D建模、绑定、渲染、合成等。

Animation students learn 2D/3D animation production, including character animation, visual effects, game animation, and film VFX. Curriculum covers drawing, animation principles, 3D modeling, rigging, rendering, compositing, and more.
