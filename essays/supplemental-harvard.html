Harvard Supplemental Essays

哈佛补充文书题库

2025-26 application cycle · Required + Optional prompts

Overview 题目总览

哈佛补充文书由 5 题必答 + 1 题选答组成，每题 200 字左右，重在补充主文书未呈现的面。

Harvard requires five short supplemental essays (around 200 words each) alongside the Common App personal essay. The prompts are intentionally open so applicants can show different facets of themselves: intellectual life, extracurricular depth, future plans, diversity, and a topic of choice. Treat each as a separate "window" into your profile rather than a continuation of the main essay.

Required Prompts 必答题

  • H1Travel, Living, or Work Experience~200 words
    "Harvard has long recognized the importance of enrolling a diverse student body. How will the life experiences that shape who you are today enable you to contribute to Harvard?"
    策略：用一段具体经历（旅居、跨文化家庭、社区参与）展示你带来的视角；避免空谈"多元化是好东西"。
  • H2Intellectual Experience~200 words
    "Describe a time when you had a positive intellectual experience — a book, idea, conversation, project, or problem that genuinely excited you."
    策略：选一件让你"停不下来想"的事，写出思考过程而非结论；展现好奇心而非成就。
  • H3Extracurricular / Work Activity~200 words
    "Briefly describe any extracurricular activities, employment, or family care responsibilities you've been involved in over the past 2-3 years that you'd like to tell us about."
    策略：避免和活动列表重复；写做这件事时你的决策与成长，而不是再次罗列成就。
  • H4How You Hope to Use Your College Education~200 words
    "How do you hope to use your Harvard education after graduation?"
    策略：具体到一件你真正想解决的问题或方向；不要写"改变世界"，写"在 X 领域做 Y"。
  • H5Roommate Letter~200 words
    "Harvard roommate letter: write a letter to your future roommate that helps them get to know you."
    策略：用日常语气写真实习惯、兴趣或怪癖；这题最忌假装成熟，要保持 17 岁声音。

Optional Prompt 选答题

  • H6Topic of Your ChoiceOptional
    "You may wish to include an additional essay if you feel the college application forms do not provide sufficient opportunity to convey important information about yourself."
    策略：仅在主文书 + 5 题没讲清的关键事实需要补充时使用；不要为了凑数硬写。

Writing Strategy 写作策略

  • Cover different facets (覆盖不同维度)
    5 题加起来应呈现 5 个不同的"你"：思想者、行动者、未来规划者、社群成员、室友。彼此不重叠。
  • Concrete over abstract (具体胜抽象)
    200 字字数紧张，避免抽象价值陈述。用一件具体事件 + 一个反思就足够。
  • No repetition with main essay (不重复主文书)
    主文书讲过的故事在补充文书里不出现；如果必须关联，用不同角度切入。
  • Voice consistency (语气一致)
    5 题语气可以微调（室友题更随意、教育题更认真），但应能看出是同一个作者。

Word Budget 字数分配建议

  • H1 Diversity
    场景引入 ~50 字 + 影响 ~80 字 + 对哈佛的贡献 ~70 字
  • H2 Intellectual
    事件描述 ~70 字 + 思考过程 ~80 字 + 后续影响 ~50 字
  • H3 Activity
    角色与决策 ~100 字 + 反思与成长 ~100 字
  • H4 Future
    具体方向 ~80 字 + 哈佛如何帮你 ~60 字 + 落地动作 ~60 字
  • H5 Roommate
    1 个习惯 / 兴趣 / 怪癖，写透即可，不要拼凑多条
