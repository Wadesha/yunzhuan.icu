Personal Statement

个人陈述

General framework · 500-1000 words

Overview 个人陈述概述

个人陈述是用第一人称讲述"我是谁、我为何申请、我能带来什么"的核心文书。

A Personal Statement is a first-person essay that tells the admissions committee who you are, why you are applying, and what you will contribute. It is used across US, UK and other overseas application systems in slightly different forms. Unlike a transcript or test score, it reveals personality, motivation and fit through a coherent personal narrative. Length typically falls in the 500-1000 word range, and the focus is on showing growth and reflection rather than listing achievements.

Structure Framework 结构框架

开头 / 主体 / 结尾三段式框架，开头抓注意，主体讲故事，结尾点主题。

  • Opening (开头)
    Hook the reader with a specific scene, question or moment; avoid generic quotes. Establish the central theme in 2-3 sentences.
  • Body Paragraphs (主体段落)
    2-4 paragraphs, each anchored by a concrete experience. Show action, reflection and growth; link each story back to the central theme.
  • Transitions (过渡)
    Connect paragraphs by theme or chronology; avoid abrupt jumps between unrelated anecdotes.
  • Conclusion (结尾)
    Reframe the opening theme, look forward to future goals, and briefly state fit with the program. Avoid introducing entirely new content.

Key Elements 关键要素

个人故事、学术兴趣、未来目标、匹配度四个要素需在文中交织呈现。

Personal Story (个人故事)Concrete experiences that reveal character, values and formative moments
Academic Interest (学术兴趣)How an intellectual curiosity developed, with specific evidence of engagement
Future Goals (未来目标)Short-term and long-term aspirations that the program will help achieve
Fit / Match (匹配度)Why this school, major or country fits your trajectory; show genuine research
Reflection (反思)What you learned and how you changed, not merely what you did

Writing Process 写作流程

brainstorm / draft / revise / polish 四阶段，建议预留 4-6 周。

  • 1. Brainstorm (选题与素材)
    List 10-15 experiences, then narrow to 2-3 that share a common theme. Ask: what does each reveal about me?
  • 2. Draft (初稿)
    Write a complete first draft without worrying about word count. Focus on getting the story and reflection down.
  • 3. Revise (修改)
    Restructure for flow and theme; cut unrelated content; strengthen the opening and conclusion. Seek feedback from 2-3 readers.
  • 4. Polish (润色)
    Check word choice, sentence variety, tone and grammar. Confirm it fits the word limit and prompt requirements.

Common Mistakes 常见误区

套模板、空洞抒情、流水账式罗列是三大典型问题。

Template-driven (套模板)Using cliched openings or generic structures that could belong to anyone
Empty emotion (空洞抒情)Big claims ("I am passionate") without concrete evidence to back them up
Resume in prose (流水账)Listing activities chronologically without reflection or theme
Over-ambitious scope (贪多)Trying to cover too many stories; depth beats breadth
Tone mismatch (语气不当)Too formal, too casual, or overly dramatic; aim for genuine and reflective
Ignoring the prompt (偏离题目)Writing a strong essay that does not actually answer the question asked

Tips & Examples Framework 技巧与示例框架

"展示而非告知"是核心技巧，用具体场景代替抽象描述。

  • Show, don't tell (展示而非告知)
    Replace "I am resilient" with a scene that demonstrates resilience through action and dialogue.
  • Specific details (具体细节)
    One vivid, specific image beats a paragraph of generalities. Name the book, the tool, the moment.
  • Voice and authenticity (真实声音)
    Write in your own voice; admissions officers can detect writing that sounds like a parent or AI.
  • Reflective framing (反思框架)
    For each experience, include: what happened, what you did, what you learned, how it shaped you.
  • Example scaffold (示例骨架)
    Opening scene → central conflict or question → action taken → realization → forward-looking conclusion. Use this as a flexible template, not a rigid formula.
