Supplemental Essays

补充文书

School-specific · varies

Overview 补充文书概述

补充文书是各校单独要求的短文书，用于评估申请人与该校的具体匹配度。

Supplemental Essays are school-specific writing requirements that sit alongside the Common App main essay. Each college sets its own prompts, word limits and number of essays, ranging from a single 50-word short answer to multiple 250-650 word pieces. They let a school assess fit: why this campus, why this major, what you would contribute. Strong supplements require genuine research into each institution and should never be reused verbatim across schools.

Common Types 常见类型

五大常见类型：Why School / Why Major / Activity / Community / Diversity。

Why School (为何选校)Why this specific college fits your goals; requires named programs, courses, clubs
Why Major (为何选专业)How your academic interest developed and how the school will advance it
Activity Essay (活动文书)A meaningful extracurricular, often 150 words; show impact and reflection
Community Essay (社区文书)Your role in a family, school or local community and what you learned
Diversity Essay (多样性文书)What perspective or background you would add to the campus community
Short Answer (短回答)Very brief (50-200 words) prompts testing voice, curiosity or creativity

Why School Essays Why School 写作框架

核心是具体到校的研究：列出每个学校独有的资源，避免空泛套话。

  • Research checklist (研究要点)
    Identify 2-3 specific courses, a professor or lab, a student club, a research centre and a distinctive program. Note names, not categories.
  • Avoid generic praise (避免空泛赞美)
    Cut "great reputation" or "beautiful campus"; every school hears this. Replace with concrete fit arguments.
  • Connect to your goals (连接个人目标)
    For each resource you name, explain how it advances a specific academic or career goal of yours.
  • Structure scaffold (结构骨架)
    Opening: your goal or interest → 2-3 school-specific resources that fit → how you would engage → closing on contribution.
  • The swap test (替换测试)
    If you can swap the school name and the essay still works, it is too generic.

Why Major Essays Why Major 写作框架

讲清"兴趣如何产生—如何发展—未来如何延伸"的脉络。

  • Origin of interest (兴趣起源)
    A specific moment, book, project or question that sparked curiosity; avoid "I have always loved..."
  • Development (兴趣发展)
    Concrete steps you took: courses, independent reading, projects, competitions, research or internships.
  • Future direction (未来方向)
    A specific question or problem you want to explore, ideally one the school's program addresses.
  • Evidence over declaration (证据胜过宣称)
    "I am passionate about economics" is weak; "I built a demand model for my school canteen" is strong.
  • School fit (结合学校)
    Where relevant, tie the major interest to specific school resources, but keep the focus on your intellectual journey.

Short Answer Tips 短回答技巧

短回答字数有限，每一句都要有信息量，避免铺垫。

Cut the throat-clearing (去掉铺垫)Open with the answer or the action, not "I believe that..." or "In my opinion..."
One idea per answer (一题一意)Do not cram two stories into 100 words; depth in a narrow frame reads better.
Voice matters (语气更重要)In very short prompts, personality and phrasing carry more weight than content.
Use the word count (用足字数)A 50-word limit is a ceiling, but aim close to it; 20 words reads as under-invested.
Specific nouns (具体名词)Name the book, the tool, the city; specifics signal authenticity in tight space.
Revise ruthlessly (狠心删改)Write long, then cut. Every word in a short answer must earn its place.

School-by-school Guide 分校指南框架

先列代表性学校的文书要求框架，后续可逐校扩展。

  • Harvard (哈佛大学)
    Open-topic supplemental; room for intellectual or personal exploration. No fixed prompts; treat as a second personal essay.
  • Stanford (斯坦福大学)
    Known short-answer prompts ("What matters most to you and why") plus a few 250-word essays. Tests voice and reflection.
  • Yale (耶鲁大学)
    Mix of short answers (35-200 words) on academic interest, community and a "Why Yale" piece requiring specific campus resources.
  • MIT (麻省理工)
    Several short prompts (100-250 words) on your world, department of interest, community contribution and what you do for fun.
  • UPenn (宾夕法尼亚大学)
    School-specific prompts (Wharton, Engineering, Nursing, Arts & Sciences) plus a "Thank You" note and a joint-degree essay.
  • How to extend (扩展方法)
    For each school, capture: number of prompts, word limits, distinctive question types, and required research depth.
