How to Use This Library 使用说明

范文仅供学习结构和思路参考，绝对不能抄袭或套用。录取委员会使用反剽窃软件，一经发现直接拒录。

  • 读结构，不读内容
    学习范文如何开头、如何展开故事、如何结尾、如何把经历与成长联系起来。
  • 找灵感，不复制情节
    范文的故事是别人的，你的文书必须讲你自己的真实经历。用范文启发你想起自己的故事。
  • 分析写作技巧
    注意作者如何用细节代替形容词、如何制造画面感、如何"Show, don't tell"。
  • 对比不同风格
    有的文书幽默、有的深情、有的学术感强，找到适合你个性的写作风格。

Personal Statement Samples 主文书范例

Common App 主文书（650 字），按主题分类，每篇附简要分析。

  • The Kitchen Chemist
    Personal Growth Common App Prompt 1 620 words Admitted: Chemistry major
    "I was twelve when I accidentally turned my mother's kitchen into a mini-laboratory. What started as a simple baking soda volcano experiment quickly evolved into a weekend ritual: measuring precise proportions of vinegar and baking soda, timing the eruption duration, and even adding food coloring to test if it affected the reaction height. My mother never quite forgave me for the red dye stain on her marble countertop, but that stain marked the beginning of something far more important — my obsession with understanding why things happen..."
  • Crossing the Language Barrier
    Challenge Common App Prompt 2 638 words Admitted: Undeclared
    "On my first day of seventh grade in America, I raised my hand when the teacher asked who was new. 'Where are you from?' she asked. I opened my mouth to say 'Shanghai,' but the word got stuck somewhere between my throat and my tongue. Twenty-six letters rearranged themselves in my head, refusing to form anything recognizable. The classroom grew quiet. Thirty pairs of eyes stared at me. I sat down, face burning, and didn't speak again in class for three months..."
  • The Night I Became a Storyteller
    Creativity Common App Prompt 1 605 words Admitted: English / Film
    "My grandmother had dementia, but every night before bed, she'd ask me to tell her a story. At first, I recycled the fairy tales my mother used to read — Cinderella, Little Red Riding Hood, Jack and the Beanstalk. But after a week, she'd heard them all. Actually, she'd heard them all twice, because she forgot the first time. That's when I started making things up. Each night, a new story. Each night, characters who'd never existed before sprang to life in the dim lamplight of her bedroom..."
  • Why I Code at 3 AM
    Why Major Common App Prompt 6 642 words Admitted: Computer Science
    "It was 3:17 AM when the code finally compiled. I'd been staring at the same 12 lines of Python for three hours, wondering why my sorting algorithm kept returning an empty list. And then I saw it — a single missing colon. That's the thing about programming: the difference between a working program and a broken one can be invisible. It's not about the big ideas, it's about the tiny details. That's what I love about it..."
  • The Garbage Collector's Daughter
    Diversity Common App Prompt 1 610 words Admitted: Environmental Science
    "Every Saturday morning, I rode with my dad in his garbage truck. Most kids my age were at soccer practice or sleepovers, but I was sitting next to my dad, watching him lift bins and operate the mechanical arm. The smell was terrible, especially in summer. But what I remember most isn't the smell — it's the conversations we had. My dad only finished eighth grade, but he could talk for hours about how our town's recycling system was broken..."
  • Learning to Lose
    Challenge Common App Prompt 5 595 words Admitted: Business
    "I lost 47 debate competitions in a row. Not just lost — got destroyed. My opponents would make points I'd never considered, and I'd stand there, mouth open, like a goldfish on a dock. My debate coach kept telling me I had potential, but after the 47th loss, I was ready to quit. That's when my partner said something that changed everything: 'You keep trying to win. Stop trying to win and start trying to learn.'"

Supplemental Essay Samples 补充文书范例

各类常见补充文书题目范例，含"为什么选我们学校"、"为什么选这个专业"等。

  • Why Columbia: From Subway Maps to Core Curriculum
    Why School Columbia University 298 words
    "I've collected subway maps since I was ten. Not the pretty, touristy ones — the folded, worn-out kind you find on park benches. What fascinates me about subway maps is how they connect seemingly unrelated places through an invisible network. Columbia's Core Curriculum feels like that subway map to me: Literature Humanities, Contemporary Civilization, Art Humanities — these aren't just separate classes, they're stations on a line that connects everything I'm curious about..."
  • Why MIT: Building Things That Matter
    Why School Why Major MIT 315 words
    "In eighth grade, I built a birdhouse. It was terrible. The roof leaked, the walls were crooked, and the entrance hole was too big — squirrels kept raiding it. But I learned something important that day: building things is hard, but it's also the most satisfying thing in the world. MIT is the only place I've found where people are as excited about bad birdhouses (and better versions of them) as I am..."
  • Stanford Short Answer: What Matters to You?
    Personal Stanford 147 words
    "What matters to me is making my grandmother laugh. She had a stroke three years ago and can't speak much anymore, but she still laughs — this loud, snorting laugh that fills the whole room. I've become a collector of things that make her laugh: bad puns, silly dances, stories about my day at school. Some days I'm successful, some days I'm not. But I keep trying, because that laugh is the best sound in the world..."
  • UChicago Extended Essay: The Physics of Pancakes
    Creative UChicago 602 words
    "I've made 1,247 pancakes in my life. I know because I count them. Not because I'm weird (okay, maybe a little weird), but because each pancake is a physics experiment. The perfect pancake requires precise control of temperature, viscosity, surface tension, and timing. Too much batter and it's too thick; too little and it's a crepe. Flip too early and it breaks; flip too late and it burns. Pancakes are a metaphor for life, except you can eat the failed experiments..."

Analysis Framework 范文分析方法

读范文时用这个框架拆解，学习效果翻倍。

  • Hook (开头钩子)
    第一句话是什么？为什么能吸引你继续读？是场景、对话、悬念、还是反差？
  • Story Arc (故事弧线)
    开头是什么状态？中间发生了什么冲突或转折？结尾是什么状态？有什么成长变化？
  • Show, Don't Tell (展示而非陈述)
    作者用了哪些具体细节、动作、对话来展示性格，而不是直接说"我很坚强/聪明"？
  • Reflection (反思部分)
    作者从经历中学到了什么？这个经历如何影响了今天的你？和申请的专业/学校有什么联系？
  • Voice (语气/声音)
    这篇文书听起来像什么样的人？幽默的？严肃的？真诚的？你能感受到作者的个性吗？
