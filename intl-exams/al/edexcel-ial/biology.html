A-Level Biology (Edexcel IAL)

A-Level 生物（Edexcel IAL）

Edexcel IAL WBI11-15

Course Overview 课程介绍

Edexcel IAL 生物采用模块化按 Unit 考试，可重考单模块，医学、生命科学与生物医学申请核心科目。

Edexcel International A-Level Biology is a modular course assessed unit by unit across five written units. Students sit each Unit exam separately and may retake individual units. Content spans molecules and genes, cells and biodiversity, energy and immunity, respiration and gene technology, with practical skills examined in Unit 3. A core subject for medicine, dentistry, veterinary science, biomedical sciences and biology.

Key Facts 基本信息

Grading (评分)A* - E for full A-Level; a - e for AS

Practical (实验)Unit 3 Practical Skills in Biology I (written practical paper)

AS Syllabus AS 阶段章节（Units 1-3）

  • Unit 1Molecules
    Water, carbohydrates, lipids, proteins and nucleic acids (DNA and RNA), enzyme action
  • Unit 1Diet and Transport
    Diet and digestion, absorption, the heart and blood vessels, blood, transport in plants (xylem and phloem)
  • Unit 1Genes
    DNA replication, protein synthesis, genetics, inheritance, monohybrid and dihybrid crosses, mutations
  • Unit 2Cells and Development
    Cell structure, membranes and transport, cell division (mitosis and meiosis), reproduction, growth, differentiation and stem cells
  • Unit 2Biodiversity and Conservation
    Classification, biodiversity, measuring species diversity, conservation of ecosystems
  • Unit 3Practical Skills in Biology I
    Experimental techniques, microscopy, data analysis, statistical tests and evaluation (written practical paper)

A2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节（Units 4-5）

  • Unit 4Energy and Environment
    Photosynthesis, ecosystems, energy flow, nutrient cycles (carbon, nitrogen), human impact on the environment
  • Unit 4Microbiology and Immunity
    Bacteria and viruses, microbes in biotechnology, the immune system, vaccination, infectious disease
  • Unit 5Respiration
    Cellular respiration, ATP, glycolysis, Krebs cycle, electron transport chain, aerobic and anaerobic respiration
  • Unit 5Internal Environment and Coordination
    Homeostasis, excretion and the kidney, nervous system, brain, receptors and muscle
  • Unit 5Gene Technology
    Genetic engineering, recombinant DNA technology, biotechnology applications and ethical issues

Unit 4 (WBI14)Energy, Environment, Microbiology and Immunity · 1h45m · 25% of IAL

Modular FeatureModular structure, unit-by-unit assessment, single unit retake allowed

Suitable Majors & Pathway 适合专业与升学路径

适合医学、生物医学、生命科学、药学与兽医专业，是英国医学与生命科学专业的重要必选科目。

Biology is essential for medicine, dentistry, veterinary science, biomedical sciences, biochemistry, pharmacy and biological sciences. Combined with Chemistry it forms the standard subject combination for medical school applications. The modular Edexcel IAL structure lets students retake individual units to strengthen their final grade before university deadlines.
