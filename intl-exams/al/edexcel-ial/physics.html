A-Level Physics (Edexcel IAL)

A-Level 物理（Edexcel IAL）

Edexcel IAL WPH11-15

Course Overview 课程介绍

Edexcel IAL 物理采用模块化按 Unit 考试，可重考单模块，理工科与医学申请核心科目。

Edexcel International A-Level Physics is a modular course assessed unit by unit across five written units. Students sit each Unit exam separately and may retake individual units to improve their overall grade. Content spans mechanics and materials, waves and electricity, fields and further mechanics, and thermodynamics, radiation, oscillations and cosmology, with practical skills examined in Unit 3. A core subject for engineering, physics, computer science and medicine applicants.

AS Syllabus AS 阶段章节（Units 1-3）

  • Unit 1Mechanics
    Motion (kinematics, equations of motion), forces, Newton's laws, momentum and impulse, work, energy and power
  • Unit 1Materials
    Springs and Hooke's law, Young's modulus, elastic and plastic behaviour, fluid flow, viscosity, laminar and turbulent flow
  • Unit 2Waves
    Progressive waves, transverse and longitudinal waves, superposition, stationary waves, refraction, diffraction, interference, polarisation
  • Unit 2Electricity
    Current and voltage, resistance, resistivity, series and parallel circuits, EMF and internal resistance, Kirchhoff's laws, electrical power
  • Unit 3Practical Skills in Physics I
    Experimental methods, data collection and analysis, uncertainty and error propagation, graphing and evaluation (written practical paper)

A2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节（Units 4-5）

  • Unit 4Further Mechanics
    Circular motion, angular velocity, centripetal acceleration and force, momentum in two dimensions
  • Unit 4Gravitational and Electric Fields
    Newton's law of gravitation, gravitational field strength and potential, Coulomb's law, electric field strength and potential, uniform fields
  • Unit 4Capacitance
    Capacitors and capacitance, energy stored, charging and discharging capacitors, dielectrics
  • Unit 4Magnetic Fields and Electromagnetic Induction
    Magnetic force on charges and conductors, magnetic flux, Faraday's law, Lenz's law, transformers and generators
  • Unit 5Thermodynamics
    Thermal energy transfer, ideal gases, specific heat capacity, internal energy, Boyle's and Charles's laws
  • Unit 5Nuclear Radiation
    Radioactive decay, half-life, nuclear fission and fusion, mass-energy equivalence, radiation safety
  • Unit 5Oscillations
    Simple harmonic motion, damping, resonance, forced oscillations, pendulum and mass-spring systems
  • Unit 5Astrophysics and Cosmology
    Stars and stellar evolution, Doppler effect, Hubble's law, the Big Bang model, cosmology and the universe

Suitable Majors & Pathway 适合专业与升学路径

适合工程、物理、计算机、医学等理工科专业，是英国顶尖大学理工科录取的首选科目之一。

Physics is a required or strongly preferred subject for engineering (mechanical, electrical, aerospace, civil), physics, astrophysics, computer science, and many medicine and dentistry programmes. Combined with Mathematics and Further Mathematics, it opens pathways to top universities in the UK, Hong Kong, Singapore and beyond. The modular structure lets students strengthen weak units through retakes before the final A-Level award.
