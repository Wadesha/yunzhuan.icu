A-Level Further Mathematics (Edexcel IAL)

A-Level 进阶数学（Edexcel IAL）

Edexcel IAL WFM01-04

Course Overview 课程介绍

Edexcel IAL 进阶数学采用模块化按 Unit 考试，FP1/FP2 加应用单元，顶尖理工与数学专业核心加分科目。

Edexcel International A-Level Further Mathematics is a modular course extending A-Level Mathematics through Further Pure units (FP1, FP2 and FP3) combined with advanced applied units in Mechanics, Statistics and Decision Mathematics. Students sit each unit separately and may retake individual units. Content spans complex numbers, matrices, proof, further calculus, hyperbolic functions, differential equations and polar coordinates. Designed for students targeting mathematics, engineering, physics and computer science at top universities.

Applied Options (应用单元)M2/M3 Mechanics, S2/S3 Statistics, D2 Decision, or FP3 Further Pure

AS Syllabus AS 阶段章节（FP1）

  • FP1Complex Numbers
    Operations with complex numbers, Argand diagrams, modulus and argument, polar form
  • FP1Numerical Solution of Equations
    Interval bisection, linear interpolation, Newton-Raphson method, iteration
  • FP1Coordinate Systems
    Parabola, hyperbola, rectangular hyperbola, tangents and normals, parametric forms
  • FP1Matrices
    Matrix operations, determinants, inverses, 2D linear transformations, simultaneous equations
  • FP1Series and Proof
    Summation of series, method of differences, mathematical induction
  • FP1Vectors
    Three-dimensional vectors, scalar and vector products, equations of lines

A2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节（FP2 / FP3 + applied）

  • FP2Inequalities and Series
    Inequalities involving modulus, Maclaurin and Taylor series, approximations
  • FP2Complex Numbers (Advanced)
    De Moivre's theorem, roots of unity, exponential form, loci in the Argand diagram
  • FP2Matrices and Proof
    3x3 matrices, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, proof by induction and contradiction
  • FP2Further Calculus and Hyperbolic Functions
    Reduction formulae, arc length and surface area, hyperbolic functions and their inverses
  • FP2Differential Equations and Polar Coordinates
    First and second order differential equations, integrating factor, polar curves and area
  • FP3 / AppliedFurther Vectors and Applied Options
    Vector planes and intersections; plus one applied unit from M2/M3, S2/S3 or D2

Exam Structure (Edexcel IAL WFM01-04) 考试结构

FP1 (WFM01)Further Pure Mathematics 1 · 1h30m · AS Further Maths unit
FP2 (WFM02)Further Pure Mathematics 2 · 1h30m · A2 unit
FP3 (WFM03)Further Pure Mathematics 3 · 1h30m · A2 unit
Applied (WFM04)One applied unit from M2/M3, S2/S3 or D2 · 1h30m · A2 unit
Modular FeatureModular structure, unit-by-unit assessment, single unit retake allowed

Suitable Majors & Pathway 适合专业与升学路径

适合数学、理论物理、计算机、工程等顶尖理工专业，是牛津剑桥与 G5 数学相关专业的强烈推荐科目。

Further Mathematics is strongly preferred (and sometimes required) for mathematics, theoretical physics, computer science and engineering at top universities such as Oxford, Cambridge, Imperial, NUS and HKU. It develops the rigour and abstraction needed for STEM-intensive degrees. The modular Edexcel IAL format lets students retake individual FP or applied units to optimise their final grade.
