A-Level 物理（OCR）

OCR H156 (AS) / H556 (A-Level)

Course Overview 课程介绍

OCR A-Level 物理 A，线性结构，6 个模块覆盖力学、电磁、量子与天体物理，重视实验技能。

OCR A-Level Physics A (H556) is a linear two-year course assessed by three written papers at the end of Year 13, plus a teacher-assessed practical endorsement. OCR A-Level has moved to a linear structure since 2015, so all exams are taken at the end of the course. It is built around six modules spanning mechanics, electricity, waves, quantum physics, thermal physics, fields, astrophysics, particles and medical physics, with strong emphasis on practical skills and mathematical methods. A core subject for engineering, physics, CS and medicine applicants.

AS / Year 1 Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • ASModule 1: Development of Practical Skills in Physics
    Practical skills assessed in a written examination and in the practical endorsement; planning, implementing, analysis and evaluation
  • ASModule 2: Foundations in Physics
    Physical quantities and units, making measurements and analysing data, scalars and vectors, nature of quantities
  • ASModule 3: Forces and Motion
    Motion, forces in action, work energy and power, materials, Newton's laws of motion and momentum
  • ASModule 4: Electrons, Waves and Photons
    Charge and current, energy power and resistance, electrical circuits, waves, quantum physics

A2 / Year 2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A2Module 5: Newtonian World and Astrophysics
    Thermal physics, circular motion, oscillations, gravitational fields, astrophysics and cosmology
  • A2Module 6: Particles and Medical Physics
    Capacitors, electric fields, electromagnetism, nuclear and particle physics, medical imaging

Suitable Majors & Pathway 适用专业与衔接

理工科申请核心科目，工程、物理、计算机与医学的常见必修或推荐科目。

A-Level Physics is a key subject for engineering (mechanical, electrical, aerospace, civil), physics, computer science, mathematics, and medicine. Most UK STEM programmes recommend or require A-Level Physics. It builds strong foundations in mechanics, fields and quantitative problem-solving, and pairs naturally with A-Level Mathematics and Further Mathematics for competitive engineering and physical science degrees.
