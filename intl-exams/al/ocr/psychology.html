A-Level Psychology (OCR)

A-Level 心理学（OCR）

OCR H167 (AS) / H567 (A-Level)

Course Overview 课程介绍

OCR A-Level 心理学，线性结构，含五大核心领域与应用心理学，强调研究方法与实证分析。

OCR A-Level Psychology (H567) is a linear two-year course assessed by three written papers at the end of Year 13. OCR A-Level has moved to a linear structure since 2015, so all exams are taken at the end of the course. The specification is built around five core psychological domains (cognitive, social, developmental, biological and individual differences) plus two applied areas (clinical psychology and one option from criminal, child, health or sport), with strong emphasis on research methods, statistical analysis and the evaluation of studies. A useful subject for psychology, neuroscience, medicine, sociology, education and criminology applicants.

AS / Year 1 Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • ASComponent 01: Research Methods
    Planning, conducting, analysing and reporting research; experiments, observations, self-reports, correlations; descriptive and inferential statistics; sampling and ethics
  • ASComponent 02: Psychological Themes Through Core Studies
    Five core areas (social, cognitive, developmental, biological, individual differences) explored through 20 landmark studies, e.g. Milgram, Loftus and Palmer, Bandura, Sperry, Freud

A2 / Year 2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A2Component 01: Research Methods (Advanced)
    Extended research methods, inferential statistics (Mann-Whitney, Wilcoxon, Spearman's rho, chi-square), types of data, validity and reliability, report writing
  • A2Component 02: Psychological Themes Through Core Studies (Advanced)
    The 20 core studies revisited with deeper evaluation; perspectives and debates (nature/nurture, free will/determinism, holism/reductionism, usefulness, ethnocentrism)
  • A2Component 03: Applied Psychology
    Compulsory Clinical Psychology (mental health, classifications, therapies) plus one option: Criminal Psychology, Child Psychology, Health Psychology or Sport and Exercise Psychology

Suitable Majors & Pathway 适用专业与衔接

心理学、神经科学、医学、社会学、教育与犯罪学的实用科目，强调研究方法与批判思维。

A-Level Psychology is a useful subject for psychology, neuroscience, medicine, biomedical sciences, sociology, criminology, education, nursing and sports science. Most UK psychology programmes (BPS accredited) welcome A-Level Psychology as it builds a strong foundation in research methods, statistical reasoning and the evaluation of evidence. It develops critical thinking, scientific literacy and written argumentation, and pairs naturally with A-Level Biology, Mathematics, Sociology or Chemistry for applicants to competitive psychology and neuroscience degrees.
