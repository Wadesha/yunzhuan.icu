A-Level Geography (OCR)

A-Level 地理（OCR）

OCR H081 (AS) / H481 (A-Level)

Course Overview 课程介绍

OCR A-Level 地理，线性结构，含自然与人文地理、地理议题研究与独立调查，强调实地考察。

OCR A-Level Geography (H481) is a linear two-year course assessed by three written papers and one independent investigation at the end of Year 13. OCR A-Level has moved to a linear structure since 2015, so all exams are taken at the end of the course. The specification balances physical geography (landscape systems, Earth's life support systems), human geography (changing spaces, global connections) and geographical debates, plus an independently designed fieldwork investigation. A facilitating subject for geography, environmental science, urban planning, international development and earth science applicants.

AS / Year 1 Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • ASComponent 01: Landscape and Place
    Landscape systems (coasts, drylands or glaciers) and Changing spaces; making places — the meaning and shaping of places, plus fieldwork in human and physical geography
  • ASComponent 02: Geography debates and fieldwork
    Two geographical debates (e.g. climate change, disease, hazardous earth, migration, human rights) and a fieldwork-based skills assessment

A2 / Year 2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A2Component 01: Physical Systems (Advanced)
    Landscape systems (coastal, dryland or glacial) and Earth's life support systems (the carbon cycle, the water cycle)
  • A2Component 02: Human Interactions (Advanced)
    Changing spaces; making places and global connections (global migration, human rights, power and borders, trade and global systems)
  • A2Component 03: Geographical Debates
    Two debates chosen from: Climate Change, Disease Dilemmas, Exploring Oceans, Future of Food, Hazardous Earth, Human Rights, Migration — examined with resource booklet
  • A2Component 04: Independent Investigation (NEA)
    An independently designed fieldwork investigation of 3000-4000 words on a geography question of the candidate's choice, written up as a report

Suitable Majors & Pathway 适用专业与衔接

地理、环境科学、城市规划与国际发展的核心科目，强调空间分析与实地调查。

A-Level Geography is a facilitating subject listed by Russell Group universities and is highly valued for geography, environmental science, urban planning, international development, earth science, geology, ecology, surveying, sustainability and tourism management. It develops spatial analysis, GIS literacy, fieldwork skills, statistical reasoning and structured essay writing, and pairs naturally with A-Level Biology, Economics, Mathematics or Politics for applicants to competitive geography, environmental and social science degrees.
