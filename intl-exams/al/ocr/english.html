A-Level English Literature (OCR)

A-Level 英语文学（OCR）

OCR H072 (AS) / H472 (A-Level)

Course Overview 课程介绍

OCR A-Level 英语文学，线性结构，覆盖莎士比亚、比较与语境文本，含课程作业。

OCR A-Level English Literature (H472) is a linear two-year course assessed by two written papers and one coursework unit at the end of Year 13. OCR A-Level has moved to a linear structure since 2015, so all exams are taken at the end of the course. The specification centres on close reading, genre study and the comparison of texts across periods and cultures, spanning Shakespeare, pre-1900 poetry and prose, post-1900 literature and a free-choice coursework topic. A facilitating subject for English, law, journalism, history, PPE and the humanities.

AS / Year 1 Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • ASComponent 01: Shakespeare and the Pre- and Post-1900 Poetry
    A Shakespeare play (e.g. Hamlet, The Tempest) and a paired poetry anthology spanning pre-1900 and post-1900 poets; close reading and comparative analysis
  • ASComponent 02: Drama and Prose Pre-1900
    One pre-1900 drama text and one pre-1900 prose text; exploration of form, genre, context and literary criticism

A2 / Year 2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A2Component 01: Drama and Poetry Pre-1900
    Shakespeare (e.g. Twelfth Night, The Winter's Tale), pre-1900 drama (e.g. Webster, Marlowe) and pre-1900 poetry (e.g. Chaucer, Milton, Christina Rossetti); comparative and contextual analysis
  • A2Component 02: Comparative and Contextual Study
    One topic chosen from American Literature 1880-1940, The Gothic, Dystopia, The Immigrant Experience, Women in Literature; two set texts plus wider reading
  • A2Component 03: Literature Post-1900 (Coursework)
    Close reading of a post-1900 text and a re-creative writing piece with commentary, 3000 words total; free choice of post-1900 texts

Suitable Majors & Pathway 适用专业与衔接

人文学科核心科目，英语、法律、新闻、历史与 PPE 的常见必修或推荐科目。

A-Level English Literature is a facilitating subject listed by Russell Group universities and is highly valued for English, law, journalism, history, PPE, classics, philosophy, education and the humanities. It develops close reading, critical analysis, argumentation and academic writing, and pairs naturally with A-Level History, Politics, Modern Languages or Classics for applicants to competitive humanities and law degrees.
