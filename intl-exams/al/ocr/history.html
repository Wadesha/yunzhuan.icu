A-Level 历史（OCR）

OCR H105 (AS) / H505 (A-Level)

Course Overview 课程介绍

OCR A-Level 历史，线性结构，含英国史、世界史与课题研究，强调史料分析与历史论证。

OCR A-Level History (H505) is a linear two-year course assessed by three written papers and one coursework unit at the end of Year 13. OCR A-Level has moved to a linear structure since 2015, so all exams are taken at the end of the course. The specification combines a British period study, a non-British period study, a thematic study over 100+ years and a historical interpretation topic, plus a coursework essay on a topic of the candidate's choice. A facilitating subject for history, law, PPE, international relations, journalism and the humanities.

AS / Year 1 Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • ASUnit Y1: British Period Study and Enquiry
    A British period such as Alfred the Great, England 1485-1558 (Early Tudors), or England 1603-1702 (Stuarts); combined with a source-based enquiry
  • ASUnit Y2: Non-British Period Study
    A non-British period such as France 1494-1610, The USA 1803-1890, or International Relations 1890-1941; essay-based assessment

A2 / Year 2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A2Unit Y1: British Period Study and Enquiry (Advanced)
    Extended British period study plus a source-based enquiry; e.g. The Early Stuarts and the Civil War 1603-1660, Britain 1930-1997
  • A2Unit Y2: Non-British Period Study (Advanced)
    Extended non-British period study; e.g. The French Revolution and Napoleon 1774-1815, The Cold War in Europe 1941-1995, Dictatorships in Germany 1919-1963
  • A2Unit Y3: Thematic Study and Historical Interpretations
    A 100+ year thematic study with linked historiography; e.g. Civil Rights in the USA 1865-1992, The Making of Russia 1855-1991, Rebellion and Disorder under the Tudors 1485-1603
  • A2Unit Y100: Topic Based Essay (Coursework)
    An independently researched essay of 3000-4000 words on a topic of the candidate's choice, drawing on primary and secondary sources

Suitable Majors & Pathway 适用专业与衔接

人文学科核心科目，历史、法律、PPE、国际关系与新闻的常见必修或推荐科目。

A-Level History is a facilitating subject listed by Russell Group universities and is highly valued for history, law, PPE (Philosophy, Politics and Economics), international relations, journalism, archaeology, classics and politics. It develops source analysis, critical reasoning, essay writing and historiographical understanding, and pairs naturally with A-Level English Literature, Politics, Economics or Modern Languages for applicants to competitive humanities, law and social science degrees.
