A-Level Chemistry (OCR)

A-Level 化学（OCR）

OCR H032 (AS) / H432 (A-Level)

Course Overview 课程介绍

OCR A-Level 化学 A，线性结构，6 个模块涵盖无机、有机、物理化学与过渡元素。

OCR A-Level Chemistry A (H432) is a linear two-year course assessed by three written papers at the end of Year 13, plus a teacher-assessed practical endorsement. OCR A-Level has moved to a linear structure since 2015, so all exams are taken at the end of the course. It is organised into six modules spanning foundations, the periodic table and energy, core organic chemistry, physical chemistry, the transition elements, and organic chemistry and analysis. A core subject for chemistry, medicine, chemical engineering, pharmacy and biosciences applicants.

AS / Year 1 Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • ASModule 1: Development of Practical Skills in Chemistry
    Practical skills assessed in a written examination and in the practical endorsement; planning, implementing, analysis and evaluation
  • ASModule 2: Foundations in Chemistry
    Atoms, compounds, molecules and equations, amount of substance, acid-base and redox reactions, bonding and structure
  • ASModule 3: Periodic Table and Energy
    The periodic table and periodicity, group 2 and group 7, qualitative analysis, enthalpy changes, rates and equilibrium
  • ASModule 4: Core Organic Chemistry
    Basic concepts, hydrocarbons, alcohols and haloalkanes, organic synthesis, analytical techniques (IR and MS)

A2 / Year 2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A2Module 5: Physical Chemistry and the Transition Elements
    Rates and equilibrium, pH and buffers, enthalpy, entropy and free energy, redox and electrode potentials, transition elements
  • A2Module 6: Organic Chemistry and Analysis
    Aromatic compounds, carbonyls, carboxylic acids and esters, amines, amino acids and polymers, organic synthesis, chromatography and NMR spectroscopy

Suitable Majors & Pathway 适用专业与衔接

化学、医学、药学、化工与生命科学的核心科目，实验技能与分析能力并重。

A-Level Chemistry is essential for chemistry, medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, chemical engineering, veterinary science, biomedical sciences and many bioscience degrees. It is frequently a required subject for medical school applications in the UK. The course develops strong analytical and laboratory skills, quantitative reasoning, and an understanding of molecular processes that underpin both physical and life sciences.
