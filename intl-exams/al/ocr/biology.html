A-Level 生物（OCR）

OCR H020 (AS) / H420 (A-Level)

Course Overview 课程介绍

OCR A-Level 生物 A，线性结构，6 个模块涵盖细胞、遗传、生态与稳态。

OCR A-Level Biology A (H420) is a linear two-year course assessed by three written papers at the end of Year 13, plus a teacher-assessed practical endorsement. OCR A-Level has moved to a linear structure since 2015, so all exams are taken at the end of the course. It is organised into six modules spanning foundations, exchange and transport, biodiversity and disease, communication and homeostasis, energy, and genetics, evolution and ecosystems. A core subject for medicine, biosciences, biomedical sciences and psychology applicants.

AS / Year 1 Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • ASModule 1: Development of Practical Skills in Biology
    Practical skills assessed in a written examination and in the practical endorsement; planning, implementing, analysis and evaluation
  • ASModule 2: Foundations in Biology
    Cell structure, biological molecules, nucleotides and nucleic acids, enzymes, biological membranes, cell division and cellular organisation
  • ASModule 3: Exchange and Transport
    Exchange surfaces, ventilation, transport in animals (blood and the heart), transport in plants (xylem and phloem)
  • ASModule 4: Biodiversity, Evolution and Disease
    Communicable diseases, disease prevention and the immune system, biodiversity, classification and evolution

A2 / Year 2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A2Module 5: Communication, Homeostasis and Energy
    Communication and homeostasis, excretion, neuronal and hormonal communication, plant responses, photosynthesis, respiration
  • A2Module 6: Genetics, Evolution and Ecosystems
    Cellular control, patterns of inheritance, manipulating genomes, cloning and biotechnology, ecosystems, populations and sustainability

Suitable Majors & Pathway 适用专业与衔接

医学、生物科学、生物医药与心理学的核心科目，强调实验设计与数据分析。

A-Level Biology is essential for medicine, dentistry, veterinary science, biomedical sciences, biosciences, and psychology. It is frequently a required subject for medical school applications in the UK. The course develops strong practical and analytical skills, an understanding of biological systems from molecular to ecosystem level, and data handling abilities valued across the life and health sciences.
