A-Level Psychology

A-Level 心理学

CAIE 9990

Course Overview 课程介绍

CAIE 高级心理学，涵盖生物、认知、社会、学习四大取向，研究方法贯穿全程。

A-Level Psychology is a UK advanced secondary course split into AS (Year 1) and A2 (Year 2). It covers biological, cognitive, social and learning approaches, with research methods running throughout. A2 explores applied areas such as abnormality, consumer, health and organisational psychology. It emphasises empirical research, critical evaluation and psychological methodology. A core subject for psychology, neuroscience, education, social work, market research and HR.

AS Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • AS1. Research Methodology
    Experimental design, observation/survey/case study, sampling, ethics
  • AS2. Data Analysis and Statistics
    Descriptive statistics, inferential statistics, significance testing, correlation analysis
  • AS3. The Biological Approach
    Neurons and neurotransmitters, brain structure and function, endocrine system, evolution
  • AS4. The Cognitive Approach
    Memory models, short-term and long-term memory, forgetting theories, thinking and problem solving
  • AS5. The Learning Approach
    Classical conditioning, operant conditioning, social learning theory, behaviourism
  • AS6. The Social Approach
    Conformity and obedience, prejudice and discrimination, group dynamics, social cognition
  • AS7. Issues and Debates in Psychology
    Nature vs nurture, free will vs determinism, reductionism, cultural bias
  • AS8. Classic Studies in Psychology
    Pavlov, Skinner, Milgram, Asch, Loftus and others
  • AS9. Ethical Issues and Animal Research
    Informed consent, deception and privacy, ethical guidelines for animal research
  • AS10. Reliability, Validity and Generalisability
    Reliability, internal and external validity, cross-cultural generalisation

A2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A211. Abnormality (Specialist Option)
    Defining and diagnosing abnormality, depression and anxiety, schizophrenia, treatments
  • A212. Consumer Behaviour (Specialist Option)
    Consumer decision making, advertising psychology, brand loyalty, online behaviour
  • A213. Health (Specialist Option)
    Stress and coping, health behaviour, patient compliance, pain management
  • A214. Organisations (Specialist Option)
    Work motivation, leadership styles, job satisfaction, teams and conflict
  • A215. Application of Approaches
    Applying the four approaches to applied phenomena, integrating theory and practice
  • A216. Advanced Research Methods
    Quasi-experiments, correlational designs, longitudinal studies, cross-cultural comparison
  • A217. Inferential Statistics (Advanced)
    Non-parametric tests, Mann-Whitney, Wilcoxon, chi-square
  • A218. Issues in Application
    Ethics of applied psychology, applied validity, cultural adaptation
  • A219. Contemporary Studies
    Recent empirical studies in applied areas, methodological critique, strength of evidence
  • A220. Synoptic Evaluation
    Cross-approach integration, multi-perspective argumentation, synoptic evaluation

Suitable Majors 适用专业与衔接

心理学、神经科学、教育、社会工作等专业的核心基础科目。

A-Level Psychology is a key subject for psychology, neuroscience, behavioural science, education, social work, HR, market research, consumer research, clinical psychology and counselling. Most UK university psychology programmes recommend or require A-Level Psychology. It provides a solid foundation for university experimental psychology, psychological statistics and abnormal psychology.
