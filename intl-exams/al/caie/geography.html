A-Level Geography

A-Level 地理

CAIE 9696

Course Overview 课程介绍

CAIE 高级地理，平衡自然与人文地理，强调调查与跨尺度分析。

A-Level Geography is a UK advanced secondary course split into AS (Year 1) and A2 (Year 2). It balances physical and human geography, covering hydrology, landforms, climate, population, settlements, and environmental management. It emphasises geographical investigation, data handling and cross-scale analysis. A core subject for geography, environmental science, urban planning, earth sciences, international development and sustainability.

AS Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • AS1. Hydrology and Fluvial Geomorphology
    Drainage basin water cycle, river processes, fluvial landforms, flood management
  • AS2. Atmosphere and Weather
    Atmospheric structure, climate systems, weather processes, urban climate
  • AS3. Rocks and Weathering
    Rock types, weathering processes, mass movement, geological hazards
  • AS4. Population
    Population dynamics, birth and death rates, population structure, population policy
  • AS5. Migration
    Migration motives, international and internal migration, refugee issues, migration impacts
  • AS6. Settlement Dynamics
    Urbanisation, urban structure, rural-urban fringe, urban issues and governance
  • AS7. Fieldwork and Geographical Skills
    Sampling methods, data collection, mapping and statistics, introduction to GIS
  • AS8. Map Interpretation and Data Analysis
    Contour interpretation, statistical charts, data visualisation
  • AS9. Physical Geography Core Themes
    Interactions of natural systems, landform-climate-hydrology coupling
  • AS10. Human Geography Core Themes
    Population-settlement-migration systems, socio-economic spatial patterns

A2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A211. Tropical Environments
    Tropical climate, rainforest and savanna, tropical soils, tropical landforms
  • A212. Coastal Environments
    Coastal processes, coastal landforms, coastal management, sea level change
  • A213. Hazardous Environments
    Earthquakes and volcanoes, tropical cyclones, landslides, disaster risk and response
  • A214. Arid and Semi-Arid Environments
    Desertification, aeolian processes, water resource management in arid regions
  • A215. Production, Location and Change
    Agricultural and industrial location, global production networks, regional transformation
  • A216. Environmental Management
    Sustainable development, resource management, pollution control, environmental policy
  • A217. Global Interdependence
    Globalisation, trade and inequality, transnational flows, global governance
  • A218. Economic Transition
    Post-industrial economy, service and new economy, regional inequality
  • A219. Settlement Themes and Urban Change
    Global cities, urban regeneration, informal settlements
  • A220. Synoptic Geography and Decision Making
    Cross-theme integration, real-world decision making, evaluation and argumentation

Suitable Majors 适用专业与衔接

地理学、环境科学、城市规划、地球科学等专业的核心基础科目。

A-Level Geography is a core subject for geography, environmental science, urban planning, earth sciences, geology, sustainability, international development, natural hazard management, meteorology, and town & country planning. Most UK university geography programmes require or recommend A-Level Geography. It provides a solid foundation for university physical geography processes, human geography, GIS, and environmental policy, and develops spatial thinking and synoptic argumentation.
