A-Level Further Mathematics

A-Level 进阶数学

CAIE 9231

Course Overview 课程介绍

CAIE 进阶数学，含矩阵、复数、微分方程等大学预备内容。

A-Level Further Mathematics extends A-Level Mathematics, split into AS (Year 1) and A2 (Year 2). It covers further pure mathematics, further mechanics, and further probability & statistics, reaching into complex numbers, matrices, differential equations and abstract algebra as university-level preparation. A valuable additional subject for mathematics, physics, engineering, theoretical CS and other mathematically demanding programmes.

AS Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • AS1. Roots of Polynomial Equations
    Roots of polynomial equations, Vieta's formulae, relationships between roots of cubic and quartic equations
  • AS2. Rational Functions and Graphs
    Rational functions and graphs, asymptotes, singularities, curve sketching
  • AS3. Summation of Series
    Summation of series, standard summation formulae, finite differences, generating functions
  • AS4. Matrices
    Matrices, matrix operations, determinants, inverse matrices, systems of linear equations
  • AS5. Polar Coordinates
    Polar coordinates, polar equations, polar curves, area in polar coordinates
  • AS6. Vectors
    Vectors, 3D vectors, cross product, equations of lines and planes, scalar triple product
  • AS7. Proof by Induction
    Proof by induction, sequences and series, inequalities, matrices and divisibility
  • AS8. Momentum and Impulse
    Further mechanics: momentum and impulse, collisions, coefficient of restitution
  • AS9. Circular Motion
    Further mechanics: circular motion, conical pendulum, vertical circular motion
  • AS10. Hooke's Law and Elasticity
    Further mechanics: Hooke's law, elastic potential energy, elastic collisions
  • AS11. Continuous Distributions
    Further statistics: continuous distributions, moment generating functions, distribution functions
  • AS12. Hypothesis Testing
    Further statistics: hypothesis testing, tests for mean and proportion, power of a test

A2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A213. Hyperbolic Functions
    Hyperbolic functions, sinh/cosh/tanh definitions, identities, inverse hyperbolic functions
  • A214. Matrices (Advanced)
    Matrices (advanced), eigenvalues and eigenvectors, diagonalisation, linear transformations
  • A215. Differentiation (Advanced)
    Differentiation (advanced), hyperbolic function derivatives, inverse trig/hyperbolic derivatives, Maclaurin series
  • A216. Integration (Advanced)
    Integration (advanced), hyperbolic function integrals, residue method introduction, surface of revolution
  • A217. Complex Numbers (Advanced)
    Complex numbers (advanced), de Moivre's theorem, complex roots, loci formula, complex plane transformations
  • A218. Differential Equations
    Differential equations, first-order linear, second-order constant coefficient, simultaneous systems
  • A219. Circular Motion and SHM
    Further mechanics: circular motion and simple harmonic motion, period of oscillation, damped oscillations
  • A220. Rotational Dynamics
    Further mechanics: rotational dynamics, moment of inertia, conservation of angular momentum
  • A221. Centre of Mass and Stability
    Further mechanics: centre of mass and stability, composite body centre of mass, toppling conditions
  • A222. Inference and Chi-squared Tests
    Further statistics: statistical inference, chi-squared tests, goodness of fit, contingency tables
  • A223. Sampling and Estimation
    Further statistics: sampling and estimation, central limit theorem, confidence intervals
  • A224. Linear Combinations of Variables
    Further statistics: linear combinations of variables, expectation and variance, covariance and correlation

Suitable Majors 适用专业与衔接

牛剑等顶尖院校数学/物理/工程方向加分或必修科目。

A-Level Further Mathematics is a valuable or required subject for applying to top universities such as Cambridge, Oxford and Imperial for mathematics, physics, engineering, theoretical CS, natural sciences, and economics & mathematical finance. It leads directly into university linear algebra, abstract algebra, differential equations, probability theory and quantum mechanics, and is especially important for students targeting mathematics or theoretical physics.
