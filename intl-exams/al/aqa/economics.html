A-Level Economics (AQA)

A-Level 经济（AQA）

AQA 7136

Course Overview 课程介绍

AQA A-Level 经济，线性结构，微观与宏观并重，经济、金融、商科申请核心。

A-Level Economics (AQA 7136) is a linear qualification combining microeconomics (individuals, firms, markets and market failure) and macroeconomics (the national and international economy), examined by three terminal papers at the end of two years. Students develop analytical and evaluative skills using diagrams, data and real-world contexts. It is a popular core subject for economics, finance, accounting, business, management and PPE applicants.

AS / Year 1 Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • AS1. Economic methodology and the economic problem
    Economic methodology, the economic problem, production possibility frontiers, scarcity, choice and opportunity cost, specialisation and trade
  • AS2. Price determination in a competitive market
    Demand, supply, market equilibrium, price/income/cross elasticity of demand, price elasticity of supply, interrelationships between markets
  • AS3. Production, costs and revenue
    Production and productivity, specialisation and division of labour, costs (fixed, variable, marginal, average), economies of scale, total/average/marginal revenue, profit, technologically and dynamically efficient production
  • AS4. Competitive and concentrated markets
    Market structures, perfect competition, monopolistic competition, oligopoly, monopoly, contestable markets, efficiency and competition policy
  • AS5. The market mechanism, market failure and government intervention
    Externalities, public goods, information gaps, government intervention (taxes, subsidies, regulation, price controls), government failure
  • AS6. The measurement of macroeconomic performance
    Macroeconomic indicators (GDP, inflation, unemployment, balance of payments), index numbers, national income data, limitations of using GDP
  • AS7. How the macroeconomy works
    Circular flow of income, aggregate demand and aggregate supply analysis, the multiplier, output gaps, deflationary and inflationary gaps
  • AS8. Macroeconomic policy
    Demand-side policies (fiscal, monetary), supply-side policies, objectives and conflicts of policy, the policy mix

A2 / Year 2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A29. The labour market
    Demand for labour, supply of labour, wage determination in competitive and imperfectly competitive markets, trade unions, national minimum wage, discrimination
  • A210. The distribution of income and wealth: poverty and inequality
    Distribution of income and wealth, poverty (absolute and relative), inequality measurement (Lorenz curve, Gini coefficient), policies to redistribute
  • A211. Economic performance
    Inflation and deflation, employment and unemployment, balance of payments, economic growth and the macroeconomic objectives, conflicts and trade-offs
  • A212. The financial sector
    Role of the financial sector, banks and the money supply, monetary policy and Bank of England, financial market failure, regulation
  • A213. The international sector
    Globalisation, trade theory, protectionism, exchange rates, balance of payments, the European Union, economic development, role of IMF/World Bank/WTO

Exam Structure (AQA 7136) 考试结构

Paper 1Markets and market failure (Sections 1-5 + labour market + distribution of income) · 2h · 80 marks · 33.3% of A-Level
Paper 2National and international economy (measurement, AD/AS, performance, policy, financial sector, international sector) · 2h · 80 marks · 33.3% of A-Level
Paper 3Economic principles and issues (synoptic, covering all specification content) · 2h · 80 marks · 33.3% of A-Level
NotesLinear structure, terminal exams at end of 2 years; multiple choice, data response and essay questions in each paper

Suitable Majors & Pathway 适用专业与衔接

经济、金融、商科、管理、PPE 等方向的核心科目，文理兼顾。

A-Level Economics (AQA) is one of the most popular subjects for economics, finance, accounting, business, management, PPE (Philosophy, Politics and Economics), international relations and actuarial science applicants. The linear terminal exams reward diagram-based analysis, data interpretation and evaluative essay writing, providing a strong foundation for university microeconomics, macroeconomics, econometrics and political economy modules. Many leading UK universities treat Economics as a facilitating subject.
