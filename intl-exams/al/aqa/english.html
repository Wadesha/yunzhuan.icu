A-Level English (AQA)

A-Level 英语（AQA）

AQA 7707 (English Language) / 7717 (English Literature)

Course Overview 课程介绍

AQA 英语语言与文学，线性结构，重文本分析与批判性写作，文社类申请核心。

A-Level English at AQA is offered as two separate linear qualifications: English Language (7707) and English Literature (7717). Both are assessed entirely by terminal exams at the end of two years, with a Non-Exam Assessment (NEA) component for full A-Level. Language students analyse textual variations, language diversity and change, and children's language development; Literature students study texts across time including the universal theme of "Love through the ages" and contextual literary periods. Together they form a core subject for English, journalism, law, education, media and humanities applicants.

AS / Year 1 Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • AS[Language] 1. Language and the individual
    Textual variations and representations, methods of language analysis (lexis and semantics, grammar, phonetics and phonology, pragmatics, discourse), comparing texts
  • AS[Language] 2. Language varieties
    Language diversity (regional, social, occupational, gender), dialects, sociolects, accents, language discourses, writing about language issues
  • AS[Literature] 3. Love through the ages
    Shakespeare (Othello / Antony and Cleopatra / Romeo and Juliet etc.), unseen poetry comparison, the theme of love across prose, poetry and drama
  • AS[Literature] 4. Love through the ages: prose
    Set prose pairs on the theme of love (e.g. Austen, Hardy, Fitzgerald, McEwan), comparing pre-1900 and post-1900 prose texts

A2 / Year 2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A2[Language] 5. Language, the individual and society
    Textual variations and representations, methods of language analysis, children's language development (spoken, written and multimodal 0-11 years)
  • A2[Language] 6. Language diversity and change
    Language change over time (Old to Modern English), global English, language diversity (regional, social, individual), language discourses and debates, evaluation essay
  • A2[Language] 7. NEA: Language in action
    Language investigation (original data-led research) and a piece of original writing with a supporting commentary, 3,500 words total
  • A2[Literature] 8. Texts in shared contexts
    One option from: Modern times (literature from 1945 to present) · WW1 and its aftermath · Literature from 1945 to present, including set prose, poetry and drama texts
  • A2[Literature] 9. NEA: Independent critical study
    Comparative critical study of two texts, at least one pre-1900, on a chosen theme, 2,500-word essay plus bibliography

Suitable Majors & Pathway 适用专业与衔接

英语、文学、新闻、法律、教育、传媒等方向核心科目，文社类申请首选。

A-Level English Language or Literature (AQA) is highly valued by leading UK universities for English, journalism, law, education, media, communications, publishing, creative writing, drama, history, politics and PPE. The linear terminal exams reward sophisticated textual analysis, contextual understanding and structured argument, providing a strong foundation for university literary criticism, sociolinguistics, rhetoric and critical theory modules. A facilitating subject for arts, humanities and social sciences.
