A-Level Biology (AQA)

A-Level 生物（AQA）

AQA 7402 · OxfordAQA 9650

Course Overview 课程介绍

AQA A-Level 生物，线性结构，覆盖分子、细胞、生理、遗传与进化，医学院核心科目。

A-Level Biology (AQA 7402) is a linear qualification covering biological molecules, cells, exchange, genetics, energy transfers, response, evolution and gene expression, examined by three terminal papers at the end of two years. Practical skills are developed through 12 required practicals and the separately reported Practical Endorsement. It is a core subject for biology, medicine, dentistry, veterinary science, biomedical science, neuroscience and many health-related degrees.

AS / Year 1 Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • AS1. Biological molecules
    Monomers and polymers, carbohydrates, lipids, proteins and enzymes, nucleic acids, ATP, water and inorganic ions
  • AS2. Cells
    Cell structure, eukaryotic and prokaryotic cells, cell division, transport across membranes, the immune system, monoclonal antibodies
  • AS3. Organisms exchange substances with their environment
    Surface area to volume ratio, gas exchange in insects/fish/mammals, digestion and absorption, mass transport in animals (heart, blood) and plants (xylem, phloem)
  • AS4. Genetic information, variation and relationships
    DNA, genes and chromosomes, protein synthesis, genetic diversity and adaptation, species and taxonomy, biodiversity, investigating variation

A2 / Year 2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A25. Energy transfers in and between organisms
    Photosynthesis, respiration, energy and ecosystems, nutrient cycles (nitrogen, phosphorus)
  • A26. Organisms respond to changes in their internal and external environments
    Stimuli and responses, nervous coordination, receptors, control of heart rate and breathing, skeletal muscle, homeostasis and blood glucose control
  • A27. Genetics, populations, evolution and ecosystems
    Inheritance (monohybrid, dihybrid, epistasis, chi-squared), populations, evolution may lead to speciation, ecosystems, succession, conservation
  • A28. The control of gene expression
    Alteration of the sequence, gene expression is controlled, recombinant DNA technology, differences in DNA and proteins between individuals, stem cells

Exam Structure (AQA 7402) 考试结构

Paper 1Sections 1-4 (Biological molecules, Cells, Exchange, Genetic information) + relevant practical skills · 2h · 91 marks · 35% of A-Level
Paper 2Sections 5-8 (Energy, Response, Genetics and evolution, Gene expression) + relevant practical skills · 2h · 91 marks · 35% of A-Level
Paper 3All content and practical skills, including synoptic essay · 2h · 78 marks · 30% of A-Level
Practical EndorsementNon-exam assessment, teacher-assessed across 12 required practicals, reported separately as Pass / Not Reported

Suitable Majors & Pathway 适用专业与衔接

生物、医学、生物医学、神经科学等方向的核心前置科目，医学院常要求必修。

A-Level Biology (AQA) is a core prerequisite for biology, biomedical science, medicine, dentistry, veterinary science, neuroscience, biochemistry, genetics, ecology, pharmacology and sports science. Many UK medical schools require Biology (often with Chemistry) as a mandatory A-Level. The linear terminal exams reward conceptual integration from molecules to ecosystems, plus analytical practical skills, providing a strong foundation for university cell biology, physiology, genetics and bioinformatics modules.
