A-Level Chemistry (AQA)

A-Level 化学（AQA）

AQA 7404 · OxfordAQA 9660

Course Overview 课程介绍

AQA A-Level 化学，线性结构，覆盖物理化学、无机化学与有机化学三大板块，医学院必修。

A-Level Chemistry (AQA 7404) is a linear qualification split into physical, inorganic and organic chemistry, examined by three terminal papers at the end of two years. Students develop quantitative practical skills through 12 required practicals and the separately reported Practical Endorsement. The course is a key prerequisite for chemistry, chemical engineering, medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, biochemistry and materials science.

AS / Year 1 Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • AS1. Physical chemistry (foundations)
    Atomic structure, amount of substance, bonding, energetics, kinetics, chemical equilibria, Le Chatelier's principle, oxidation-reduction
  • AS2. Inorganic chemistry (foundations)
    Periodicity, Group 2 alkaline earth metals, Group 7 halogens and their reactions, qualitative analysis
  • AS3. Organic chemistry (introduction)
    Introduction to organic chemistry, nomenclature and isomerism, alkanes, halogenoalkanes, alkenes, alcohols, organic analysis (test-tube reactions and infrared spectroscopy)

A2 / Year 2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A24. Physical chemistry (advanced)
    Thermodynamics (Born-Haber cycles, entropy, Gibbs free energy), rate equations, Kp, electrode potentials and electrochemical cells, acids and bases (Ka, Kw, pH, buffers)
  • A25. Inorganic chemistry (advanced)
    Period 3 elements and their oxides, transition metals (variable oxidation states, complex ions, ligand substitution, catalysis), reactions of ions in aqueous solution
  • A26. Organic chemistry (advanced)
    Carboxylic acids and derivatives, aromatic chemistry (benzene), amines, polymers (addition and condensation), amino acids and proteins, organic synthesis, structure determination (NMR, chromatography, mass spectrometry)

Exam Structure (AQA 7404) 考试结构

Paper 1Physical chemistry + Inorganic chemistry + relevant practical skills · 2h · 105 marks · 35% of A-Level
Paper 2Physical chemistry + Organic chemistry + relevant practical skills · 2h · 105 marks · 35% of A-Level
Paper 3All content and practical skills · 2h · 90 marks · 30% of A-Level
Practical EndorsementNon-exam assessment, teacher-assessed across 12 required practicals, reported separately as Pass / Not Reported

Suitable Majors & Pathway 适用专业与衔接

化学、医学、药学、材料等专业的重要前置科目，多数英国医学院必修。

A-Level Chemistry (AQA) is a mandatory subject for chemistry, chemical engineering, medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, veterinary science and biochemistry, and strongly recommended for materials science, environmental science, food science and forensics. Many UK medical schools require Chemistry as a non-negotiable A-Level. The linear terminal exams reward integrated understanding of physical, inorganic and organic chemistry, providing a strong base for university organic synthesis, thermodynamics and biochemistry modules.
