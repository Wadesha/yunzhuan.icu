A-Level Physics (AQA)

A-Level 物理（AQA）

AQA 7408 · OxfordAQA 9630

Course Overview 课程介绍

AQA A-Level 物理，线性结构，2 年末统一考试，理工科申请核心科目。

A-Level Physics (AQA 7408) is a linear UK advanced qualification assessed entirely by terminal exams at the end of two years. It covers measurements, particles and radiation, waves, mechanics and materials, electricity, further mechanics, thermal physics, fields, nuclear physics, plus one optional unit. Strong emphasis is placed on practical skills through the separately reported Practical Endorsement. It is a core subject for engineering, physics, computer science and medicine applicants.

AS / Year 1 Syllabus AS 阶段章节

  • AS1. Measurements and their errors
    Use of SI units and their prefixes, limitation of physical measurements, estimation of physical quantities
  • AS2. Particles and radiation
    Particles, radiation, quantum phenomena, photoelectric effect, wave-particle duality, particle classification and fundamental forces
  • AS3. Waves
    Progressive and stationary waves, refraction, diffraction, interference, interference experiments, polarisation
  • AS4. Mechanics and materials
    Force, energy and momentum, moments, work, energy and power, conservation laws, properties of materials, Young's modulus, elastic and plastic deformation
  • AS5. Electricity
    Current electricity, DC circuits, resistivity, internal resistance, Kirchhoff's laws, potential divider, alternating currents (introductory)
  • AS6. Further mechanics (periodic motion)
    Momentum in two dimensions, circular motion, simple harmonic motion, thermal physics is reserved for A2 (Section 6.2)

A2 / Year 2 Syllabus A2 阶段章节

  • A27. Thermal physics (Section 6.2)
    Thermal energy transfer, ideal gases, molecular kinetic theory, specific heat capacity, latent heat, first law of thermodynamics
  • A28. Fields and their consequences (Section 7)
    Force fields, gravitational fields, electric fields, capacitance, magnetic fields, magnetic flux, electromagnetic induction, alternating currents, transformers
  • A29. Nuclear physics (Section 8)
    Radioactive decay, nuclear instability, nuclear binding energy, fission and fusion, induced fission, safety and applications of nuclear physics
  • A210. Option (one of)
    Astrophysics · Medical physics · Engineering physics · Turning points in physics · Electronics (students study one option examined in Paper 3 Section B)

Exam Structure (AQA 7408) 考试结构

Paper 1Sections 1-5 and 6.1 (Periodic motion) · 2h · 85 marks · 34% of A-Level
Paper 2Sections 6.2, 7 and 8 (assumes knowledge of 1-6.1) · 2h · 85 marks · 34% of A-Level
Paper 3Section A: compulsory practical skills and data analysis · Section B: one option · 2h · 80 marks · 32% of A-Level
Practical EndorsementNon-exam assessment, teacher-assessed across 12 required practicals, reported separately as Pass / Not Reported

Suitable Majors & Pathway 适用专业与衔接

理工科与医学申请的核心前置科目，工程、物理、CS、医学方向强烈推荐。

A-Level Physics (AQA) is a key prerequisite for physics, engineering (mechanical, electrical, aerospace, civil), computer science, mathematics, astronomy and many medicine or dentistry programmes. The linear structure rewards sustained conceptual mastery and strong mathematical fluency, providing a solid foundation for university mechanics, electromagnetism, quantum physics and engineering modules.
