IGCSE Mathematics

IGCSE 数学

CAIE 0580 / 0980

Course Overview 课程介绍

IGCSE 数学 14-16 岁，分 Core/Extended，A-Level 数学/IB Mathematics 标准前置。

IGCSE Mathematics is the international GCSE Maths course for students aged 14-16, and is the standard preparation for A-Level Mathematics and IB Mathematics. It covers number, algebra and graphs, coordinate geometry, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry, vectors and transformations, probability and statistics. The Extended tier adds functions, composite/inverse functions, obtuse-angle trigonometry, logarithms and exponentials.

Syllabus 章节大纲

  • Core1. Number
    Integers, fractions, decimals / accuracy and significant figures / ratio and percentage / standard form / calculator use
  • Core2. Algebra and graphs
    Simplifying algebraic expressions / linear and quadratic equations / inequalities / sequences / function graphs
  • Ext3. Coordinate geometry
    Distance and midpoint between two points / line equation y=mx+c / parallel and perpendicular lines
  • Core4. Geometry
    Angles and parallel lines / polygons / circle theorems (full Extended only) / symmetry / similarity and congruence
  • Core5. Mensuration
    Perimeter, area, volume / arc and sector / surface area and volume of sphere, cone, cylinder
  • Core6. Trigonometry
    Right-angled triangles / sine and cosine rules (Extended) / 3D problems / obtuse-angle trigonometry (Extended)
  • Ext7. Vectors and transformations
    Vector addition, subtraction and scalar multiplication / column vectors / translation, rotation, reflection, enlargement / vectors in transformations
  • Core8. Probability
    Simple probability / tree diagrams / conditional probability (Extended) / mutually exclusive and independent events
  • Core9. Statistics
    Mean, median, mode / range and interquartile range (Extended) / histograms / cumulative frequency curves

Core vs Extended Core 与 Extended 区分

Core 不含圆定理、函数、对数、正余弦定理；Extended 增加全部，上限 A*/9。

CoreCore covers basic number and algebra, geometry and mensuration, simple trigonometry and statistics, without full circle theorems, function composition and inverse functions, logarithms, sine/cosine rules, conditional probability or quantitative cumulative frequency analysis. Core caps at grade C, with relatively short and direct questions.

ExtExtended builds on Core by adding full circle theorems, functions (f(x) and composite/inverse functions), logarithms and exponentials, obtuse-angle trigonometry and sine/cosine rules, vector algebra, conditional probability and advanced statistical graphs (quantitative histograms and cumulative frequency curves). Extended questions are longer and more synthesised, capping at A* / 9, and is essential for progression to A-Level Mathematics or IB Mathematics HL/SL.

Pathway 适用衔接

Extended 推荐衔接 A-Level 9709、Further Maths 9231、IB Math AA/AI，理工经济建议 A*/9。

IGCSE Mathematics (Extended recommended) is the standard preparation for A-Level Mathematics (CAIE 9709), A-Level Further Mathematics (CAIE 9231) and IB Mathematics AA/AI HL/SL. After completing IGCSE Mathematics Extended, students can progress directly to A-Level AS; those with a solid foundation in functions, coordinate geometry, trigonometry and statistics transition more smoothly to A-Level calculus, mechanics, probability and statistics. For students applying to STEM, economics or business, an A* or 9 in IGCSE Mathematics is recommended. Strong students targeting mathematics, physics or engineering may also take IGCSE Additional Mathematics (0606) concurrently.
