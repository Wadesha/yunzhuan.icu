IGCSE Additional Mathematics

IGCSE 附加数学

CAIE 0606

Course Overview 课程介绍

IGCSE 附加数学 0606 高于 0580，引入微积分向量级数，衔接 A-Level Further Maths。

IGCSE Additional Mathematics (0606) is an advanced CAIE course above IGCSE Mathematics 0580, intended for mathematically strong students preparing for A-Level Further Mathematics or IB Mathematics HL. It introduces calculus, vectors, series, permutations and combinations, and binomial expansions, bridging 0580 and A-Level Mathematics 9709. There is no Core/Extended tier; all candidates sit the same papers.

Syllabus 章节大纲

  • Ext1. Functions
    Domain and range / composite functions / inverse functions / function graph transformations
  • Ext2. Quadratic functions
    Discriminant / completing the square / quadratic inequalities / maxima and minima
  • Ext3. Equations, inequalities and graphs
    Modulus and absolute value functions / cubic and higher-degree equation graphs
  • Ext4. Indices and surds
    Index laws / rationalising denominators / surd operations
  • Ext5. Factors of polynomials
    Remainder and factor theorems / factorisation of cubic polynomials
  • Ext6. Simultaneous equations
    Linear two-variable and non-linear simultaneous / three-variable linear systems
  • Ext7. Logarithmic and exponential functions
    Exponential equations / laws of logarithms / natural logarithms
  • Ext8. Straight-line graphs
    Line equations / parallel and perpendicular / intersection of two lines / distance formula
  • Ext9. Circular measure
    Radian measure / arc length and sector area / radian-degree conversion
  • Ext10. Trigonometry
    Trigonometric identities / sine and cosine rules / trigonometric equations and graphs
  • Ext11. Differentiation
    Derivative definition / differentiation of polynomials and trigonometric functions / tangents and normals / maxima, minima and inflection points
  • Ext12. Integration
    Indefinite and definite integrals / integration of polynomials and trigonometric functions / area under a curve and volume of revolution
  • Ext13. Vectors
    2D vector addition, subtraction and scalar multiplication / magnitude and unit vectors / vectors in linear motion
  • Ext14. Series
    Arithmetic and geometric sequences / summation formulae / convergence of infinite geometric series
  • Ext15. Permutations and combinations
    Permutation and combination counts / repetition and restriction cases
  • Ext16. Binomial expansions
    Binomial theorem / positive integer and general power expansions

Core vs Extended Core 与 Extended 区分

0606 不分档，全员同卷，难度高于 0580 Extended，覆盖 A-Level P1 约 60-70%。

ExtNote: Additional Mathematics (0606) has no Core/Extended tier; all candidates sit the same papers, uniformly above IGCSE Maths Extended in difficulty. In other words, all 16 topics in 0606 are at Extended level, with content depth comparable to the early stages of A-Level AS Mathematics (CAIE 9709 P1).

Compared with IGCSE Maths 0580 Extended, 0606 adds A-Level-style algebra and analysis including calculus (differentiation and definite integration), binomial theorem with permutations and combinations, arithmetic and geometric series, radian measure, factor and remainder theorems, and logarithmic and exponential functions. After completing 0606, students enter A-Level Mathematics already covering approximately 60-70% of Pure Mathematics 1 (P1).

Pathway 适用衔接

0606 衔接 A-Level 9709 P1/M1、Further Maths 9231、IB Math HL，G5 理工强竞争力。

After completing IGCSE Additional Mathematics 0606, students hold a clear advantage in A-Level Mathematics (CAIE 9709) Pure Mathematics 1 (P1) and Mechanics 1 (M1), and may progress directly to A-Level Further Mathematics (9231) or IB Mathematics HL (Analysis and Approaches AA). 0606 is a strong differentiator for applicants to mathematics, physics, engineering, economics and computer science programmes. Students targeting G5 or equivalent top universities for STEM are advised to complete both 0580 Extended and 0606 in Year 11, to enable smooth progression to A-Level Further Maths or IB HL in Year 12.
