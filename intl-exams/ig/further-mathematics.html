IGCSE 附加数学

IGCSE Additional Mathematics

CAIE 0606

课程介绍 Course Overview

IGCSE 附加数学（Additional Mathematics，0606）是 CAIE 提供的高于 IGCSE Maths（0580）的进阶数学课程，通常面向数学能力较强、未来计划进入 A-Level 进阶数学或 IB Mathematics HL 的学生。课程引入微积分、向量、级数、排列组合、二项式展开等 A-Level 风格的内容，是 0580 与 A-Level 数学 9709 之间的关键桥梁。该课程不设 Core/Extended 分档，所有学生均考同一份卷，难度相当于 A-Level AS 阶段早期水平。

IGCSE Additional Mathematics (0606) is an advanced CAIE course above IGCSE Mathematics 0580, intended for mathematically strong students preparing for A-Level Further Mathematics or IB Mathematics HL. It introduces calculus, vectors, series, permutations and combinations, and binomial expansions, bridging 0580 and A-Level Mathematics 9709. There is no Core/Extended tier; all candidates sit the same papers.

Core 与 Extended 区分 Core vs Extended

Ext注意：Additional Mathematics（0606）本身不设 Core / Extended 两档，所有学生均考相同卷面，难度统一高于 IGCSE Maths Extended。换言之，0606 的所有 16 个主题都属 Extended 级别，且其内容深度与 A-Level AS 数学（CAIE 9709 P1）的早期内容相当。

与 IGCSE Maths 0580 Extended 的差异：0606 在 0580 的基础上新增了微积分（求导与定积分）、二项式定理与排列组合、等差等比级数、弧度制、余式与因式定理、对数与指数函数等 A-Level 风格的代数与分析内容。完成 0606 后，学生进入 A-Level 数学时已具备 P1（Pure Mathematics 1）约 60-70% 的内容基础。

Additional Mathematics 0606 has no Core/Extended tier; all candidates sit the same papers, uniformly above IGCSE Maths Extended. Compared with 0580 Extended, 0606 adds calculus (differentiation and integration), binomial theorem, permutations and combinations, arithmetic and geometric series, radian measure, factor and remainder theorems, and logarithmic/exponential functions, covering approximately 60-70% of A-Level Pure Mathematics 1 (P1) content in advance.

适用衔接 Pathway

完成 IGCSE Additional Mathematics 0606 后，学生在 A-Level 数学（CAIE 9709）的 Pure Mathematics 1（P1）与 Mechanics 1（M1）上具备明显优势，可考虑直接进入 A-Level 进阶数学（9231）或 IB Mathematics HL（分析与Approaches AA）。0606 也是申请数学、物理、工程、经济、计算机等专业的强竞争力学科。建议目标 G5 或同等顶尖大学的理工类学生在 Year 11 同步完成 0580 Extended 与 0606，以便 Year 12 顺利衔接 A-Level Further Maths 或 IB HL。

After completing 0606, students hold a clear advantage in A-Level Mathematics 9709 (P1 and M1) and may progress directly to A-Level Further Mathematics 9231 or IB Mathematics HL (Analysis and Approaches). 0606 is a strong differentiator for applicants to mathematics, physics, engineering, economics and computer science programmes at top universities.
