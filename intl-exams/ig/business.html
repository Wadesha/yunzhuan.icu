IGCSE Business

IGCSE 商科

CAIE 0450 / 0986

Course Overview 课程介绍

IGCSE 商科 14-16 岁，不分档，A-Level Business、IB Business Management 衔接课程。

IGCSE Business is the international GCSE Business course for students aged 14-16, preparing students for A-Level Business, IB Business Management and business management pathways. It covers six themes: understanding business activity, people in business, marketing, operations management, financial decisions, and external influences. There is no Core/Extended tier; all candidates sit the same papers.

Syllabus 章节大纲

  • Core1. Understanding business activity
    Business objectives and stakeholders / types of business activity / private and public enterprise / business growth
  • Core2. People in business
    Recruitment and training / motivation theory / organisational structure / communication and unions
  • Ext3. Marketing
    Market research / market segmentation / marketing mix 4P / product life cycle
  • Ext4. Operations management
    Production methods / economies of scale / quality / productivity / technology
  • Ext5. Financial information and decisions
    Accounting basics / costs and break-even / cash flow / income statement and balance sheet / financial performance analysis
  • Ext6. External influences on business activity
    Government policy / law / business cycle / environment and ethics / international trade and multinationals

Core vs Extended Core 与 Extended 区分

0450/0986 不分档，全员同卷，Paper 2 案例分析体现评估深度。

ExtNote: IGCSE Business (0450 / 0986) has no Core/Extended tier; all candidates sit the same Paper 1 and Paper 2, uniformly graded A* - G or 9-1. Difficulty gradients are reflected in question type and assessment depth rather than tiering.

Specifically: Paper 1 is a short-answer and data response paper (covering all six topics), relatively foundational; Paper 2 is a case-study-based extended question paper (all compulsory), requiring analysis, recommendation and evaluation. Topics 1-2 are foundational; topics 3-6 demand higher analysis and evaluation (involving marketing mix quantitative decisions, financial statement interpretation, break-even analysis, integrated external environment impact), so topic tags on this page distinguish core and ext by relative difficulty.

Exam Structure (CAIE 0450 / 0986) 考试结构

Paper 1Short Answer and Data Response · 1h30m · 80 marks · 50%
Paper 2Case Study · 1h30m · 80 marks · 50%

Note: Both papers cover all six topics; Paper 2 is based on a real business context case, requiring all questions to be answered. No practical exam; no Core/Extended choice.

Pathway 适用衔接

衔接 A-Level 9609、IB Business Management，商科管理建议 A*/9 并配合经济数学。

IGCSE Business is the standard preparation for A-Level Business (CAIE 9609), IB Business Management HL/SL and business management pathways. After completing IGCSE Business, students can progress directly to A-Level AS; those with a solid foundation in business objectives, marketing mix, financial basics and external influences transition more smoothly to A-Level strategic management, HR theory, marketing strategy and quantitative financial decision-making. For students applying to business, management or marketing, an A* or 9 in IGCSE Business is recommended, alongside strong IGCSE Economics and Mathematics.
