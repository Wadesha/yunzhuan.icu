IGCSE English

IGCSE 英语

CAIE 0500 / 0510

Course Overview 课程介绍

IGCSE 英语分 First Language 0500 与 Second Language 0510 两条路径。

IGCSE English in the CAIE system is split into two pathways: First Language English (0500, for near-native speakers) and English as a Second Language (0510/0511, for non-native speakers). Both develop reading, writing and speaking skills but differ in emphasis: 0500 focuses on literary and discursive writing; 0510 emphasises communicative and academic English with equal weight on listening and speaking. 0510 has Core/Extended tiers; 0500 does not.

Key Facts 基本信息

Board (考试局)CAIE (Cambridge Assessment International Education)
CAIE Code (代码)0500 (First Language) / 0510 / 0511 (Second Language)
Duration (学制)Usually 2 years (Year 10-11)
Grading (评分)0500: 9-1 (only) · 0510: A*-G
Core / Extended Tiers (分档)0500 no tier · 0510 has Core/Extended tiers

Syllabus 章节大纲

  • Core1. Reading comprehension
    Main idea and detail comprehension / inference and author intent / style and register analysis
  • Core2. Summary writing
    Information extraction / summary and paraphrase / word count control
  • Ext3. Directed writing
    Text type identification / audience and purpose / letters, reports, speeches
  • Ext4. Composition and creative writing
    Descriptive, narrative, argumentative writing / argument and structure / language style
  • Core5. Listening (Second Language 0510 only)
    Main idea and detail / inference / short dialogues and long monologues / note-taking and gap-fill
  • Ext6. Speaking and oral communication
    Personal statement / topic discussion / role-play and interaction / 0510 counts or separate endorsement

Core vs Extended Core 与 Extended 区分

0500 不分档；0510 分 Core（上限 C）与 Extended（上限 A*），文本与听力难度递增。

CoreFirst Language English (0500) has no Core/Extended tier; all candidates sit unified papers. Second Language 0510 offers both tiers: Core uses shorter texts and more direct questions, with slower listening and speaking audio, capping at grade C.

Ext0510 Extended builds on Core: reading texts are longer and more abstract (involving opinions, inference and analysis of author attitude); writing tasks are more complex (including reports, argumentative essays and proposal-style directed writing); listening materials approach authentic speed, with longer monologues and multi-speaker dialogues. 0500 (First Language) Paper 1 and Paper 2 are above 0510 Extended in difficulty, requiring literary writing and critical reading.

Exam Structure (CAIE 0500 / 0510) 考试结构

0500 Paper 1Reading · 2h · 50 marks · 50%
0500 Paper 2Directed Writing and Composition · 2h · 50 marks · 50%
0500 Component 4Speaking and Listening · separate endorsement (not in total)
0510 Paper 1Reading and Writing (Core 1h30m / Extended 2h) · 70%
0510 Paper 2Listening (Core 30-40m / Extended 50m) · 15%
0510 Component 3Speaking · 15% (0511 separate endorsement, not counted)

Note: 0500 has 9-1 grading only; 0510/0511 have Core/Extended choice. The difference between 0511 and 0510 is whether speaking counts toward the total.

Pathway 适用衔接

0500 衔接 A-Level 9093/9695、IB English A；0510 可作英语能力证明替代雅思。

IGCSE First Language English (0500) is the standard preparation for A-Level English Language (CAIE 9093), A-Level English Literature (9695) and IB English A Language and Literature HL/SL; most UK universities accept 0500 Paper 1+2 at grade 5/4+ as English proficiency evidence (some courses require 6+). IGCSE English Second Language (0510) is typically used as English proficiency evidence for non-native applicants, leading to IB English B HL/SL or as a language-score alternative for university applications; most UK universities accept 0510 Extended at B/6+ (top universities may require A/7+). Chinese students applying overseas often use 0510 as preliminary evidence in place of IELTS/TOEFL.
