IGCSE Physics

IGCSE 物理

CAIE 0625 / 0972

Course Overview 课程介绍

IGCSE 物理 14-16 岁，分 Core 和 Extended 两档，A-Level/IB 衔接课程。

IGCSE Physics is the international version of GCSE Physics for students aged 14-16, typically serving as a pathway to A-Level or IB Physics. It covers mechanics, thermal physics, waves, electromagnetism and nuclear physics, with strong emphasis on practical skills, data analysis and unit conversions. The course is split into Core and Extended tiers, where Extended adds space physics, momentum conservation and electromagnetic induction.

Syllabus 章节大纲

  • Core1. Motion, forces and energy
    Speed and acceleration / forces and Newton's laws / momentum and impulse / work, power and energy / conservation of energy
  • Core2. Thermal physics
    Kinetic particle theory / states of matter / specific heat capacity / thermal conduction
  • Core3. Waves
    Transverse and longitudinal waves / wave speed, wavelength, frequency / reflection and refraction / sound and the EM spectrum
  • Core4. Electricity and magnetism
    Static electricity / current, voltage, resistance / series and parallel circuits / magnetic force / electromagnetic induction
  • Core5. Nuclear physics
    Atomic structure / alpha, beta, gamma decay / half-life / nuclear fission and fusion
  • Ext6. Space physics
    Solar system / stellar evolution / redshift and cosmic expansion / gravity and orbits (Extended only)

Core vs Extended Core 与 Extended 区分

Core 覆盖前 5 章上限 C；Extended 增加空间物理与定量分析，上限 A*/9，衔接 A-Level。

CoreCore is suitable for average-ability students and covers only the first five topics (motion and energy, thermal physics, waves, electricity and magnetism, nuclear physics), with a maximum grade of C. Core excludes vector decomposition of momentum conservation, quantitative electromagnetic induction, and space physics.

ExtExtended builds on Core by adding the Space physics chapter, plus quantitative momentum conservation, electromagnetic induction and transformers, and radioactivity analysis. Extended caps at A* (or 9) and is the recommended path into A-Level Physics.

Exam Structure (CAIE 0625 / 0972) 考试结构

Paper 1Multiple Choice (Core) · 45 min · 40 questions · 30%
Paper 2Multiple Choice (Extended) · 45 min · 40 questions · 30%
Paper 3Theory (Core) · 1h15m · 80 marks · 50%
Paper 4Theory (Extended) · 1h15m · 80 marks · 50%
Paper 5Practical Test · 1h15m · 40 marks · 20% · Core/Extended common
Paper 6Alternative to Practical · 1h · 40 marks · 20% · replaces Paper 5

Note: Core candidates sit Paper 1+3+(5 or 6); Extended candidates sit Paper 2+4+(5 or 6). Most schools choose Paper 6 in place of the practical test.

Pathway 适用衔接

Extended 推荐衔接 A-Level 9702、IB Physics、AP Physics，理工医专业建议 A*/9。

IGCSE Physics (Extended recommended) is the standard preparation for A-Level Physics (CAIE 9702), IB Physics HL/SL and AP Physics 1/2/C. After completing IGCSE Physics, students can progress directly to A-Level AS or the first year of IB DP; those with a strong foundation in mechanics, electromagnetism and waves transition more smoothly to advanced A-Level topics such as quantum physics, circular motion and quantitative electromagnetic induction. For students applying to engineering, medicine, physics or computer science, an A* or 9 in IGCSE Physics is recommended.
