IB Business Management

IB 商业管理

IB Diploma · Business Management HL/SL

Course Overview 课程介绍

IB 商业管理分 HL/SL，围绕组织、人力、财务、营销、运营五大职能单元。

IB Business Management is a two-year secondary business course offered by IBO, available at both Higher Level (HL) and Standard Level (SL). The course covers five functional units — business organization and environment, human resources, finance and accounts, marketing, and operations management — emphasizing the use of business theory to analyze real-world cases and develop decision-making and social responsibility. HL extends each unit with additional topics and more demanding integration tasks. A key subject for business, management, marketing, and HR applicants.

HL Teaching Hours (HL 课时)240 hours
SL Teaching Hours (SL 课时)150 hours
Grading (评分)1–7

SL Syllabus SL 阶段章节

  • SL1. Business organization and environment
    Types of organizations / objectives and stakeholders / SWOT / external environment / growth and integration
  • SL2. Human resource management
    Recruitment and training / motivation theories / leadership styles / organizational culture
  • SL3. Finance and accounts
    Financial statements / ratio analysis / cash flow / costs and profit / budgets
  • SL4. Marketing
    Marketing mix 4P / market segmentation / positioning / marketing plan
  • SL5. Operations management
    Production methods / quality / inventory / location / R&D

HL Additional Topics HL 额外章节

  • HL6. Business organization (HL)
    Organizational growth / change management / multinational enterprises / cultural differences
  • HL7. Human resource (HL)
    Organizational structure / advanced motivation theories / crisis and conflict management / trade unions
  • HL8. Finance and accounts (HL)
    Investment appraisal (NPV, IRR, payback period) / depreciation methods / in-depth financial statement analysis
  • HL9. Marketing (HL)
    Brand strategy / market research methods / international marketing / marketing ethics
  • HL10. Operations management (HL)
    Lean production / production planning / supply chain management / innovation and R&D
  • HL11. Integrated cases and business plans
    HL integrated cases and cross-unit synthesis questions emphasizing multi-functional decision-making

Internal Assessment 内部评估 IA

IA 为商业评论（SL）或研究项目报告（HL），均限 1,800 字以内。

The IB Business Management IA is a written commentary or project report based on a real business issue, worth 25% of the final grade for both SL and HL. SL students produce a commentary up to 1,800 words based on primary or secondary data. HL students produce a research project up to 1,800 words requiring primary research (interviews, surveys) and integration across all five units. Assessment criteria: knowledge, application, analysis, evaluation, communication, and synthesis.
