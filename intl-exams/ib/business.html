IB 商业管理

IB Business Management

IB Diploma · Business Management HL/SL

课程介绍 Course Overview

IB 商业管理是国际文凭组织（IBO）开设的两年制高中商业课程，分 Higher Level (HL) 与 Standard Level (SL) 两个难度。课程围绕"组织环境—人力资源—财务—营销—运营"五大职能单元展开，强调用商业理论分析真实企业案例，培养决策思维与社会责任意识。HL 在 SL 基础上对每个单元增加扩展主题，并要求更高难度的综合应用。是申请商科、管理、市场营销、人力资源等专业的重要科目。

IB Business Management is a two-year secondary business course offered by IBO, available at both Higher Level (HL) and Standard Level (SL). The course covers five functional units — business organization and environment, human resources, finance and accounts, marketing, and operations management — emphasizing the use of business theory to analyze real-world cases and develop decision-making and social responsibility. HL extends each unit with additional topics and more demanding integration tasks. A key subject for business, management, marketing, and HR applicants.

内部评估 IA Internal Assessment

IB 商业管理的 IA 是一项基于真实企业问题的书面评论/项目报告，占学科总分的 SL 25% / HL 25%。SL 学生需基于一手或二手数据撰写 1,800 字以内的商业评论；HL 学生需撰写 1,800 字以内的研究项目报告，要求体现一手调研（如访谈、问卷）并整合 5 个单元内容。评估维度：知识理解、应用、分析、评估、沟通、综合。

The IB Business Management IA is a written commentary or project report based on a real business issue, worth 25% of the final grade for both SL and HL. SL students produce a commentary up to 1,800 words based on primary or secondary data. HL students produce a research project up to 1,800 words requiring primary research (interviews, surveys) and integration across all five units. Assessment criteria: knowledge, application, analysis, evaluation, communication, and synthesis.
