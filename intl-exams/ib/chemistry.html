IB Chemistry

IB 化学

IB Diploma · Chemistry HL/SL

Course Overview 课程介绍

IB 化学分 HL 与 SL，覆盖计量、原子结构、化学键、动力学等主线，IA 占 20%。

IB Chemistry is a two-year secondary chemistry course offered by IBO, available at both Higher Level (HL) and Standard Level (SL). The course builds a coherent framework of matter and reactions across stoichiometry, atomic structure, periodicity, bonding, energetics, kinetics, equilibrium, acids and bases, redox, organic chemistry and analytical techniques. HL extends each of these areas with greater quantitative depth and spectroscopic analysis. A core subject for chemistry, medicine, biology, materials and chemical engineering applicants.

SL Syllabus SL 阶段章节

  • SL1. Stoichiometric relationships
    Mole concept / chemical equations / limiting reactants / gases and solution concentration
  • SL2. Atomic structure
    Development of atomic models / electron configuration / mass spectrometry / isotopes
  • SL3. Periodicity
    Periodic table / main-group element properties / periodic trends
  • SL4. Chemical bonding and structure
    Ionic, covalent and metallic bonds / molecular geometry / VSEPR / polarity and intermolecular forces
  • SL5. Energetics / Thermochemistry
    Energy changes in reactions / enthalpy change / Hess's law / bond energy estimation
  • SL6. Chemical kinetics
    Reaction rates / collision theory / rate equations / reaction mechanisms (basics)
  • SL7. Equilibrium
    Equilibrium constant Kc / Le Chatelier's principle / industrial applications
  • SL8. Acids and bases
    Brønsted-Lowry theory / pH / weak acids and bases / buffer solutions
  • SL9. Redox processes
    Oxidation numbers / half-equation balancing / electrochemical cells (basics)
  • SL10. Organic chemistry
    Alkanes, alkenes, alkynes / functional groups / homologous series / reaction types / polymers
  • SL11. Measurement and analysis
    Uncertainty / IR / 1H NMR / MS spectroscopy basics

Internal Assessment 内部评估 IA

IA 是独立实验探究，占 20%，可涉及合成、滴定、动力学、谱学分析等方向。

The IB Chemistry IA is an individual experimental investigation worth 20% of the final grade. Students formulate a research question, design and conduct an experiment or data analysis, and produce a 6-12 page report (~10 hours of work). Assessment criteria cover personal engagement, exploration, analysis, evaluation, and communication. Topics may include synthesis, titration, kinetics or spectroscopic analysis.
