IB Chemistry

IB Diploma · Chemistry HL/SL

课程介绍 Course Overview

IB 化学是国际文凭组织（IBO）开设的两年制高中化学课程，分 Higher Level (HL) 与 Standard Level (SL) 两个难度。课程以"计量关系—原子结构—周期性—化学键—热化学—动力学—平衡—酸碱—氧化还原—有机—分析"为主线，建立物质结构与反应规律的完整体系。HL 在 SL 同一框架下进一步深化每个主题，并加入更多定量推导与谱学分析。是申请化学、医学、生物、材料、化工等专业的重要科目。

IB Chemistry is a two-year secondary chemistry course offered by IBO, available at both Higher Level (HL) and Standard Level (SL). The course builds a coherent framework of matter and reactions across stoichiometry, atomic structure, periodicity, bonding, energetics, kinetics, equilibrium, acids and bases, redox, organic chemistry and analytical techniques. HL extends each of these areas with greater quantitative depth and spectroscopic analysis. A core subject for chemistry, medicine, biology, materials and chemical engineering applicants.

内部评估 IA Internal Assessment

IB 化学的 IA 是一项独立完成的实验探究，占学科总分的 20%。学生需自行提出研究问题，设计实验或数据处理方案，收集与分析数据，撰写 6-12 页的报告（约 10 小时工作量）。评估维度包括：个人参与、探索、分析、评估、沟通。可涉及合成、滴定、动力学、谱学分析等多种方向。

The IB Chemistry IA is an individual experimental investigation worth 20% of the final grade. Students formulate a research question, design and conduct an experiment or data analysis, and produce a 6-12 page report (~10 hours of work). Assessment criteria cover personal engagement, exploration, analysis, evaluation, and communication. Topics may include synthesis, titration, kinetics or spectroscopic analysis.
