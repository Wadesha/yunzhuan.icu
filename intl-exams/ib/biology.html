IB 生物

IB Biology

IB Diploma · Biology HL/SL

课程介绍 Course Overview

IB 生物是国际文凭组织（IBO）开设的两年制高中生物课程，分 Higher Level (HL) 与 Standard Level (SL) 两个难度。SL 围绕细胞、分子、遗传、生态、进化与人体生理 6 个核心主题展开；HL 在 SL 基础上增加核酸、代谢与光合呼吸、植物生物学、遗传与进化深入、动物生理 5 个章节，并从神经生物学、生物技术、生态与保护、人体生理 4 个 Option 中选一。强调实验探究与跨学科思维，是申请医学、生物、生命科学、药学、农学等专业的重要科目。

IB Biology is a two-year secondary biology course offered by IBO, available at both Higher Level (HL) and Standard Level (SL). SL covers six core topics: cell biology, molecular biology, genetics, ecology, evolution and biodiversity, and human physiology. HL adds five more: nucleic acids, metabolism (respiration and photosynthesis), plant biology, genetics and evolution (extended), and animal physiology, plus one Option from neurobiology, biotechnology, ecology and conservation, or human physiology. It emphasizes experimental inquiry and interdisciplinary thinking — a key subject for medicine, biology, life sciences, pharmacy and agriculture.

内部评估 IA Internal Assessment

IB 生物的 IA 是一项独立完成的实验探究，占学科总分的 20%。学生需自行提出研究问题，设计实验或数据处理方案，收集与分析数据，撰写 6-12 页的报告（约 10 小时工作量）。评估维度包括：个人参与、探索、分析、评估、沟通。可涉及酶活、植物生长、行为学、生态调查、分子建模等多种方向。

The IB Biology IA is an individual experimental investigation worth 20% of the final grade. Students formulate a research question, design and conduct an experiment or data analysis, and produce a 6-12 page report (~10 hours of work). Assessment criteria cover personal engagement, exploration, analysis, evaluation, and communication. Topics may include enzyme activity, plant growth, behavioral studies, ecological surveys, or molecular modeling.
