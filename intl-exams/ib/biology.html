IB Biology

IB 生物

IB Diploma · Biology HL/SL

Course Overview 课程介绍

IB 生物分 HL 与 SL，SL 覆盖细胞、遗传、生态等 6 大主题，HL 增加 5 个章节。

IB Biology is a two-year secondary biology course offered by IBO, available at both Higher Level (HL) and Standard Level (SL). SL covers six core topics: cell biology, molecular biology, genetics, ecology, evolution and biodiversity, and human physiology. HL adds five more: nucleic acids, metabolism (respiration and photosynthesis), plant biology, genetics and evolution (extended), and animal physiology, plus one Option from neurobiology, biotechnology, ecology and conservation, or human physiology. It emphasizes experimental inquiry and interdisciplinary thinking — a key subject for medicine, biology, life sciences, pharmacy and agriculture.

SL Syllabus SL 阶段章节

  • SL1. Cell biology
    Cell theory / cell structure / membrane transport / cell division / stem cells
  • SL2. Molecular biology
    Water / carbohydrates / lipids / proteins / enzymes / DNA replication, transcription and translation
  • SL3. Genetics
    Meiosis / Mendelian inheritance / genes / genetic engineering
  • SL4. Ecology
    Species and populations / communities and ecosystems / energy flow / carbon and nitrogen cycles
  • SL5. Evolution and biodiversity
    Natural selection / speciation / classification and phylogeny
  • SL6. Human physiology
    Digestion and absorption / blood / immunity / nerves and hormones

HL Additional Topics HL 额外章节

  • HL7. Nucleic acids
    DNA structure and replication (in depth) / regulation of transcription and translation / introduction to epigenetics
  • HL8. Metabolism, cell respiration and photosynthesis
    Electron transport chain / Calvin cycle / rate-limiting enzymes
  • HL9. Plant biology
    Nutrient transport / transpiration / plant hormones / reproduction
  • HL10. Genetics and evolution
    Gene frequency / Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium / types of selection
  • HL11. Animal physiology
    Muscle and movement / kidney / immunity (in depth) / reproductive system
  • HLOption A/B/C/D
    Option: A. Neurobiology / B. Biotechnology and bioinformatics / C. Ecology and conservation / D. Human physiology (choose one; HL has one additional topic)

Internal Assessment 内部评估 IA

IA 是独立实验探究，占 20%，可涉及酶活、植物生长、生态调查等方向。

The IB Biology IA is an individual experimental investigation worth 20% of the final grade. Students formulate a research question, design and conduct an experiment or data analysis, and produce a 6-12 page report (~10 hours of work). Assessment criteria cover personal engagement, exploration, analysis, evaluation, and communication. Topics may include enzyme activity, plant growth, behavioral studies, ecological surveys, or molecular modeling.
