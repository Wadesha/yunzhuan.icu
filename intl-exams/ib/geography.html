IB 地理

IB Geography

IB Diploma · Geography HL/SL

课程介绍 Course Overview

IB 地理是国际文凭组织（IBO）开设的两年制高中地理课程，分 Higher Level (HL) 与 Standard Level (SL) 两个难度。课程结构为"地理主题（themes）— 地理视角（perspectives / core）— HL 扩展（global interactions）— 田野调查 IA"。SL 须从 7 个主题中选 2 个并完成 3 个核心视角；HL 须选 3 个主题并多一份 Paper 3 全球互动扩展卷。强调人地关系、空间思维与可持续发展，是申请地理、环境、城市规划、国际发展、社科等专业的重要学科。

IB Geography is a two-year secondary geography course offered by IBO, available at both Higher Level (HL) and Standard Level (SL). The course is structured around geographic themes, geographic perspectives (the core), an HL extension on global interactions, and a fieldwork IA. SL students choose 2 of 7 themes plus 3 core perspectives; HL students choose 3 themes and add Paper 3 on global interactions. The course emphasizes human-environment relationships, spatial thinking, and sustainability — a key subject for geography, environment, urban planning, international development, and social science applicants.

内部评估 IA Internal Assessment

IB 地理的 IA 是一项独立完成的田野调查报告，占学科总分的 SL 25% / HL 20%。学生需自选一个地理问题，进行一手数据采集（如问卷、测量、观察、GIS），结合二手数据分析，撰写 2,500 字的报告，包含引言、方法、分析、结论、评估。评估维度：田野work 计划、方法、数据分析、结论、评估、参考文献与表达。SL/HL 评估维度相同，HL 期望在地理概念与理论框架上更深入。

The IB Geography IA is an individual fieldwork report worth 25% (SL) / 20% (HL) of the final grade. Students choose a geographical issue, collect primary data (surveys, measurements, observation, GIS), combine with secondary data, and produce a 2,500 word report covering introduction, method, analysis, conclusion, evaluation. Assessment criteria: fieldwork plan, method, data analysis, conclusion, evaluation, references and presentation. SL/HL share the same criteria; HL is expected to show deeper use of geographic concepts.
