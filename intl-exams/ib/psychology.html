IB 心理

IB Psychology

IB Diploma · Psychology HL/SL

课程介绍 Course Overview

IB 心理是国际文凭组织（IBO）开设的两年制高中心理学课程，分 Higher Level (HL) 与 Standard Level (SL) 两个难度。SL 围绕生物、认知、社会文化三大核心进路展开对人类行为的解释，并从异常、发展、健康、人际关系 4 个 Option 中选 1；HL 在 SL 基础上为三大进路各增加扩展主题，并选 2 个 Option，另多一份 Paper 3 研究方法卷。课程强调用实证研究（经典实验）分析行为，并培养研究方法与伦理意识。是申请心理学、神经科学、教育、社工、商科人力资源等专业的重要学科。

IB Psychology is a two-year secondary psychology course offered by IBO, available at both Higher Level (HL) and Standard Level (SL). SL covers the three core approaches to behavior — biological, cognitive, and sociocultural — plus one Option from abnormal, developmental, health, or relationships psychology. HL extends each approach, requires two Options, and adds a Paper 3 on research methodology. The course emphasizes using empirical studies (classic experiments) to analyze behavior and develops research methods and ethical awareness. A key subject for psychology, neuroscience, education, social work, and HR applicants.

内部评估 IA Internal Assessment

IB 心理的 IA 是一项独立完成的小型实验研究，占学科总分的 SL 25% / HL 25%。学生需复制一项已发表的实验研究（如 Stroop 效应、 Loftus 灰尘瓶实验等），收集与分析数据，撰写 1,500-2,000 字的实验报告，包括引言、方法、结果、讨论。评估维度：引言、方法、结果、讨论、引用与表达。SL/HL 评估维度相同，HL 期望在研究设计、统计与讨论深度上更严谨。

The IB Psychology IA is an individual experimental study worth 25% of the final grade for both SL and HL. Students replicate a published experiment (e.g., Stroop effect, Loftus experiments), collect and analyze data, and produce a 1,500-2,000 word report covering introduction, method, results, and discussion. Assessment criteria: introduction, method, results, discussion, references and presentation. HL is expected to demonstrate greater rigor in design, statistics, and discussion.

考试结构 Exam Structure

Paper 1（SL）三大核心进路（3 道简答 + 1 道长题） · 120 min · 占 SL 50%

Paper 1（HL）三大核心进路 + HL 扩展 · 120 min · 占 HL 35%

Paper 3（HL）研究方法情境题（3 道短题） · 60 min · 占 HL 20%
