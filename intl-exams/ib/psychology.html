IB Psychology

IB 心理

IB Diploma · Psychology HL/SL

Course Overview 课程介绍

IB 心理分 HL/SL，SL 学三大进路 + 1 个 Option，HL 加扩展主题、2 个 Option 及 Paper 3 研究方法卷。

IB Psychology is a two-year secondary psychology course offered by IBO, available at both Higher Level (HL) and Standard Level (SL). SL covers the three core approaches to behavior — biological, cognitive, and sociocultural — plus one Option from abnormal, developmental, health, or relationships psychology. HL extends each approach, requires two Options, and adds a Paper 3 on research methodology. The course emphasizes using empirical studies (classic experiments) to analyze behavior and develops research methods and ethical awareness. A key subject for psychology, neuroscience, education, social work, and HR applicants.

SL Syllabus SL 阶段章节

  • SL1. Biological approach to behaviour
    Biological approach to behavior / brain structure and function / neurotransmission / hormones and behavior / genetics and behavior / evolutionary explanations
  • SL2. Cognitive approach to behaviour
    Cognitive approach to behavior / memory models / thinking and decision-making / emotion and cognition / cognitive biases
  • SL3. Sociocultural approach to behaviour
    Sociocultural approach to behavior / culture and behavior / social identity theory / conformity and groups / stereotypes
  • SL4. Research methods introduction
    Research methods introduction / experimental method / correlational research / case studies / quantitative and qualitative / research ethics
  • SL5. Option (choose 1 of 4)
    Options: Abnormal psychology / Developmental psychology / Health psychology / Psychology of human relationships

HL Additional Topics HL 额外章节

  • HL6. Biological approach (HL extension)
    Biological approach extension / brain plasticity / neurogenesis / behavior genetics methods / evolutionary psychology debates
  • HL7. Cognitive approach (HL extension)
    Cognitive approach extension / thinking biases in depth / AI and cognition / two-factor theory of emotion
  • HL8. Sociocultural approach (HL extension)
    Sociocultural approach extension / cultural dimensions theory / psychological impact of globalization / migration and identity
  • HL9. Second Option (choose 2 of 4)
    HL must take 2 Options (SL takes only 1), chosen from abnormal, developmental, health, or relationships
  • HL10. Research methods in depth
    Research methods in depth / sampling methods / reliability and validity / statistical inference / ethical review
  • HL11. Paper 3 research methods questions
    HL-only Paper 3, three short-answer questions based on a research scenario (method, ethics, application of research methods)

Internal Assessment 内部评估 IA

IB 心理 IA 是独立完成的小型实验研究，复制已发表实验并撰写 1,500-2,000 字报告，占 SL/HL 各 25%。

The IB Psychology IA is an individual experimental study worth 25% of the final grade for both SL and HL. Students replicate a published experiment (e.g., Stroop effect, Loftus experiments), collect and analyze data, and produce a 1,500-2,000 word report covering introduction, method, results, and discussion. Assessment criteria: introduction, method, results, discussion, references and presentation. HL is expected to demonstrate greater rigor in design, statistics, and discussion.
