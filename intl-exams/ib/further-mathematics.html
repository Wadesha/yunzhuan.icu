IB Further Mathematics

IB 进阶数学

IB Diploma · Further Mathematics HL (discontinued from 2019)

Course Overview 课程介绍

IB Further Mathematics HL 已于 2019 停设，内容并入现行 Math AA HL。

Please note: IB Further Mathematics HL was discontinued from 2019, with the final examination session in November 2019. Its content has been merged into the current Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches HL (Math AA HL) extension. This page is preserved as a historical entry, listing the original Further Math HL topics and pointing to Math AA HL as the current substitute. No school should offer "IB Further Math" under the current framework — please verify with IBO directly.

Former Further Math Syllabus 原 Further Math HL 内容

  • HL1. Linear algebra
    Matrix operations / determinants / vector spaces / linear transformations / eigenvalues and eigenvectors
  • HL2. Geometry
    Euclidean geometry / analytic geometry / conic sections / introduction to projective geometry / spherical geometry
  • HL3. Statistics and probability
    Probability distributions (in depth) / hypothesis testing / confidence intervals / regression analysis
  • HL4. Sets, relations and groups
    Set theory / equivalence relations / group theory basics / subgroups and homomorphisms
  • HL5. Calculus (extended)
    Introduction to real analysis / convergence / differential equations / series expansion
  • HL6. Discrete mathematics
    Graph theory / recurrence relations / combinatorics / introduction to algorithm analysis

Current Alternative 现行替代路径

  • HLMath AA HL · Number and algebra extension
    Absorbs former Further Math content: complex numbers, matrices, introduction to group theory, mathematical induction
  • HLMath AA HL · Calculus extension
    Absorbs former Further Math content: differential equations, series convergence, Maclaurin expansion
  • HLMath AA HL · Paper 3
    HL-only Paper 3 with two extended investigation questions; some question styles continue the former Further Math style
  • HLMath AI HL · Discrete extension
    If interested in graph theory, matrices and recurrence relations, choose Math AI HL instead (includes graph theory and matrices)
  • HLRecommended Path
    Math direction: choose Math AA HL · Applied statistics/data direction: choose Math AI HL · Entry at IB Mathematics

Internal Assessment 内部评估 IA

原 Further Math 的 IA 沿用数学探究框架，现行 Math AA HL 继承此框架。

The former Further Math HL IA followed the same framework as other IB mathematics courses — an individual mathematical exploration worth 20% of the final grade. Topics were expected to demonstrate greater mathematical sophistication. The current Math AA HL IA inherits this framework, encouraging exploration in complex numbers, group theory, or differential equations.
