IB Economics

IB 经济

IB Diploma · Economics HL/SL

Course Overview 课程介绍

IB 经济分 HL/SL，围绕微观、宏观、全球经济四大单元，IA 为 3 篇时事评论。

IB Economics is a two-year secondary economics course offered by IBO, available at both Higher Level (HL) and Standard Level (SL). The course is organized around four units — introduction, microeconomics, macroeconomics, and the global economy — and emphasizes using economic models to analyze real-world issues, with three real-data commentaries as the IA. HL extends SL with quantitative analysis (elasticity calculations, AD/AS derivation, Keynesian multiplier) and a separate policy paper. A key subject for economics, business, finance, and public policy applicants.

SL Syllabus SL 阶段章节

  • SL1. Introduction to economics
    Scarcity / opportunity cost / production possibility curves / economic systems
  • SL2. Microeconomics
    Supply and demand model / elasticity / market failure / government intervention (taxes, subsidies, price controls)
  • SL3. Macroeconomics
    GDP and employment / aggregate demand and supply / Keynesian model / fiscal and monetary policy
  • SL4. The global economy
    International trade / comparative advantage / tariffs / exchange rates / balance of payments / development economics

HL Additional Topics HL 额外章节

  • HL5. Microeconomics (HL)
    Market power / monopoly and oligopoly / price discrimination / comparison of market structures
  • HL6. Macroeconomics (HL)
    Rigorous AD-AS derivation / Keynesian multiplier calculation / Phillips curve / long-run equilibrium
  • HL7. The global economy (HL)
    Trade protection (in depth) / exchange rate regimes / current account imbalances / economic development indicators
  • HL8. Quantitative methods
    Elasticity calculations / cost-benefit analysis / multiplier calculation / quantitative policy impact assessment
  • HL9. Paper 3 policy questions
    HL-only Paper 3: quantitative calculation + policy recommendation questions requiring computable policy evaluation

Internal Assessment 内部评估 IA

IA 是 3 篇基于真实新闻的时事评论，分别覆盖微观、宏观、全球经济单元。

The IB Economics IA is a portfolio of three real-news commentaries worth 30% (SL) or 20% (HL) of the final grade. Each commentary must cover a different syllabus unit (micro, macro, global economy), be up to 800 words, and demonstrate the ability to apply economic concepts to interpret news and evaluate relevant diagrams and policies. HL must also include quantitative analysis.
