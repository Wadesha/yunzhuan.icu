AP Economics (Micro & Macro)

AP 经济（微观与宏观）

College Board · Advanced Placement

Course Overview 课程介绍

AP 经济分微观与宏观两门独立考试，常配套修读，4 分以上可换学分。

AP Economics, administered by the College Board, comprises two independent exams: AP Microeconomics and AP Macroeconomics. Micro focuses on individual decision-makers, market structures and market failure; Macro covers aggregate indicators, AD-AS, the financial sector, stabilization policy and the open economy. They are usually taken together.

Key Facts 基本信息

Administrator (主办方)College Board
Duration (学制)1 year per exam (two independent exams, often paired)
Scoring (评分)1 – 5 (5 is the highest)
Prerequisites (先修建议)Algebra II; basic comfort with graphs
Exam structure (考试结构)Section I MCQ + Section II FRQ; MCQ = 66.7%
Lab (实验)No lab component; emphasis on graphical and data analysis

Sub-Exam: Microeconomics AP Microeconomics 章节

微观经济 6 单元：基本概念、供求、生产成本、不完全竞争、要素市场与市场失灵。

  • MicroUnit 1. Basic Economic Concepts
    Scarcity and opportunity cost; production possibilities frontier; comparative advantage and trade
  • MicroUnit 2. Supply and Demand
    Equilibrium; elasticity; welfare analysis of policy interventions
  • MicroUnit 3. Production, Cost, and the Perfect Competition Model
    Short-run and long-run costs; firm and market supply under perfect competition
  • MicroUnit 4. Imperfect Competition
    Monopoly; oligopoly; monopolistic competition; price discrimination
  • MicroUnit 5. Factor Markets
    Labor markets; marginal revenue product; factor demand and supply
  • MicroUnit 6. Market Failure and the Role of Government
    Externalities; public goods; asymmetric information; taxes and redistribution

Sub-Exam: Macroeconomics AP Macroeconomics 章节

宏观经济 6 单元：基本概念、经济指标、AD-AS、金融部门、稳定政策与开放经济。

  • MacroUnit 1. Basic Economic Concepts
    Scarcity and opportunity cost; production possibilities frontier; comparative advantage
  • MacroUnit 2. Economic Indicators and the Business Cycle
    GDP; inflation and CPI; unemployment
  • MacroUnit 3. National Income and Price Determination
    Aggregate demand and aggregate supply; multiplier effect; equilibrium and output gaps
  • MacroUnit 4. Financial Sector
    Money and banking; money market; interest rates; central bank and monetary-policy tools
  • MacroUnit 5. Long-Run Consequences of Stabilization Policies
    Phillips curve; fiscal policy and debt; economic growth
  • MacroUnit 6. Open Economy — International Trade and Finance
    Balance of payments; exchange rates and foreign-exchange markets

Exam Structure 考试结构

Microeconomics · Section IMCQ · 60 questions · 70 min · 66.7%
Microeconomics · Section IIFRQ · 3 questions · 60 min · 33.3% (incl. graphing)
Macroeconomics · Section IMCQ · 60 questions · 70 min · 66.7%
Macroeconomics · Section IIFRQ · 3 questions · 60 min · 33.3% (incl. graphing)

Credit Transfer 学分转换与衔接

4/5 分常换算大学微观/宏观经济入门，商科经济专业常要求两门均达标。

Both AP Economics exams use a 1–5 scale; most US colleges grant credit for 4 or 5. Microeconomics typically maps to intro Micro, and Macroeconomics to intro Macro. Business and economics programs often require 4/5 on both for prerequisite waivers. Policies vary by institution.
