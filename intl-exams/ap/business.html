AP Business

AP 商科

College Board · Advanced Placement

Course Overview 课程介绍

AP 不提供 Business 独立科目，最接近组合为 Micro/Macro + Statistics。

It should be stated plainly: the AP system does not offer a stand-alone "Business" course, unlike A-Level Business or IB Business Management. The closest AP offerings are AP Microeconomics and AP Macroeconomics, optionally paired with AP Statistics for analytical support. For accounting, marketing or organizational behavior, Dual Enrollment is recommended.

Closest Substitutes 相关替代科目章节

推荐微观/宏观经济作主轴、Statistics 作数据支撑、Dual Enrollment 补足商科课程。

  • MicroAP Microeconomics
    Basic economic concepts; supply and demand; production and cost; perfect competition; imperfect competition; factor markets; market failure and government
  • MacroAP Macroeconomics
    Economic indicators and the business cycle; national income and price determination; financial sector; stabilization policy; open economy and international trade
  • StatAP Statistics (analytical support)
    Exploring data; sampling and experiments; probability and random variables; sampling distributions; inference (proportions, means, chi-square, regression)
  • DualDual Enrollment (recommended supplement)
    For accounting, marketing, organizational behavior or financial management, take university courses via Dual Enrollment during high school

Credit Transfer 学分转换与衔接

无 Business 考试，按替代 AP 科目分别换分；BBA 专业常接受组合免修。

AP has no Business exam; credit is granted per substitute AP course. Micro/Macro 4/5 typically map to introductory economics courses, and Statistics 4/5 to intro statistics. BBA programs often accept this combination as prerequisite waivers. See the AP Economics and AP Mathematics pages for details.
