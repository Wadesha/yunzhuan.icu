AP Biology

AP 生物

College Board · Advanced Placement

Course Overview 课程介绍

AP 生物相当于大学普通生物学一学年，8 单元，5 分制，4 分以上可换学分。

AP Biology, administered by the College Board, is equivalent to a full-year university General Biology course. Organized around eight units and four Big Ideas (evolution, energetics, information, systems), it covers the chemistry of life, cells, energetics, cell communication, heredity, gene expression, natural selection and ecology, with strong emphasis on inquiry and data analysis.

Key Facts 基本信息

Administrator (主办方)College Board
Duration (学制)1 year
Scoring (评分)1 – 5 (5 is the highest)
Prerequisites (先修建议)High school biology and chemistry
Exam structure (考试结构)Section I MCQ + Section II FRQ, 50% each
Lab (实验)At least 13 guided-inquiry labs required; no separate lab exam

Sub-Exam Topics 子科目章节

8 个单元，从生命化学到生态，强调科学探究与数据分析。

  • BioUnit 1. Chemistry of Life
    Water and the four biological macromolecules; enzymes and metabolism
  • BioUnit 2. Cell Structure and Function
    Membrane structure and transport; organelles and cell compartmentalization
  • BioUnit 3. Cellular Energetics
    Enzymes; cellular respiration; photosynthesis
  • BioUnit 4. Cell Communication and Cell Cycle
    Signal transduction; feedback regulation; mitosis and cell-cycle control
  • BioUnit 5. Heredity
    Meiosis; Mendelian genetics; non-Mendelian genetics and linkage
  • BioUnit 6. Gene Expression and Regulation
    DNA replication; transcription and translation; regulation of gene expression and mutations
  • BioUnit 7. Natural Selection
    Mechanisms of evolution; population genetics; phylogeny and speciation
  • BioUnit 8. Ecology
    Population and community ecology; energy flow and matter cycling; biodiversity and conservation

Exam Structure 考试结构

Section IMCQ · 60 questions · 90 min · 50%
Section IIFRQ · 6 questions · 90 min · 50% (methods & data analysis, visual interpretation, scientific investigation, concept analysis, model analysis, data analysis)

Credit Transfer 学分转换与衔接

4/5 分常换算大学生物基础课（含实验），医学预科常作免修前置。

AP Biology is scored 1–5; most US colleges grant credit for 4 or 5, typically mapping to one or two semesters of introductory biology (with lab). Pre-med and life-science programs often accept a 4/5 as a prerequisite waiver. Policies vary by institution.
