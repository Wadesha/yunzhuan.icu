AP History (World / US / European)

AP 历史（世界 / 美国 / 欧洲）

College Board · Advanced Placement

Course Overview 课程介绍

AP 历史含世界史、美国史与欧洲史三门，结构相似，含 SAQ/DBQ/LEQ。

AP History, administered by the College Board, comprises three common exams: AP World History: Modern, AP United States History, and AP European History. All emphasize historical thinking skills—contextualization, continuity and change, causation, comparison, evidence analysis and argumentation—with a shared exam format of SAQ, DBQ and LEQ.

Key Facts 基本信息

Administrator (主办方)College Board
Duration (学制)1 year per exam (three independent exams)
Scoring (评分)1 – 5 (5 is the highest)
Time span (时间范围)World: 1200–present; US: 1491–present; European: 1450–present
Exam structure (考试结构)Section I MCQ + SAQ · Section II DBQ + LEQ; FRQ = 60%
Writing (写作)DBQ is document-based argument; LEQ is a long essay

Sub-Exam: World History — Modern AP World History: Modern 章节

世界史 9 单元，从 1200 年全球织锦到当代全球化。

  • WorldUnit 1. The Global Tapestry (1200–1450)
    State of civilizations in East Asia, South Asia, Southeast Asia, the Americas, Africa and Europe
  • WorldUnit 2. Networks of Exchange (1200–1450)
    Silk Roads, Indian Ocean trade, trans-Saharan trade and cultural diffusion
  • WorldUnit 3. Land-Based Empires (1450–1750)
    Expansion and rule of the Ottoman, Safavid, Mughal and Ming/Qing empires
  • WorldUnit 4. Transoceanic Interconnections (1450–1750)
    Maritime exploration, Columbian Exchange, colonial empires and the slave trade
  • WorldUnit 5. Revolutions (1750–1900)
    Enlightenment; American, French and Haitian revolutions; Industrial Revolution and nationalism
  • WorldUnit 6. Consequences of Industrialization (1750–1900)
    Imperialism, new imperialism and migration patterns
  • WorldUnit 7. Global Conflict (1900–present)
    World War I, World War II and the Holocaust, origins of the Cold War
  • WorldUnit 8. Cold War and Decolonization (1900–present)
    Bipolar order, decolonization movements and emerging states
  • WorldUnit 9. Globalization (1900–present)
    Technology, economic integration, cultural and environmental global interactions

Sub-Exam: US History AP United States History 章节

美国史按 9 个时段组织，从 1491 年原住民社会到 21 世纪当代美国。

  • USHPeriod 1 (1491–1607)
    Native American societies; European exploration and pre-colonial Americas
  • USHPeriod 2 (1607–1754)
    Founding and social development of British North American colonies; origins of slavery
  • USHPeriod 3 (1754–1800)
    Revolutionary War and nation-building; Constitution and the early Republic
  • USHPeriod 4 (1800–1848)
    Jefferson to Jackson era; westward expansion and reform movements
  • USHPeriod 5 (1844–1877)
    Civil War and Reconstruction; slavery, secession and postwar rebuilding
  • USHPeriod 6 (1865–1898)
    Gilded Age; industrialization, urbanization and the western frontier
  • USHPeriod 7 (1890–1945)
    Progressivism through WWII; imperialism, world wars and the Great Depression
  • USHPeriod 8 (1945–1980)
    Cold War era; civil rights movement, Vietnam and domestic social change
  • USHPeriod 9 (1980–present)
    Contemporary America; conservatism, end of the Cold War and 21st-century challenges

Sub-Exam: European History AP European History 章节

欧洲史 9 单元，从文艺复兴与探索到冷战与当代欧洲。

  • EuroUnit 1. Renaissance and Exploration (1450–1648)
    Humanism, Italian city-states and the Age of Discovery
  • EuroUnit 2. Age of Reformation (c. 1450–c. 1648)
    Protestant Reformation, Catholic Reformation and wars of religion
  • EuroUnit 3. Absolutism and Constitutionalism (c. 1648–c. 1815)
    Louis XIV, English constitutionalism and Eastern European absolutism
  • EuroUnit 4. Scientific, Philosophical, and Political Developments (c. 1648–c. 1815)
    Scientific Revolution and Enlightenment; rationalism and political thought
  • EuroUnit 5. Conflict, Crisis, and Reaction in the Late 18th Century (c. 1648–c. 1815)
    Late-18th-century conflict and crisis; French Revolution and Napoleon
  • EuroUnit 6. Industrialization and Its Effects (c. 1815–c. 1914)
    Industrial Revolution, social change and ideologies
  • EuroUnit 7. 19th-Century Perspectives and Political Developments (c. 1815–c. 1914)
    Nationalism, liberalism, unification and imperialism
  • EuroUnit 8. 20th-Century Global Conflicts (c. 1914–present)
    World War I, World War II, totalitarianism and the Holocaust
  • EuroUnit 9. Cold War and Contemporary Europe (c. 1914–present)
    Bipolar order, EU integration and post-Cold War transition

Exam Structure 考试结构

World / US / Euro · Section I-AMCQ · 55 questions · 55 min · 40%
World / US / Euro · Section I-BSAQ · 3 questions · 40 min · 20%
World / US / Euro · Section II-ADBQ · 1 question · 60 min · 25%
World / US / Euro · Section II-BLEQ · 1 question (choose one of three) · 40 min · 15%
Note (说明)All three history exams share the same structure and timing; FRQ total = 60%

Credit Transfer 学分转换与衔接

4/5 分常换算历史通识或人文选修，历史专业常要求 5 分换核心学分。

All three AP History exams use a 1–5 scale; most US colleges grant credit for 4 or 5, typically mapping to general education history or humanities electives. Some schools accept US History as the American-history requirement. Policies vary by institution; history majors often require a 5 for core credit.
