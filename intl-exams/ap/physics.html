AP Physics

AP 物理

College Board · Advanced Placement

Course Overview 课程介绍

AP 物理含 Physics 1/2/C 四门，1 年制，5 分制评分，4 分以上可换大学学分。

AP Physics, administered by the College Board, comprises four independent exams: Physics 1, Physics 2, Physics C: Mechanics, and Physics C: Electricity & Magnetism. Physics 1 and 2 are algebra-based and cover mechanics through modern physics; the two Physics C courses are calculus-based and approach university-level engineering physics. Scores of 4 or above commonly earn college credit.

Key Facts 基本信息

Sub-Exam: Physics 1 Topics AP Physics 1 章节

代数版力学入门，含运动学、动力学、能量、动量、转动等 7 个单元。

  • P11. Kinematics
    Displacement, velocity, acceleration; 1D and 2D motion; projectile motion
  • P12. Dynamics
    Newton's laws of motion; friction; force analysis and systems
  • P13. Circular Motion and Gravitation
    Centripetal acceleration and force; universal gravitation; satellite orbits
  • P14. Energy
    Work-energy theorem; kinetic and potential energy; conservation of energy
  • P15. Momentum
    Impulse; conservation of momentum; elastic and inelastic collisions
  • P16. Simple Harmonic Motion
    Spring-mass and pendulum; period, frequency, amplitude
  • P17. Torque and Rotational Motion
    Moment of inertia; rotational kinetic energy; angular momentum conservation

Sub-Exam: Physics 2 Topics AP Physics 2 章节

代数版续修，含流体、热力学、电磁、光学与现代物理 7 个单元。

  • P21. Fluids
    Density and pressure; buoyancy; continuity and Bernoulli's equation
  • P22. Thermodynamics
    Ideal gas law; kinetic theory; laws of thermodynamics and PV diagrams
  • P23. Electric Force, Field, and Potential
    Coulomb's law; electric field and potential; potential energy and conservative fields
  • P24. Electric Circuits
    Series and parallel resistors; Kirchhoff's rules; capacitors and RC circuits
  • P25. Magnetism and Electromagnetic Induction
    Magnetic force on charges and currents; Faraday's and Lenz's laws
  • P26. Geometric and Physical Optics
    Reflection and refraction; interference and diffraction; double-slit and thin films
  • P27. Quantum, Atomic, and Nuclear Physics
    Photoelectric effect; wave-particle duality; decay and mass-energy equivalence

Sub-Exam: Physics C — Mechanics AP Physics C: Mechanics 章节

微积分版力学，含运动学、牛顿定律、能量、动量、转动、振动与引力。

  • C-M1. Kinematics
    Calculus-based kinematics; 1D and 2D motion; differential and integral descriptions
  • C-M2. Newton's Laws of Motion
    Force analysis; applications of second-order differential equations
  • C-M3. Work, Energy, and Power
    Work-energy theorem; conservative forces and potential energy
  • C-M4. Systems of Particles and Linear Momentum
    Center of mass; impulse and momentum; collisions
  • C-M5. Rotation
    Torque and moment of inertia; rotational kinetic energy; angular momentum and conservation
  • C-M6. Oscillations
    Simple harmonic motion; pendulum and spring-mass; differential-equation description
  • C-M7. Gravitation
    Universal gravitation; Kepler's laws; orbital mechanics; gravitational potential energy

Sub-Exam: Physics C — Electricity & Magnetism AP Physics C: EM 章节

微积分版电磁学，含静电、导体电容、电路、磁场与电磁感应。

  • C-EM1. Electrostatics
    Coulomb's law; electric field and potential; Gauss's law (integral form)
  • C-EM2. Conductors, Capacitors, Dielectrics
    Conductors; capacitors; dielectrics; energy storage and field distribution
  • C-EM3. Electric Circuits
    Resistors and Kirchhoff's rules; transient behavior of RC circuits
  • C-EM4. Magnetic Fields
    Lorentz force; Ampere's law; Biot-Savart law
  • C-EM5. Electromagnetism
    Faraday's law of induction; Lenz's law; Maxwell's equations intro; LC and LR circuits

Credit Transfer 学分转换与衔接

4/5 分常换算大学学分，Physics C 可换算微积分版大学物理。

AP Physics uses a 1–5 scale; most US colleges grant credit for scores of 4 or 5. Physics 1/2 typically map to algebra-based intro physics, while the two Physics C exams map to calculus-based sequences required by engineering programs. Policies vary by institution; top STEM schools often require a 5 for core credit.
