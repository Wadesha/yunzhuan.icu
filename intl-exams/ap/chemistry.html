AP Chemistry

AP 化学

College Board · Advanced Placement

Course Overview 课程介绍

AP 化学相当于大学普通化学一学年，9 单元，5 分制，4 分以上可换学分。

AP Chemistry, administered by the College Board, is equivalent to a full-year university General Chemistry course. Organized around nine units and Big Ideas, it covers atomic structure, bonding, intermolecular forces, reactions, kinetics, thermochemistry, equilibrium, acids/bases and electrochemistry, with strong emphasis on quantitative analysis and inquiry-based lab work.

Key Facts 基本信息

Administrator (主办方)College Board
Duration (学制)1 year
Scoring (评分)1 – 5 (5 is the highest)
Prerequisites (先修建议)General chemistry and Algebra II
Exam structure (考试结构)Section I MCQ + Section II FRQ
Lab (实验)At least 16 guided-inquiry labs required; no separate lab exam

Sub-Exam Topics 子科目章节

9 个单元，从原子结构到热力学应用，强调定量分析与实验探究。

  • ChemUnit 1. Atomic Structure and Properties
    Moles and stoichiometry; electron configurations; periodic trends and photoelectron spectroscopy
  • ChemUnit 2. Compound Structure and Properties
    Ionic and covalent bonding; Lewis structures; VSEPR molecular geometry and hybridization
  • ChemUnit 3. Properties of Substances and Mixtures
    Intermolecular forces; solutions and solubility; chromatography and distillation
  • ChemUnit 4. Chemical Reactions
    Chemical equations and stoichiometry; precipitation and acid-base reactions; redox
  • ChemUnit 5. Kinetics
    Rate laws; reaction mechanisms; catalysis; Arrhenius equation
  • ChemUnit 6. Thermochemistry
    Enthalpy changes; calorimetry; Hess's law; bond-energy estimation
  • ChemUnit 7. Equilibrium
    Equilibrium constant K; reaction quotient Q; Le Châtelier's principle
  • ChemUnit 8. Acids and Bases
    pH; weak acids and bases; buffer solutions; titration curves
  • ChemUnit 9. Applications of Thermodynamics
    Entropy and free energy; electrochemistry; galvanic cells and electrolysis

Exam Structure 考试结构

Section IMCQ · 60 questions · 90 min · 50%
Section IIFRQ · 7 questions · 105 min · 50% (lab design, intermolecular forces, kinetics, thermodynamics, equilibrium, acids/bases, free energy)

Credit Transfer 学分转换与衔接

4/5 分常换算大学普通化学（含实验），医学预科常作免修前置。

AP Chemistry is scored 1–5; most US colleges grant credit for 4 or 5, typically mapping to one or two semesters of General Chemistry (with lab). STEM and pre-med programs often accept a 4/5 as a prerequisite waiver. Policies vary by institution.
